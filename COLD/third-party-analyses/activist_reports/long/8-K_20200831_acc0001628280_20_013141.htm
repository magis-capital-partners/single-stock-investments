Item 8.01— Other Events.

On August 31, 2020, Americold Realty Trust (the “Company”) issued a press release announcing the acquisition of a cold storage facility in Tampa, FL for $25.0 million, as well as the completion of its previously announced acquisition of AM-C Warehouses in the Dallas-Fort Worth, TX market for $82.5 million. The Company also announced that it has signed an agreement to extend the settlement date on its outstanding September 2018 equity forward contract to March 2022, from September 2020.

A copy of the press release announcing the acquisitions and the extension of the equity forward contract is attached hereto as Exhibit 99.1 and incorporated herein by reference.
