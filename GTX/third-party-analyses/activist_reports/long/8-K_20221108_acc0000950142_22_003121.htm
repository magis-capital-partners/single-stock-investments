Item 8.01 Other Events.

 

On November 2, 2022, the Board of Directors of Garrett Motion Inc. (the “Company”) extended the Company’s existing $100 million share repurchase program by one year, to November 15, 2023. As of September 30, 2022, approximately $76 million remained under the program for purchases of the Company’s common stock and Series A preferred stock. The share repurchase program is described further in the Company’s Quarterly Report on Form 10-Q for the period ended September 30, 2022, filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission on October 26, 2022.
