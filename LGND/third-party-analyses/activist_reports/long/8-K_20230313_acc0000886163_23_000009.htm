Item 8.01 Other Events.
On March 10, 2023, Silicon Valley Bank (“SVB”) was closed by the California Department of Financial Protection and Innovation, which appointed the Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation (“FDIC”) as receiver.

Ligand Pharmaceuticals Incorporated (“Ligand”) does not hold any deposits or securities or maintain any accounts at SVB. Although the situation with SVB and the FDIC is highly fluid and uncertain, based on information of which Ligand is currently aware, Ligand does not expect any material adverse effect on its business or results of operations as a result of its partners’ potential exposure to SVB.
