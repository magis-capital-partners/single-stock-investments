Item 8.01 Other Events.

On December 21, 2020 we issued a press release to report that we and GlaxoSmithKline (GSK) had formed a collaboration which aims to, utilizing our Icagen discovery technology and our unique expertise in small molecule therapeutics targeting transmembrane proteins, identify and develop inhibitors of a specific genetically-validated molecular target relevant to neurological diseases. The collaboration will be governed by the Research Collaboration, Option and License Agreement dated December 18, 2020 between our subsidiary Icagen, LLC and GSK.
