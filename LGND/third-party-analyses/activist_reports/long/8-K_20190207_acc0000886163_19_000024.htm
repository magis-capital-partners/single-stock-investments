Item 8.01 Other Events.
On February 4, 2019, we entered into a worldwide Commercial Platform License Agreement with Genagon Therapeutics AB, a Sweden-based, immuno-oncology focused biotech. Under the terms of the Agreement, Genagon gained access to the full OmniAb® transgenic animal technology platform, including OmniRat®, OmniMouse®, OmniFlic® and OmniChicken™, for its drug discovery efforts. We received a modest upfront payment and are eligible to receive development milestone payments and tiered royalties of four-to-six percent for each product incorporating an OmniAb-derived antibody. Genagon will be responsible for all costs related to its programs.
