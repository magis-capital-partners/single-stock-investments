Item 5.07    Submission of Matters to a Vote of Security Holders.

On July 14, 2022, The Oncology Institute, Inc. (the "Company") held its 2022 Annual Meeting of Stockholders (the "2022 Annual Meeting"). At the meeting, a total of 59,998,914 shares were voted, representing 80.58% of the 74,455,497 shares of the Company’s Common Stock outstanding as of the May 16, 2022 record date. The result of the items voted on at the 2022 Annual Meeting are as follows:

Proposal I:    Election of Directors
