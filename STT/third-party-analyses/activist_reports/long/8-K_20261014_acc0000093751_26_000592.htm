Item 5.02.    Departure of Directors or Certain Officers; Election of Directors; Appointment of Certain Officers; Compensatory Arrangements of Certain Officers.
On October 9, 2026, State Street Corporation’s Board of Directors elected Alexander Vollert as an independent director of the corporation. Dr. Vollert has also been appointed as a member to the Board’s Technology and Operations Committee and its Risk Committee effective the date of his election to the Board. There are no arrangements or understandings between Dr. Vollert and any other persons pursuant to which Dr. Vollert was elected as a director of the corporation. Dr. Vollert will be entitled to a pro rata share of the 2026-2027 $110,000 annual retainer and $235,000 common stock retainer and to other director compensation arrangements under terms consistent with those previously disclosed in Exhibit 10.9 to State Street’s annual report on Form 10-K for the year ended December 31, 2025. The pro-rated stock award will be based on the closing price of State Street’s common stock on the New York Stock Exchange on the date of election. Dr. Vollert will be entitled to enter into an indemnification agreement with State Street consistent with the forms of indemnification agreement entered into by State Street’s other non-employee directors and previously disclosed in Exhibit 10.11A to State Street’s annual report on Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2025.

Senior Notes Redemption
On October 14, 2026, State Street notified the holders of its $500,000,000 aggregate principal amount of 1.684% Fixed-to-Floating Rate Senior Notes due 2027 (the “Notes”) that it will redeem all of the Notes on November 18, 2026 (the “Redemption Date”). The Notes will be redeemed at a redemption price equal to 100% of the principal amount of the Notes to be redeemed, plus accrued and unpaid interest thereon to, but excluding, the Redemption Date. On and after the Redemption Date, interest will cease to accrue on the Notes.
State Street intends to fund the aggregate redemption price using cash on hand.
