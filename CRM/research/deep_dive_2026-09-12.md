## What this business is
Salesforce, Inc. sells cloud software that helps enterprises manage customer relationships, sales pipelines, marketing campaigns, and service operations. **[Fact]** FY2026 revenue (year ended January 31, 2026) was **$41.5 billion**, up from **$34.9 billion** in FY2025 per `investor-documents/sec-edgar/10-K_20260302_rpt20260131_acc0001108524_26_000060.htm`. **[Fact]** The company reports subscription and support revenue as the dominant stream and discloses three clouds (Sales, Service, Platform and Other) plus Marketing and Commerce. **[Fact]** Customers are large and mid-market enterprises globally; the model is recurring subscription fees with professional services attach. **[Fact]** Marc Benioff remains chair and CEO; capital allocation emphasizes share repurchases and selective acquisitions.

---

## Why the market might be wrong
The market may be treating Salesforce as a mature, low-growth CRM vendor while Agentforce and data-cloud cross-sell still have a long reinvestment runway. **[Fact]** FY2026 operating cash flow was **$15.0 billion** and capital spending was **$594 million**, leaving normalized owner earnings near **$14.4 billion** per the locked fact ledger. **[Inference]** Generalists may overweight competitive fear from Microsoft Dynamics and underweight switching costs in enterprise workflows and the installed base of custom integrations. At **$173.79** (July 2026), the stock trades below the contract base present value of **$282** per share, which may embed skepticism on incremental returns after a debt-funded buyback wave.

**Switching costs (Munger):** Enterprises embed Salesforce in sales, service, and custom apps. **[Inference]** Migration off the platform is costly in retraining, data migration, and integration rebuilds.

**Moat trajectory (Munger):** Is competitive advantage widening? **Unproven.** Microsoft bundles CRM with Office and Azure; AI agents may commoditize some workflow layers. **[Inference]** Moat depends on continued innovation in data cloud and Agentforce, not on historical CRM dominance alone.
