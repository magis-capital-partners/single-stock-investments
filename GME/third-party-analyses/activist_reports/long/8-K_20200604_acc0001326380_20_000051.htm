Item 5.02  Departure of Directors or Certain Officers; Election of Directors; Appointment of Certain Officers; Compensatory Arrangements of Certain Officers

On June 1, 2020, Daniel J. Kaufman resigned as Chief Transformation Officer of GameStop Corp. (the “Company”), effective immediately. In accordance with the terms of his Executive Employment Agreement, as amended on March 1, 2018, May 31, 2018 and May 8, 2019 (the “Employment Agreement”), Mr. Kaufman will be entitled to the severance benefits payable thereunder for a resignation with “Good Reason.” The Company has previously filed copies of the Employment Agreement and related amendments, as reflected in the list of exhibits included in the Company’s Annual Report on Form 10-K filed with the Securities Exchange Commission on March 27, 2020. In connection with such resignation and in fulfillment of a condition required for the payment of his severance benefits, the Company and Mr. Kaufman entered into a Separation and Release Agreement, a copy of which is attached hereto as Exhibit 10.1 and incorporated herein by reference.
