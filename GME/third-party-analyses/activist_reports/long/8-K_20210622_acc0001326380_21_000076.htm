Item 8.01 Other Events.

On June 22, 2021, GameStop Corp. (the “Company”) announced that it has completed its previously announced “at-the-market” equity offering program (the “ATM Program”). The Company ultimately sold 5,000,000 shares of its common stock under the ATM Program and generated aggregate gross proceeds before commissions and offering expenses of approximately $1,126,000,000.
