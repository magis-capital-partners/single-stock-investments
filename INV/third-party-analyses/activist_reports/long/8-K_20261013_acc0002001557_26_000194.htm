Item 8.01 Other Events.
On October 12, 2026, Accelsius Holdings LLC (“Accelsius”), a subsidiary of Innventure, Inc. (the “Company”), issued a press release announcing that it has upgraded its Springdale lab and facility in Austin, Texas to multi-megawatt power for testing two-phase, direct-to-chip liquid cooling at megawatt-class rack densities, and that it expects to expand its lease footprint at the facility by an additional 18,000 square feet in January 2027. The press release also announced that Accelsius is expanding its NeuCool® multi-rack product family with a 375kW-capable unit.
A copy of the press release is attached hereto as Exhibit 99.1 and is incorporated by reference herein.
