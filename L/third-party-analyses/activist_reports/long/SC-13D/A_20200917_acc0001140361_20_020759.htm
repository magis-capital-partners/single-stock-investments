Item 5.
Interest in Securities of the Issuer.
 
Item 5 is amended by deleting the text thereof in its entirety and replacing it with the following:
 
As of September 16, 2020, Mr. Tisch beneficially owned a total of 16,829,404 shares of Common Stock of the Issuer, including 11,540,903 shares held by trusts of which he is trustee, 4,218,377 shares held by trusts of which his wife is trustee, 1,060,000 shares held by a charitable foundation of which he is a director, 568 shares which he had the right to acquire upon exercise of Stock Appreciation Rights which were then exercisable and 9,556 shares underlying vested RSUs of which he deferred receipt that could be delivered to him within 60 days of September 16, 2020 if his service with the Company were to terminate as of that date. The total number of shares beneficially owned by Mr. Tisch constituted approximately 6.1% of the 275,739,943 shares of Common Stock of the Issuer outstanding on September 16, 2020, according to information provided by the Issuer.
 
Mr. Tisch had sole voting and dispositive power over 11,540,903 shares held by trusts of which he is trustee and would have sole voting and dispositive power over 568 shares he had the right to acquire upon exercise of Stock Appreciation Rights if he had exercised them and 9,556 shares underlying vested RSUs if he were to receive them. Mr. Tisch may be deemed to have shared voting and dispositive power over 4,218,377 shares held by trusts of which his wife is trustee and 1,060,000 shares held by a charitable foundation of which he is a director.
 
During the 60 days preceding the filing of this statement, the only transactions in the Common Stock of the Issuer engaged in by Mr. Tisch were as follows: on September 14, 2020, trusts of which Mr. Tisch or his wife is trustee received gifts totaling 1,475,984 shares of Common Stock, respectively, which in each case did not change Mr. Tisch’s beneficial ownership.
