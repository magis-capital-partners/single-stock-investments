Item 5.02.    Departure of Director or Certain Officers; Election of Directors; Appointment of Certain Officers; Compensatory Arrangements of Certain Officers.

(b) On October 26, 2022, Jonathan M. Tisch informed Loews Corporation (the “Corporation”) that, effective January 1, 2023, he will resign from his position as Chief Executive Officer of the Corporation’s subsidiary, Loews Hotels Holding Corporation (“Loews Hotels”). He will retain his position as Chairman of the Board of Loews Hotels and, from and after January 1, 2023, he also will serve as Executive Chairman of Loews Hotels. In addition, he will continue to serve as a member of the Office of the President and Co-Chairman of the Board of the Corporation.

Item 8.01.    Other Events.

On October 26, 2022, the Board of Directors of Loews Hotels elected Alexander Tisch as President and Chief Executive Officer of Loews Hotels, effective January 1, 2023. He also will continue to serve as a Vice President of the Corporation.
