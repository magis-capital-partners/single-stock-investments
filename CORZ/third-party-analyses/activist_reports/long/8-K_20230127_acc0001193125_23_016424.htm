Item 3.01

Notice of Delisting or Failure to Satisfy a Continued Listing Rule or Standard; Transfer of Listing.

As previously disclosed, on December 22, 2022, Core Scientific, Inc. (the “Company”) received written notice (the “Delisting Notice”) from the staff of The Nasdaq Stock Market LLC (“Nasdaq”) notifying the Company that, as a result of voluntary petitions filed by the Company and certain of its affiliates in the United States Bankruptcy Court for the Southern District of Texas seeking relief under Chapter 11 of Title 11 of the United States Code, and in accordance with Nasdaq Listing Rules 5101, 5110(b) and IM-5101-1, the staff of Nasdaq had determined that the Company’s common stock will be delisted from Nasdaq.

Following receipt of the Delisting Notice, the Company requested an appeal of Nasdaq’s determination and a hearing before a Nasdaq hearings panel, which appeal stayed the Company’s delisting and the filing of a Form 25-NSE with the Securities and Exchange Commission. After careful deliberations, the Company has now determined to withdraw its appeal of the delisting proceedings.

Accordingly, the Company’s common stock will be delisted from trading on Nasdaq with the filing of the Form 25-NSE. Trading of the Company’s common stock on the OTC Bulletin Board will continue under the symbol “CORZ.”
