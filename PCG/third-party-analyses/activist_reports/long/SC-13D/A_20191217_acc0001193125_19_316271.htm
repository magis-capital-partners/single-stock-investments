Explanatory Note

This Amendment No. 6 amends the statement on Schedule 13D filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission by Knighthead Capital Management, LLC (the “Reporting Person”) on August 7, 2019, as amended (the “Original Schedule 13D”), with respect to common stock of PG&E Corporation (the “Company”). Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Amendment No. 6 have the meanings set forth in the Original Schedule 13D.

 

Item 6.

Contracts, Arrangements, Understandings or Relationships With Respect to Securities of the Issuer.

Item 6 is hereby amended by adding the following paragraph:

On December 16, 2019, the Reporting Person, solely on behalf of certain funds and accounts it manages and/or advises, entered into an amendment (the “RSA Amendment”) to the Restructuring Support Agreement with the Company, Pacific Gas and Electric Company, the Requisite Consenting Fire Claimant Professionals (as defined in the Restructuring Support Agreement, which includes the TCC) and certain funds and accounts managed or advised by the Other Shareholder. A copy of the RSA Amendment is filed as Exhibit 99.1 hereto.

 

Item 7.

Materials to be Filed as Exhibits.

 

99.1

First Amendment to Restructuring Support Agreement, dated December 16, 2019, by and among Knighthead Capital Management, LLC, solely on behalf of certain funds and accounts it manages and/or advises, PG&E Corporation, Pacific Gas and Electric Company, the Consenting Fire Claimant Professionals, the TCC and certain funds and accounts managed or advised by Abrams Capital Management, LP (incorporated by reference to Exhibit 10.1 to the Company’s Current Report on Form 8-K, filed on December 17, 2019).
