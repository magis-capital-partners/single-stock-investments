Item 5.02 - Departure of Directors or Certain Officers; Election of Directors; Appointments of Certain Officers; Compensatory Arrangements or Certain Officers.
Effective as of January 2, 2024, the board of directors ("Board") of Redwire Corporation (the "Company") increased the size of the Board to nine (9) directors and appointed Joanne Isham to fill the vacancy on the Board resulting from the increased Board size. She will serve as a Class I director with a term of office expiring at the Company's 2025 Annual Meeting of Stockholders. Ms. Isham will be paid in accordance with the Company’s non-employee director compensation policy. In connection with Ms. Isham's appointment to the Board, the Company and Ms. Isham entered into the Company's form of Director and Officer Indemnification Agreement, a copy of which has been filed as Exhibit 10.4 to the Company's Current Report on Form 8-K filed with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission on September 10, 2021. The Company is not aware of any related transactions or relationships between Ms. Isham and the Company that would require disclosure under Item 404(a) of Regulation S-K.
