Item 4. Ownership:
   
 
The information required by Items 4(a) - (c) is set forth in Rows (5) - (11) of the cover pages for the Reporting Persons and is incorporated herein by reference.

The percentage set forth in Row (11) of the cover pages for the Reporting Persons are based on 34,386,950 shares of Common Stock outstanding as of March 5, 2020 as reported to the Reporting Persons by the Issuer.

The terms, rights, obligations and preferences of the Series 1 Preferred Stock, par value $0.01 per share (the “Series 1 Preferred Stock”), are set forth in the Issuer’s Certificate of Designation of Series 1 Preferred Stock (the “Certificate of Designation”), which was filed with the Secretary of State of the State of Delaware on March 5, 2020.  Pursuant to the Certificate of Designation, each Series 1 Preferred Stock is convertible into 405.05 shares of Common Stock (subject to adjustment as provided in the Certificate of Designation) at any time at the option of the holder subject to the Blocker (as defined below).  Holders of Series 1 Preferred Stock are prohibited from converting shares of Series 1 Preferred Stock into shares of Common Stock if, as a result of such conversion, the holder, together with its affiliates, would beneficially own in excess of 9.9% of the total number of shares of Common Stock outstanding immediately after giving effect to the conversion (the “Blocker”). Prior to the fifth (5th) anniversary of the original issuance of the Series 1 Preferred Stock (the “Mandatory Conversion Date”), each share of Series 1 Preferred Stock will be entitled to 301.98 votes (the “Voting Rate”) on all matters on which stockholders are generally entitled to vote (provided that no holder of Series 1 Preferred Stock will be entitled to such number of votes in excess of the Blocker). Additionally, prior to the Mandatory Conversion Date, the vote or written consent of holders of a majority of the outstanding shares of Series 1 Preferred Stock, voting separately as a single class, will be required for certain amendments to the Issuer’s certificate of incorporation, to incur certain indebtedness other than permitted indebtedness, to enter into certain affiliate transactions, to issue additional shares of Series 1 Preferred Stock and to issue any capital stock senior or having parity in preference to the Series 1 Preferred Stock, other than preferred shares that may be issued in one or more financing transactions as an alternative to the Issuer incurring permitted indebtedness. After the Mandatory Conversion Date, the shares of Series 1 Preferred Stock will have no voting rights, except as required by applicable law. References to
