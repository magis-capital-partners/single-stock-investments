Item 7.01 Regulation FD Disclosure.

On September 25, 2019, Best Buy Co., Inc. (“Best Buy” or the “registrant” or the “company”) issued a news release relating to an investor update it is hosting. The purpose of the event is to highlight the progress the company has made on its Building the New Blue growth strategy, detail how it plans to move forward with the strategy and discuss the company’s long-term financial outlook.

The event will begin at 10:00 a.m. Eastern Time (9:00 a.m. Central Time) on September 25, 2019. A video webcast of the event and a question-and-answer session, as well as presentation materials, will be available both live and after the event on the registrant’s website at www.investors.bestbuy.com.

The news release issued on September 25, 2019, is furnished as Exhibit 99 to this Current Report on Form 8-K. The company’s Annual Report to Shareholders and its reports on Forms 10-K, 10-Q and 8-K and other publicly available information should be consulted for other important information about the registrant.

The information in Item 7.01 of this Current Report on Form 8-K, including Exhibit 99 hereto, shall not be deemed “filed” for purposes of Section 18 of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as amended, or otherwise subject to liability of that Section unless the registrant specifically incorporates it by reference in a document filed under the Securities Act of 1933, as amended, or the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as amended.
