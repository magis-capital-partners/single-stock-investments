## 1. Bottom line
- **The business got stronger. The cash got weaker. The stock is still priced for perfection.**
- **Revenue [F→I]:** AWS grew 39% and reported a $578B backlog (contracted future revenue), more than double a year ago. Management expects AWS growth of over 40% in both 2027 and 2028. That backlog makes AWS revenue for the next three years more visible than ever. Retail grew slowly this quarter (+6% in North America), but that was mostly Prime Day moving into Q2. Advertising slowed to 12%, which is the one real revenue soft spot.
- **Margins [F→I]:** the operating margin was 13.9%, Amazon's highest ever. The AWS margin was 38.7% even with heavy new depreciation. The North America retail margin was 7.7% (TTM 8.2%, up from 6.6%). So the profit engine is working, and the extra AI depreciation has not hurt AWS margins so far.
- **Owner earnings [F→I]:** this is where the quarter hurt. Q3 capex was $77.3B, about $20B above LSEG estimates (Reuters). Trailing free cash flow is **–$42.4B**. Management now says 2027 capex will be "meaningfully more" than ~$220B. Amazon raised $115.1B of new long-term debt in the first nine months of 2026 to help fund it. The cash owners can take out is negative today, and the date it turns positive has moved later.
- **Thesis: unchanged to slightly stronger on the business, weaker on cash and the balance sheet.** The AWS moat and the retail margin story are confirmed. The "capex normalises soon" leg is weaker.
- **IRR at $243.79 (close 2026-10-29): base 4.2%** vs the 15% hurdle (bear –9.8%, bull 14.5%). The 15% price in the base case is about **$130**. Verdict: **keep as a core compounder (hold), no new money.** That follows PRINCIPLES B3/E: hold a proven compounder unless the price is egregious or a falsifier hits.

**Red lines (would move the thesis to impaired):**
1. AWS growth falls below 25% while capex is still rising. Check at the Q2 2027 print (~late Jul 2027).
2. AWS segment margin below 32% for two quarters. That would mean AI is diluting the core's returns.
3. TTM free cash flow is still negative at the Q4 2027 report (~early Feb 2028), after 2027 capex. That would mean the payback claim failed.
4. Backlog growth falls below AWS revenue growth for two quarters. That would mean new demand is fading.
5. Amazon has to fund capex with equity, or with net debt above ~1× TTM OCF.

**What would prove our view wrong (dated):**
- *Too cautious:* capex/revenue falls toward 15% by FY2028, with AWS still growing 30%+. Then the bull column (~14.5%) is the base, and the stock was cheap at today's price. First check: FY2027 capex guide (Q4-26 print, ~early Feb 2027).
- *Too generous:* 2027 capex is above $300B and AWS growth slows below 30% in 2027. Then the base IRR is closer to the bear case.

## 6. Lens views (lens files on the box, short)
- **Buffett:** a wonderful business (buffett.md Q11 great/good/gruesome). It is drifting from "great" toward "good", because growth now needs capital in proportion to earnings. Owner earnings are below reported earnings (falsifier in §4). Hold the business; at 4% base this is not a fair price for new money.
- **Munger:** Q18 cash-take-out test: today every dollar is reinvested. Invert (Q1): the likeliest disappointment is AI capex that earns cloud-like revenue but utility-like returns. Q19: after the narrative, it is a retailer plus a cloud landlord. Both are working.
- **Hohn:** AWS sits in a three-player oligopoly with a real barrier. The return-math test is "around 15% pa without a re-rating". At 4.2% it fails. The crisis test asks whether the AI build raises or lowers the barrier: it raises it, because only a few can spend $200B+ a year. Quality yes, price no.
- **Pabrai:** a widening moat is "held until egregiously overvalued" (Jan 2021). This is not yet egregious, so hold. There is no Dhandho asymmetry at this price for new money.
- **Li Lu:** five questions. Is it cheap? No. Is it good? Yes. Why does the opportunity exist? It doesn't: this is a crowded, index-heavy AI name (G&D 2026 concentration warning).
- **Stahl / Horizon Kinetics (context only, never in base IRR):** a textbook "asset-light turning capital-intensive" case (HK Q1/Q4 2025 on hyperscalers). It is a consumer of power and chips, not the scarce provider. Heavily indexed, so "efficiently priced, or overpriced."
- **Tarasoff / TS21:** a culture and feedback-loop franchise. Run the normalised case (bull 14.5%) alongside the base, and do not anchor on reported FCF alone.

**Red-team (PRINCIPLES §F):**
- Kill case: Munger Q18 plus Stahl's capital-intensity test. If AI capex never normalises, AMZN is a ~2.6T utility earning cloud margins on a utility asset base, and the bear IRR (–10%) applies.
- Closest case: TS21 Amazon 2012 (the hurdle was wrong). That argues against over-weighting the bear case.
- We size for the base case and do not sell.

## 7. Key risks and what to watch next quarter (Q4 2026 print, ~early Feb 2027)
1. **FY2027 capex guide.** A number, not "meaningfully more". This is the single most important figure for owner earnings.
2. **Q4 cash capex.** Does it really fall to ~$45B (implied by ~$220B), or does FY2026 rise?
3. **Advertising.** Does it re-accelerate to high teens as guided? If not, the slowdown is not about timing.
4. **AWS growth and margin.** 40%+ is the 2027 promise. A margin of 35% or more is the proof that depreciation is being absorbed.
5. **Backlog growth vs revenue growth.**
6. **Debt.** New issuance, interest expense, and the size and nature of AI-lab investments (10-Q, due early Nov 2026).
7. **Amazon Leo costs and Zoox.** These are new money sinks in the Q4 guide.
