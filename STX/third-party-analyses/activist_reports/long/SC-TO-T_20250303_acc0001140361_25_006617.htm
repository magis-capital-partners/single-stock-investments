This Tender Offer Statement on Schedule TO (this “Schedule TO”) relates to the tender offer by Irvine Acquisition Holdings, Inc., a Delaware corporation (“Purchaser”) and an indirect wholly owned subsidiary of Seagate Technology Holdings plc, an Irish public limited company (“Seagate”), for all of the issued and outstanding shares of the common stock, par value $0.001 per share (the “Shares”), of Intevac, Inc., a Delaware corporation (“Intevac”), at a price per Share of $4.00, payable in cash at closing, without interest and subject to reduction for any applicable withholding taxes, pursuant to the terms and conditions set forth in the offer to purchase, dated March 3, 2025 (the “Offer to Purchase”), a copy of which is attached as Exhibit (a)(1)(A), and in the related letter of transmittal (the “Letter of Transmittal”), a copy of which is attached as Exhibit (a)(1)(B), which, as each may be amended or supplemented from time to time, collectively constitute the “Offer.”
All the information set forth in the Offer to Purchase, including Schedule I thereto, is incorporated by reference herein in response to Items 1 through 9 and Item 11 of this Schedule TO, and is supplemented by the information specifically provided in this Schedule TO.
