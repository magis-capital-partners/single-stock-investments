Item 5.03

Amendments to Articles of Incorporation or Bylaws; Change in Fiscal Year.

 

On September 29, 2020, the Board of Directors of Heritage Global Inc. (the “Company”) approved and adopted Restated Bylaws. These Restated Bylaws amended the prior bylaws to update the name and address of the Company and to allow the Company to issue uncertificated shares. The Restated Bylaws also amended the prior bylaws to permit current methods of notification and Board of Directors communications, to update the slate of authorized officer titles, delete the stockholder notification provision for certain indemnification events and to permit a single inspector of election at an annual meeting of Company.

 

The foregoing description of the Restated Bylaws does not purport to be complete and is qualified in its entirety by reference to the full text of the Restated Bylaws, attached hereto as Exhibit 3.1 to this Current Report on Form 8-K and incorporated by reference herein.
