Item 8.01Other Events

On February 13, 2023, HomeStreet Bank (the "Bank") a wholly owned subsidiary of HomeStreet, Inc. (the "Company") announced the completion of the purchase of three former MUFG Union Bank, N.A. branches in Southern California. The acquisition included deposits, customer relationships, related loans, owned real estate and fixed assets associated with the branches. We also retained the employees of the branches. As part of the purchase agreement, HomeStreet assumed approximately $400 million in deposits and purchased $22 million in loans.

A press release issued by HomeStreet Bank announcing the completion of the purchase is attached as Exhibit 99.1
