Item  8.01Other Events
On May 11, 2022, HomeStreet Bank (the “Bank”), a wholly owned subsidiary of HomeStreet, Inc. (the “Company”) entered into a Purchase and Assumption Agreement with the Bank of Idaho, a state-chartered bank headquartered in Idaho and a wholly owned subsidiary of Bank of Idaho Holding Company (“BOID”), pursuant to which BOID will acquire from the Bank an Eastern Washington branch network, including certain related assets in Spokane, Kennewick, Dayton, Yakima and Sunnyside. A press release issued by the Company and BOID is furnished herewith as Exhibit 99.1 to this Current Report on Form 8-K.

The information in the press release shall not be deemed “filed” for purposes of Section 18 of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as amended (the “Exchange Act”), or otherwise subject to the liabilities of that section. Furthermore, the information included in Exhibit 99.1 furnished under Item 9.01, shall not be deemed incorporated by reference into any filing of the Company under the Securities Act of 1933, as amended, or the Exchange Act, except as shall be expressly set forth by specific reference in any such filing.
