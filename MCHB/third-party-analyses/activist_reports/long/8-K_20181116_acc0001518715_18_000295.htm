Item 8.01.
Other Events
On November 15, 2018, HomeStreet Bank (the "Bank") a wholly owned subsidiary of HomeStreet, Inc. (the "Company") entered into a Purchase and Assumption Agreement with Silvergate Bank, a California commercial bank headquartered in San Diego ("Silvergate") and Silvergate Capital Corporation, the parent of Silvergate Bank, pursuant to which the Bank will acquire the San Marcos, California retail banking branch of Silvergate along with its business lending team. A press release issued by HomeStreet and Silvergate announcing the agreement is attached as Exhibit 99.1.

The information in this Current Report, including the press release furnished as Exhibit 99.1, shall not be deemed to be “filed” for purposes of Section 18 of the Securities and Exchange Act of 1934, as amended (the “Exchange Act”), or otherwise subject to the liabilities of that Section, and shall not be incorporated by reference into any filing under the Securities Act of 1933, as amended, or the Exchange Act, except as shall be expressly set forth by specific reference in such filing.
