Item 8.01 Other Events

 

By letter dated June 3, 2019, the State of Washington Department of Financial Institutions—Division of Banks (the “DFI”) informed Charles W. Griege, Jr. that, subject to the conditions set forth in the letter, the DFI had no objection to Mr. Griege’s Change in Control Notice filed with respect to HomeStreet, Inc. (the “Company”). A copy of the letter is attached hereto as Exhibit 99.1 to this Current Report on Form 8-K and is incorporated into this Item 8.01 by reference.
