Item 5.02 Departure of Directors or Certain Executive Officers; Election of Directors; Appointment of Certain Officers; Compensatory Arrangements of Certain Officers

On October 21, 2021, Cedric Prouvé, who is Group President, International, The Estée Lauder Companies Inc. (the “Company”), and is a Named Executive Officer in the Company’s 2021 Proxy Statement, notified the Company of his decision to retire, effective on or about June 30, 2022. Mr. Prouvé joined the Company in 1994, and the Company is grateful for his leadership and valuable contributions.

Mr. Prouvé's successor will be announced shortly, and Mr. Prouvé is expected to partner with this individual to support a smooth and successful transition.
