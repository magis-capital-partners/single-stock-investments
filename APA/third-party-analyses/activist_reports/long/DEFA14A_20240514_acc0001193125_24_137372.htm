(3) Prudently allocate capital to drilling projects to optimize long-term returns. Drilling capital efficiency is measured using the profitability index (P/I) metric, defined as the ratio of the discounted cumulative operating cash flow generated by a project relative to the discounted capital investment in the project using a 10% discount rate.
