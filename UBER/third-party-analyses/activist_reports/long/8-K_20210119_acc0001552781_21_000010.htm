Item 8.01Other Events.

 

On January 19, 2021 (the “Closing Date”), Uber Technologies, Inc. (the “Company”) completed the previously announced sale of Apparate USA LLC, the Company’s subsidiary focused on the development and commercialization of autonomous vehicles technologies, to Aurora Innovation, Inc. (“Aurora”). On the Closing Date, the Company also made a $400 million cash investment in Aurora and entered into a collaboration agreement with Aurora pursuant to which the parties will collaborate with respect to the launch and commercialization of self-driving vehicles on the Company’s ridesharing network.
