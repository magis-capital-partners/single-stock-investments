Item 5.02Departure of Directors or Principal Officers; Election of Directors; Appointment of Principal Officers

 

On August 7, 2019, the Board of Directors of Delta Air Lines, Inc. (“Delta”) elected David S. Taylor to Delta’s Board of Directors. The Board of Directors appointed Mr. Taylor to serve on the Board’s Personnel & Compensation Committee and Finance Committee. Mr. Taylor will be compensated for his service as a director on the same basis as other non-employee directors of Delta.

 

A copy of Delta’s press release announcing Mr. Taylor’s election to the Board of Directors is attached to this report as Exhibit 99.1.
