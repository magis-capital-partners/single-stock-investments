Item 5.02 Departure of Directors or Principal Officers; Election of Directors; Appointment of Principal Officers

 

(c)       Delta Air Lines, Inc. (“Delta”) today announced the promotion of John E. Laughter, 50, to Executive Vice President – Chief of Operations, effective June 1, 2021, becoming Delta’s principal operating officer. Mr. Laughter currently serves as Delta’s Senior Vice President and Chief of Operations, a position he has held since October 2020. Mr. Laughter previously served as Delta’s Senior Vice President – Flight Operations (March 2020 – October 2020), Senior Vice President - Corporate Safety, Security and Compliance (August 2013 – March 2020), Senior Vice President – Maintenance Operations (March 2008 – July 2013), and Vice President – Maintenance (December 2005 – March 2008). Mr. Laughter joined Delta in 1993.

 

As of the date of this report, no new compensatory arrangements have been entered into in connection with this appointment. The material terms of any new compensatory arrangements in connection with Mr. Laughter’s promotion will be disclosed in an amendment to this Form 8-K.
