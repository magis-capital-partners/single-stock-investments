The 2019 Annual Meeting of Shareowners of United Technologies Corporation (“UTC”) will take place on April 29, 2019, at the UTC Center for Intelligent Buildings in Palm Beach Gardens, Florida.

A voting instruction form is enclosed for your convenience, along with a pre-paid envelope addressed to the official vote tabulator, Broadridge Financial Solutions. Please remember to sign and date the form.
