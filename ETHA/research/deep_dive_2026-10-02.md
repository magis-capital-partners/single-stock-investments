## What this business is
iShares Ethereum Trust ETF (NASDAQ: **ETHA**) is a passive Delaware statutory trust sponsored by iShares Delaware Trust Sponsor LLC (BlackRock affiliate). **[Fact]** The trust holds **ether and cash only** and seeks to reflect the price of ether **before** trust expenses and liabilities. **[Fact]** Shares trade on NASDAQ; authorized participants create and redeem **40,000-share baskets** against cash and ether via prime execution and custodial arrangements. **[Fact]** The trust seeded on **May 21, 2024** and began operations on **June 24, 2024** when seed proceeds purchased roughly **3,031 ether**. **[Fact]** At **March 31, 2026**, net asset value was **$6.38 billion** on **401.88 million** shares (about **$15.87** net asset value per share), down from **$10.30 billion** and **458.72 million** shares at **December 31, 2025**, after ether fell **29%** in the quarter and redemptions exceeded creations.

---

## Why the market might be wrong
**Predictive attribute:** **wrapper tracking basis** (exchange-traded share price versus net asset value, fee drag, and after-hours ether moves versus NASDAQ session).

**[Inference]** Investors may treat ETHA as a generic “compounder” equity when the filing describes a **non-managed** vehicle with **no distributions** and a single recurring cost: the **0.25%** sponsor fee (partially waived at launch). **[Fact]** Shares **may trade at, above, or below** net asset value; non-concurrent ether market hours versus NASDAQ can widen discounts or premiums. **[Inference]** After the **July 23, 2025** fee-waiver expiry, total expense drag on assets **increased** for the first **$2.5 billion** of scale, which easy-flow inflows during 2025 may have masked. **[Inference]** Q1 2026 **net redemptions** (131.5 million shares redeemed versus 74.7 million created) may signal allocator rotation among spot ether products rather than a change in ether fundamentals alone.

## Executive summary
At **$20.37** (Yahoo close 2026-10-01), the **base case** implies about **3.9% per year** over **seven years** if filed net asset value per share compounds with **8% per year** ether growth and a **0.25%** sponsor fee, holding share count flat for illustration. The share price already sits **roughly 28% above** the last published net asset value per share (**$15.87** at March 31, 2026), so much of the ether upside is priced in. ETHA is **not** an operating compounder: economics are **look-through ether** minus fees. Marvin proposes **watch** until premium or discount to net asset value and the ether path fit the allocator mandate. Downside is ether beta plus paying a rich wrapper price; upside is regulated spot exposure if ether re-rates higher.

## Risks & inversion
**Primary risk:** Ether price declines materially and stays depressed; the trust cannot “reinvest” its way out because it is passive.

**Inversion:** If ether re-rates higher on institutional adoption, ETHA captures beta minus fees with daily liquidity versus self-custody.

- **Regulatory:** SEC and court uncertainty on ether security status (10-K risk factors).
- **Protocol:** Ethereum network changes could affect ether value (10-K risk factors).
- **Operational:** Custody, prime execution agent, trade credit facility failures (10-K / 10-Q).
- **Market structure:** Premium/discount persistence; AP withdrawal; thin trading (10-K).
- **Concentration:** Large ether holders moving price (10-K digital asset market risks).

**Lens failure mode:** Applying **owner-earnings reinvestment** math to a **zero free cash flow** wrapper, or ignoring **fee compounding** and **NAV basis** at purchase.

#### IRR arithmetic (show your work)

**Base case** (dated payoff on projected net asset value per share).

1. Starting net asset value per share **$15.87** (10-Q Mar 31, 2026).
2. Grow with **8% per year** ether path for seven years: $15.87 × 1.08^7 ≈ **$27.19**.
3. Apply cumulative **0.25% per year** fee drag: × 0.9975^7 ≈ **0.983** → projected net asset value ≈ **$26.70** per share.
4. Entry price **$20.37** (Yahoo 2026-10-01).
5. Total return factor = 26.70 ÷ 20.37 ≈ **1.311** over seven years.
6. Annual return = 1.311^(1/7) − 1 ≈ **3.9% per year**.

**Returns statement:** Base case annual return **3.9%** at price **$20.37** (ether spot grows eight percent per year in the model; sponsor fee 0.25% per year on net asset value).

**Upside / downside from price:** Buying at filed net asset value (**$15.87**) with the same ether path raises the annual return to roughly **9% per year**. Buying above net asset value, as today, compresses the return even if ether performs well.
