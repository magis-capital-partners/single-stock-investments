APPENDIX

Fiscal 2021 Approved Grant Values WHY DOES THE SUMMARY COMPENSATION TABLE REFLECT HIGHER GRANT VALUES? The equity grants for Mr. Tan, Dr. Kawwas and Mr. Krause were approved in December 2020, contingent upon stockholder support of our Stock Incentive Plan obtained at the annual meeting on April 5, 2021, which established the grant date for the GAAP value reflected in the Summary Compensation Table disclosure. Broadcom’s stock price increased from $418.06 on December 15, 2020, the effective date of the awards, to $488.48 on April 5, 2021, the actual grant date. Relative TSR is a “market-based measure” where GAAP requires the award to be valued using a Monte Carlo simulation model, which also contributed to a higher grant date share value. Award for Ms. Spears was approved and granted in December 2020 and is reported in the Summary Compensation Table using a Monte Carlo simulation model rather than the approved grant value.
