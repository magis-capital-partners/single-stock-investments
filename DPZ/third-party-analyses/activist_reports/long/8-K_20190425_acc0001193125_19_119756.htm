Item 5.07. Submission of Matters to a Vote of Security Holders.

The 2019 Annual Meeting of Shareholders of Domino’s Pizza, Inc. (the “Company”) was held on April 23, 2019. A total of 35,868,632 shares were present or represented by proxy at the meeting, representing 87.34% of all shares entitled to vote at the Annual Meeting. The matters presented for a vote and the related results are as follows:

1. ELECTION OF DIRECTORS

Proposal one was the election of nine nominees to serve as Directors of the Company, each for a term of one year. The result of the vote was as follows:
