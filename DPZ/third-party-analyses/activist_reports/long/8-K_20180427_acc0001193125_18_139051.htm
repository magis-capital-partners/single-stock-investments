Item 5.07. Submission of Matters to a Vote of Security Holders.

The 2018 Annual Meeting of Shareholders of Domino’s Pizza, Inc. (the “Company”) was held on April 24, 2018. A total of 37,856,503 shares were present or represented by proxy at the meeting, representing 87.89% of all shares entitled to vote at the Annual Meeting. The matters presented for a vote and the related results are as follows:
