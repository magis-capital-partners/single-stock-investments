Item 5.07 Submission of Matters to a Vote of Security Holders.

The 2021 Annual Meeting of Stockholders (the “Annual Meeting”) of American Tower Corporation (the “Company”) was held virtually on May 26, 2021. At the Annual Meeting, the Company's stockholders elected twelve individuals to the Board of Directors (the “Board”), approved Proposals 2 and 3 and rejected Proposals 4 and 5. The proposals are described in more detail in the Company's definitive proxy statement on Schedule 14A filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission on April 14, 2021.
