Item 8.01    Other Events.

On October 18, 2021, American Tower Corporation (the “Company”) issued a press release (the “Press Release”) announcing that it had completed its previously announced redemption of all of its outstanding 4.70% senior unsecured notes due 2022 (the “4.70% Notes”). In accordance with the redemption provisions of the 4.70% Notes and the Indenture, dated as of May 13, 2010, as supplemented by the Supplemental Indenture No. 5, dated as of March 12, 2012, between the Company and The Bank of New York Mellon Trust Company, N.A., as trustee, the 4.70% Notes were redeemed at a price equal to 101.7270% of the principal amount of the 4.70% Notes. In addition, the Company paid accrued and unpaid interest on the 4.70% Notes up to, but excluding, October 18, 2021.

A copy of the Press Release announcing the redemption of the 4.70% Notes is filed herewith as Exhibit 99.1 and incorporated herein by reference.
