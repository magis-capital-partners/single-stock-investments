Item 5.07    Submission of Matters to a Vote of Security Holders.

The 2023 Annual Meeting of Stockholders (the “Annual Meeting”) of American Tower Corporation (the “Company”) was held virtually on May 24, 2023. At the Annual Meeting, the Company's stockholders elected twelve individuals to the Board of Directors (the “Board”), approved Proposals 2 and 3 and voted for one year on Proposal 4. The proposals are described in more detail in the Company's definitive proxy statement on Schedule 14A filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission on April 12, 2023.
