Item 7.01    Regulation FD Disclosure.

United Airlines, Inc. ("United"), a wholly-owned subsidiary of United Airlines Holdings, Inc. ("UAL" and, together with United, the "Company"), has experienced an acceleration in ticketed yields for the second quarter, ahead of previous expectations. As of the date of this report, consolidated yields on tickets issued since the beginning of May 2021 for travel in the second quarter 2021 have reached levels similar to 2019, with domestic leisure yields exceeding 2019 levels for the same time period. As a result, the Company now expects Total Revenue per Available Seat Mile (TRASM) to be down around 12% for the second quarter 2021 versus the second quarter 2019. This compares to previous guidance of down approximately 20%.

Due to the recent reduction in the Company's flying to India and Israel1 which the Company expects to continue in the near-term, the Company now expects second quarter 2021 capacity to be down at least 46% compared to second quarter 2019. This compares to previous guidance of down approximately 45%. Second quarter 2021 adjusted operating expenses2 excluding special charges are expected to decline 33% versus second quarter 2019, as compared to previous guidance of a decline of 32%.

As a result of improved second quarter 2021 yield performance, adjusted EBITDA margin3 for second quarter 2021 is now expected to be around (11%) as compared to previous guidance of around (20%). Additionally, the Company expects to achieve positive adjusted EBITDA3 in the month of June 2021.

The Company continues to expect domestic leisure yields for summer travel4 to exceed 2019 levels. Business demand continues to be significantly depressed, though bookings for business travel are starting to recover. As a result of these trends the Company now expects third quarter 2021 adjusted EBITDA3 to be positive.
