Item 7.01   Regulation FD Disclosure.

 

As of March 13, 2019, United Airlines, Inc. (“United”), a wholly owned subsidiary of United Continental Holdings, Inc. (“UAL,” and together with United, the “Company”), was operating 14 Boeing 737 MAX aircraft that accounted for approximately 40 flights a day.  At this time, the Company is not experiencing a significant operational or financial impact as a result of the Federal Aviation Administration order on March 13, 2019 grounding these aircraft.  However, the financial and operational impact to the Company is expected to increase if the grounding extends into the peak summer travel season.  For additional information regarding the Company’s financial outlook, please see the Company’s Current Report on Form 8-K dated March 5, 2019.

 

The information in this Item 7.01 is being furnished and shall not be deemed to be “filed” for purposes of Section 18 of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as amended, (the “Exchange Act”) or otherwise subject to the liabilities of that Section and shall not be deemed incorporated by reference into any registration statement or other document filed pursuant to the Securities Act of 1933, as amended, except as shall be expressly set forth by specific reference in such filing.
