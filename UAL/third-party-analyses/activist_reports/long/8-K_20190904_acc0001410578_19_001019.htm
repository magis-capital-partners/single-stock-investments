Item 7.01  Regulation FD Disclosure.

 

United Airlines Holdings, Inc. (“UAL”), the holding company whose primary subsidiary is United Airlines, Inc. (“United,” and together with UAL, the “Company”), is providing guidance regarding its financial and operational trends in connection with a presentation by Gerald Laderman, Executive Vice President and Chief Financial Officer, and Andrew Nocella, Executive Vice President and Chief Commercial Officer, of the Company, at the Cowen and Company 12th Annual Global Transportation Conference on September 4, 2019. As previously announced, this presentation will be webcast.

 

The Company expects third-quarter 2019 consolidated passenger unit revenue to be near the midpoint of the previously provided guidance range of up 0.5 percent to 2.5 percent compared to the third-quarter of 2018. This incorporates an approximately 50 basis point headwind due to weaker than expected demand trends in China, including Hong Kong. The Company also continues to expect third-quarter 2019 adjusted pre-tax margin(1) to be between 10 percent and 12 percent and full-year 2019 adjusted diluted earnings per share(1) to be between $10.50 and $12.

 

The information in this Item 7.01 is being furnished and shall not be deemed to be “filed” for purposes of Section 18 of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as amended, (the “Exchange Act”) or otherwise subject to the liabilities of that Section and shall not be deemed incorporated by reference into any registration statement or other document filed pursuant to the Securities Act of 1933, as amended, except as shall be expressly set forth by specific reference in such filing.
