Item 8.01

Other Events.

On May 9, 2019, United Continental Holdings, Inc. (“UAL”) issued in a public offering $350,000,000 principal amount of its 4.875% Senior Notes due 2025 (the “Notes”), which are guaranteed (the “Guarantee”) by UAL’s wholly-owned subsidiary United Airlines, Inc. (“United”). The issuance of the Notes and the Guarantee were registered pursuant to UAL’s and United’s automatic shelf registration statement on Form S-3 (Registration Nos. 333-221865 and 333-221865-1) (the “Registration Statement”), filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission on December 1, 2017. This Current Report on Form 8-K is being filed for the purpose of adding the opinion of Hughes Hubbard & Reed LLP that the Notes and the Guarantee are binding obligations as an exhibit with respect to the Registration Statement. See Item 9.01 of this Current Report on Form 8-K.
