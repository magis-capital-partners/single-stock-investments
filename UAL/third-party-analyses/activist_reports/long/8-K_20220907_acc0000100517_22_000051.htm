Item 7.01    Regulation FD Disclosure.
United Airlines Holdings, Inc. ("UAL"), the holding company whose subsidiary is United Airlines, Inc. ("United," and together with UAL, the "Company"), is providing an update regarding its financial outlook for third quarter 2022.

Exiting a robust summer, the Company continues to see a strong demand environment and now expects third quarter 2022 total operating revenue to be up around 12% versus third quarter 2019. The Company has seen improving operational reliability over the course of 2022. As a result, third quarter 2022 capacity is trending higher than originally expected and is now expected to be down 10% to 11% versus third quarter 2019. With costs in line with, to slightly better than original expectations but spread over greater capacity, the Company now expects third quarter 2022 CASM-ex1 to be up around 16% versus third quarter 2019. As a result, adjusted operating margin2 is expected to improve to approximately 10.5%.
