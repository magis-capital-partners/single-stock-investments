Item 8.01Other Events

 

On June 25, 2020, United Airlines, Inc. (“United”) issued a press release announcing the pricing and upsize of the previously announced private offering by Mileage Plus Holdings, LLC (“MPH”), a direct wholly-owned subsidiary of United, and Mileage Plus Intellectual Property Assets, Ltd., an indirect wholly-owned subsidiary of MPH (“MIPA”). An aggregate of $3.8 billion in principal amount of 6.50% senior secured notes due 2027 (the “Notes”) is expected to be issued on July 2, 2020, subject to customary closing conditions. Concurrently with the issuance of the Notes, United expects MPH and MIPA to enter into a credit agreement providing for a term loan facility (the “Term Loan Facility”) for an aggregate of $3.0 billion, also subject to customary closing conditions. The Notes and the Term Loan Facility, in a total aggregate amount of $6.8 billion, replace the previously announced committed term loan facility.

 

The press release is filed as Exhibit 99.1 to this Form 8-K.
