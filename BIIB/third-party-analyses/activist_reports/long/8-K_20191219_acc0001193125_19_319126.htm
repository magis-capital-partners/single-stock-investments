Item 8.01 Other Events.

On December 19, 2019, the Board of Directors of Biogen Inc. (the “Company”) authorized a program to repurchase up to $5.0 billion of the Company’s common stock (the “December 2019 Share Repurchase Program”). The December 2019 Share Repurchase Program does not have an expiration date. All share repurchases under the December 2019 Share Repurchase Program will be retired. The December 2019 Share Repurchase Program is in addition to the share repurchase program authorized by the Board of Directors of the Company in March 2019.
