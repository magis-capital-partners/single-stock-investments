Item 8.01.

Other Events.

Walmart Inc., a Delaware corporation (the “Company”), and BNP Paribas Securities Corp., BofA Securities, Inc., Morgan Stanley & Co. LLC, Citigroup Global Markets Inc., Goldman Sachs & Co. LLC and Wells Fargo Securities, LLC, acting for themselves and as representatives of the other several underwriters named in Schedule I to the U.S. Offering Pricing Agreement (as defined below) (collectively, the “U.S. Offering Underwriters”), have entered into a Pricing Agreement, dated October 19, 2026 (the “U.S. Offering Pricing Agreement”), pursuant to which, subject to the satisfaction of the conditions set forth therein, the Company has agreed to sell to the U.S. Offering Underwriters, and the U.S. Offering Underwriters have agreed to purchase from the Company, $750,000,000 aggregate principal amount of the Company’s 5.050% Notes Due 2029 (the “2029 Notes”), $500,000,000 aggregate principal amount of the Company’s 5.150% Notes Due 2031 (the “2031 Notes”), $1,000,000,000 aggregate principal amount of the Company’s 5.350% Notes Due 2033 (the “2033 U.S. Notes”) and $750,000,000 aggregate principal amount of the Company’s 5.550% Notes Due 2036 (the “2036 Notes” and, collectively with the 2029 Notes, the 2031 Notes and the 2033 U.S. Notes, the “U.S. Notes”). The U.S. Offering Pricing Agreement incorporates by reference the terms and conditions of an Underwriting Agreement, dated October 19, 2026, between the Company and the U.S. Offering Underwriters (the “U.S. Offering Underwriting Agreement” and, together with the U.S. Offering Pricing Agreement, the “U.S. Offering Agreement”). The Company and the U.S. Offering Underwriters expect to consummate the sale and purchase of the U.S. Notes pursuant to the U.S. Offering Agreement on October 23, 2026.

The Company and Merrill Lynch Canada Inc., Scotia Capital Inc. and TD Securities Inc., acting for themselves and as representatives of the other several underwriters named in Schedule I to the Canadian Offering Pricing Agreement (as defined below) (collectively, the “Canadian Offering Underwriters”), have entered into a Pricing Agreement, dated October 19, 2026 (the “Canadian Offering Pricing Agreement”), pursuant to which, subject to the satisfaction of the conditions set forth therein, the Company has agreed to sell to the Canadian Offering Underwriters, and the Canadian Offering Underwriters have agreed to purchase from the Company, C$750,000,000 aggregate principal amount of the Company’s 4.150% Notes Due 2030 (the “2030 Notes”), C$2,000,000,000 aggregate principal amount of the Company’s 4.450% Notes Due 2033 (the “2033 Canadian Notes”), C$1,750,000,000 aggregate principal amount of the Company’s 4.950% Notes Due 2038 (the “2038 Notes”) and C$500,000,000 aggregate principal amount of the Company’s 5.250% Notes Due 2056 (the “2056 Notes” and, collectively with the 2030 Notes, the 2033 Canadian Notes and the 2038 Notes, the “Canadian Notes” and, collectively with the U.S. Notes, the “Notes”). The Canadian Offering Pricing Agreement incorporates by reference the terms and conditions of an Underwriting Agreement, dated October 19, 2026, between the Company and the Canadian Offering Underwriters (the “Canadian Offering Underwriting Agreement” and, together with the Canadian Offering Pricing Agreement, the “Canadian Offering Agreement”). The Company and the Canadian Offering Underwriters expect to consummate the sale and purchase of the Canadian Notes pursuant to the Canadian Offering Agreement on October 23, 2026.

The 2029 Notes will be sold to the public at a price equal to 99.994% of the aggregate principal amount of the 2029 Notes. The net proceeds to the Company from the sale of the 2029 Notes, after the underwriting discount, but before transaction expenses allocable to the sale of the 2029 Notes, will be $748,080,000.

The 2030 Notes will be sold to the public at a price equal to 99.996% of the aggregate principal amount of the 2030 Notes. The net proceeds to the Company from the sale of the 2030 Notes, after the underwriting discount, but before transaction expenses allocable to the sale of the 2030 Notes, will be C$747,720,000.

The 2031 Notes will be sold to the public at a price equal to 99.800% of the aggregate principal amount of the 2031 Notes. The net proceeds to the Company from the sale of the 2031 Notes, after the underwriting discount, but before transaction expenses allocable to the sale of the 2031 Notes, will be $497,250,000.

The 2033 U.S. Notes will be sold to the public at a price equal to 99.873% of the aggregate principal amount of the 2033 U.S. Notes. The net proceeds to the Company from the sale of the 2033 U.S. Notes, after the underwriting discount, but before transaction expenses allocable to the sale of the 2033 U.S. Notes, will be $994,730,000.

The 2033 Canadian Notes will be sold to the public at a price equal to 99.946% of the aggregate principal amount of the 2033 Canadian Notes. The net proceeds to the Company from the sale of the 2033 Canadian Notes, after the underwriting discount, but before transaction expenses allocable to the sale of the 2033 Canadian Notes, will be C$1,991,520,000.

The 2036 Notes will be sold to the public at a price equal to 99.780% of the aggregate principal amount of the 2036 Notes. The net proceeds to the Company from the sale of the 2036 Notes, after the underwriting discount, but before transaction expenses allocable to the sale of the 2036 Notes, will be $744,975,000.

The 2038 Notes will be sold to the public at a price equal to 99.991% of the aggregate principal amount of the 2038 Notes. The net proceeds to the Company from the sale of the 2038 Notes, after the underwriting discount, but before transaction expenses allocable to the sale of the 2038 Notes, will be C$1,742,842,500.

The 2056 Notes will be sold to the public at a price equal to 99.805% of the aggregate principal amount of the 2056 Notes. The net proceeds to the Company from the sale of the 2056 Notes, after the underwriting discount, but before transaction expenses allocable to the sale of the 2056 Notes, will be C$496,525,000.

The U.S. Notes and Canadian Notes will be sold to the public at an aggregate price of $2,996,035,000 and C$4,997,757,500, respectively, before underwriting discounts and transaction expenses allocable to the sale of the U.S. Notes and Canadian Notes, as applicable. The aggregate net proceeds to the Company from the sale of the U.S. Notes and Canadian Notes, after underwriting discounts, but before transaction expenses allocable to the sale of the Notes, will be $2,985,035,000 and C$4,978,607,500, respectively.

The 2029 Notes will constitute part of the Company’s newly created series of 5.050% Notes Due 2029 (the “2029 Series”), the 2030 Notes will constitute part of the Company’s newly created series of 4.150% Notes Due 2030 (the “2030 Series”), the 2031 Notes will constitute part of the Company’s newly created series of 5.150% Notes Due 2031 (the “2031 Series”), the 2033 U.S. Notes will constitute part of the Company’s newly created series of 5.350% Notes Due 2033 (the “2033 U.S. Series”), the 2033 Canadian Notes will constitute part of the Company’s newly created series of 4.450% Notes Due 2033 (the “2033 Canadian Series”), the 2036 Notes will constitute part of the Company’s newly created series of 5.550% Notes Due 2036 (the “2036 Series”), the 2038 Notes will constitute part of the Company’s newly created series of 4.950% Notes Due 2038 (the “2038 Series”) and the 2056 Notes will constitute part of the Company’s newly created series of 5.250% Notes Due 2056 (the “2056 Series” and, collectively with the 2029 Series, the 2030 Series, the 2031 Series, the 2033 U.S. Series, the 2033 Canadian Series, the 2036 Series and the 2038 Series, the “New Series”). The Notes of each New Series will be senior, unsecured debt securities of the Company and will rank equally with each other and with all of the other senior, unsecured debt obligations of the Company.

The New Series were created and established, and the terms and conditions of each New Series were established, by action of the Company and an authorized officer of the Company pursuant to, and in accordance with, the terms of the Indenture, dated as of July 19, 2005, as amended and supplemented (the “Indenture”), between the Company and The Bank of New York Mellon Trust Company, N.A., as successor trustee (the “Trustee”), and the Indenture and the related series terms certificates pursuant to the Indenture will govern the Notes of each New Series. The respective terms of the 2029 Notes, the 2031 Notes, the 2033 U.S. Notes and the 2036 Notes are as set forth in the Indenture and in the forms of the Global Notes (referred to below) that will represent the Notes of the 2029 Series, the 2031 Series, the 2033 U.S. Series and the 2036 Series, respectively, to be sold pursuant to the U.S. Offering Pricing Agreement. The respective terms of the 2030 Notes, the 2033 Canadian Notes, the 2038 Notes and the 2056 Notes are as set forth in the Indenture and in the forms of the Global Notes (referred to below) that will represent the Notes of the 2030 Series, the 2033 Canadian Series, the 2038 Series and the 2056 Series, respectively, to be sold pursuant to the Canadian Offering Pricing Agreement.

The material terms of the U.S. Notes are described in the Company’s prospectus supplement dated October 19, 2026, which relates to the offer and sale of the U.S. Notes (the “U.S. Prospectus Supplement”), and the Company’s prospectus dated December 4, 2023, which relates to the offer and sale from time to time of an

 

indeterminate amount of the Company’s debt securities, including the Notes (the “Prospectus”). A Final Term Sheet, dated October 19, 2026, relating to, and setting forth certain terms of, the U.S. Notes was filed with the Commission pursuant to Rule 433 under the Securities Act on October 20, 2026.

The material terms of the Canadian Notes are described in the Company’s prospectus supplement dated October 19, 2026, which relates to the offer and sale of the Canadian Notes (the “Canadian Prospectus Supplement”), and the Prospectus. The Canadian Prospectus Supplement, together with the Prospectus, was filed by the Company with the Commission pursuant to Rule 424(b)(5) under the Securities Act on October 21, 2026, in connection with the offer and sale of the Canadian Notes. A Final Term Sheet, dated October 19, 2026, relating to, and setting forth certain terms of, the Canadian Notes was filed with the Commission pursuant to Rule 433 under the Securities Act on October 20, 2026.

The Notes of each New Series will be initially issued and delivered in book-entry form only and will be represented by one or more global notes, which will be in definitive, fully registered form without interest coupons. The 2029 Notes will be represented by two global notes, one of which will be in the principal amount of $500,000,000 and one of which will be in the principal amount of $250,000,000 (the “2029 Global Notes”). The 2031 Notes will be represented by one global note in the principal amount of $500,000,000 (the “2031 Global Note”). The 2033 U.S. Notes will be represented by two global notes, each of which will be in the principal amount of $500,000,000 (the “2033 U.S. Global Notes”). The 2036 Notes will be represented by two global notes, one of which will be in the principal amount of $500,000,000 and one of which will be in the principal amount of $250,000,000 (the “2036 Global Notes” and, collectively with the 2029 Global Notes, the 2031 Global Note and the 2033 U.S. Global Notes, the “U.S. Global Notes”). Each U.S. Global Note will be payable to Cede & Co., a New York corporation, as nominee of The Depository Trust Company. The U.S. Global Notes will be executed by the Company and authenticated by the Trustee in accordance with the Indenture.

The 2030 Notes will be represented by one global note, which will be in the principal amount of C$750,000,000 (the “2030 Global Note”). The 2033 Canadian Notes will be represented by one global note, which will be in the principal amount of C$2,000,000,000 (the “2033 Canadian Global Note”). The 2038 Notes will be represented by one global note, which will be in the principal amount of C$1,750,000,000 (the “2038 Global Note”). The 2056 Notes will be represented by one global note in the principal amount of C$500,000,000 (the “2056 Global Note” and, collectively with the 2030 Global Note, the 2033 Canadian Global Note and the 2038 Global Note, the “Canadian Global Notes” and, the Canadian Global Notes together with the U.S. Global Notes, the “Global Notes”). Each Canadian Global Note will be payable to CDS & Co., as nominee of CDS Clearing and Depository Services Inc. The Canadian Global Notes will be executed by the Company and authenticated by the Trustee through the Trustee’s authenticating agent in accordance with the Indenture.
