Item 7.01 Regulation FD Disclosure

On October 26, 2018, Phillips 66 (the “Company”) hosted a conference call webcast to discuss the Company’s third-quarter 2018 financial results.  On the call, in response to a question, Chairman and CEO Greg Garland stated that he expects the Company’s 2019 consolidated capital budget to be between $2 billion and $2.5 billion.  Mr. Garland’s statement referred to the expectation of the Company’s growth capital in 2019.
