Item 8.01
Other Events.

On November 14, 2018, Markel Corporation (“Markel”) and Nephila Holdings Ltd. (“Nephila”) issued a joint press release announcing that Markel has completed its acquisition of Nephila.

Nephila is a leading investment manager specializing in reinsurance and weather risk transfer and ESG-driven business. Nephila offers a broad range of investment products focusing on instruments such as insurance-linked securities, catastrophe bonds, insurance swaps, and weather derivatives. Nephila, whose revenue is driven primarily through management and incentive fees, has assets under management of approximately $12.3 billion as of October 1, 2018 for over 300 geographically diverse investors and has been managing institutional assets in this space since it was founded in 1998. The firm has over 200 employees.

A copy of the joint press release is furnished as Exhibit 99.1 to this report.
