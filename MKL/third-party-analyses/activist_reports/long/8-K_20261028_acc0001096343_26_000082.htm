Item 1.01Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement

On October 27, 2026, Markel Global Reinsurance Company, Markel Insurance Company, Evanston Insurance Company, Markel American Insurance Company, and Markel Bermuda Limited (collectively, the Ceding Companies), each a subsidiary of Markel Group Inc. (the Company), and solely for limited purposes, Markel Insurance Group, LLC and Markel Services, Inc., entered into an Adverse Development Cover Agreement (the Agreement) with Cavello Bay Reinsurance Limited (the Reinsurer), a wholly owned subsidiary of Enstar Group Limited. The Agreement covers a portfolio of the run-off Global Reinsurance business and certain exited lines within its insurance operations (the Subject Business) and net reserves of $4.8 billion (the Reserves).

Pursuant to the Agreement, the Reinsurer will reinsure, on an indemnity basis, 100% of covered losses paid by the Ceding Companies on or after April 1, 2026 (the Effective Date) in respect of the Subject Business exceeding an aggregate retention of $2.6 billion. After giving effect to the retention, the Agreement provides coverage for $2.3 billion of the Reserves and $1.2 billion of additional coverage for adverse development above the Reserves.

Under the Agreement: (a) the Ceding Companies will pay the Reinsurer a reinsurance premium of $2.4 billion; (b) the reinsurance premium, less an amount equal to applicable federal excise tax, will be satisfied through a transfer of assets at closing, inclusive of any earnings, gains, or losses on such assets between signing and closing (the Transferred Assets); (c) $500 million of the Transferred Assets will be transferred to the Reinsurer outright and the balance will be deposited into a trust account for the benefit of the Ceding Companies to secure the Reinsurer’s obligations; (d) the Ceding Companies will retain claims control, subject to certain claims protocols in accordance with the Agreement; and (e) the Reinsurer will be required to maintain collateral in the trust account, with collateral requirements subject to increase upon the occurrence of certain events. The Agreement includes customary representations and warranties, indemnification obligations, covenants, and termination rights of the parties. The Agreement provides the Ceding Companies with the right to commute the agreement upon the occurrence of certain recapture trigger events. The closing of the transaction is subject to regulatory approval and other customary closing conditions and is expected to occur by the end of the fourth quarter of 2026.

The foregoing description of the Agreement is a summary and is qualified in its entirety by reference to the Agreement filed as Exhibit 10.1 hereto and incorporated herein by reference.
