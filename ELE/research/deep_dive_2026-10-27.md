## Business & moat

Elemental gets paid when other companies mine. It does not run the mines. **[Fact]** 2025 royalty revenue was $43.359 million. Other revenue was small. Operating cash rose from $4.816 million in 2024 to $30.811 million in 2025, and borrowings fell from $2.687 million to zero. **[Fact]** Investing cash outflow was $56.325 million in 2025, while purchases of property and plant were $4,000. The investing outflow is portfolio spending, not sustaining plant. Profit before tax was $6.280 million and profit for the year was $1.772 million. **[Fact]** Those are different lines. Do not quote the pretax figure as earnings.

The share count is the dilution fact that matters. Ending shares are more than double the weighted average. A per-share cash figure that uses the average would overstate what each share owns today. This pass uses ending shares.

### Mental models

The royalty holder is a toll on someone else's mine. The toll is only as good as the mines, the contracts, and the metal price. A merger year mixes two histories into one column. Inversion: if the combined portfolio does not earn more than 2025 operating cash, the current price is a high multiple of a number that will not repeat upward.

### Business mechanics

Cash comes in as royalties, and a thin layer of other income. Cash goes out as overhead, tax, and new royalties. Debt was paid off. The company can still issue shares. 2025 proceeds from issuing shares were $100 million. **[Fact]** That is financing, not operating cash.

### Moat

The moat is the contract file: royalties that keep paying after the purchase price is spent. **[Inference]** One reported year of the combined company is not enough to call that file wide. Narrow is the classification, and it is a judgment, not a filing line.

## Risks & inversion

**Primary risk:** 2025 operating cash is not a run rate, and a later acquisition can change both the cash and the share count before a buyer is paid.

- Metal prices and mine volumes can fall. The model does not forecast that. It sets growth to zero.
- Share issuance already doubled the count inside 2025. More equity would cut cash per share again.
- Counterparty and asset detail for 2026 is a gap. A concentrated mine would matter, and this pass does not pretend to have the latest production table.

**Lens failure mode:** Treating a royalty file as a bond. The contracts are real. The metal price and the mine plans are not a coupon.

**Upside / downside from price:** Upside is a full year of combined royalties that clears the $15.60 price on flat or better cash. Downside is that 2025 cash was the high point of a merger year and the share count stays elevated.
