Item 8.01 Other Events.

As previously disclosed, on April 19, 2023, Digital World Acquisition Corp., a Delaware corporation (the “Company”), received a notice from the Listing Qualifications Department of The Nasdaq Stock Market LLC (“Nasdaq”) stating that it was not in compliance with Nasdaq Listing Rule 5250(c)(1) (the “Rule”) because it had not yet filed its Annual Report on Form 10-K for the period ended December 31, 2022 (the “Form 10-K”) with the Securities and Exchange Commission (the “SEC”). The Rule requires listed companies to timely file all required periodic financial reports with the SEC.

On April 26, 2023, the Company received a notice from Nasdaq indicating that, as a result of the April 26, 2023 filing of the Company’s Annual Report Form 10-K for the year ended December 31, 2022, Nasdaq has determined that the Company has regained compliance with the Rule and this matter is now closed.

On April 27, 2023, the Company issued a press release announcing the Company has regained compliance with the Rule. A copy of the press release is attached hereto as Exhibit 99.1 and is incorporated herein by reference.
