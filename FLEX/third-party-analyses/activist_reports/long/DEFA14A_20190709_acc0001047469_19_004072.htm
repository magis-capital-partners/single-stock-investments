Dear Shareholder,

FLEX LTD. (the "Company")
ANNUAL REPORT 2019, AGM NOTICE, EGM NOTICE, PROXY STATEMENT, FORM 10-K AND AUDITED SINGAPORE FINANCIAL STATEMENTS

        We are pleased to inform you that our Annual Report for the financial year ended March 31, 2019 ("AR 2019"), Notice of Annual General Meeting dated July 9, 2019 ("AGM Notice"), Notice of Extraordinary General Meeting dated July 9, 2019 ("EGM Notice"), Joint Proxy Statement dated July 9, 2019 ("Proxy Statement"), Form 10-K for the financial year ended March 31, 2019 ("Form 10-K") and Singapore audited financial statements for the financial year ended March 31, 2019 ("Financial Statements") will be available on the Company's website at www.flex.com (the "Flex Website") on and from July 9, 2019. This is part of our ongoing green initiative to reduce paper usage and contribute to greater environmental sustainability

AGM and EGM Materials hosted on the Flex Website

        The AR 2019, AGM Notice, EGM Notice, Proxy Statement, Form 10-K and Financial Statements will be found under "Financials" in the "Investor Relations" section of the Flex Website. As the AR 2019, AGM Notice, EGM Notice, Proxy Statement, Form 10-K and Financial Statements are in Portable Document Format (PDF), you may need a PDF viewer software or application. To open and/or save the relevant document, click on the link to the document.

AGM and EGM Materials hosted on the Broadridge Website

        The AR 2019, AGM Notice, EGM Notice and Proxy Statement have also been made available on Broadridge's website at www.proxyvote.com (the "Broadridge Website") on and from July 9, 2019. To access such materials, you will need the information in the box marked by the arrow set forth on the Notice of Availability of Proxy Materials on the Internet that has been provided to you along with this letter. As with the process to access the AGM and EGM materials described above, you may need a PDF viewer software or application. To open and/or save the relevant document, click on the link to the document and follow the instructions for opening and/or saving the document.

        The latest version of Adobe Acrobat Reader is available free of charge at https://get.adobe.com/reader/ to anyone that wishes to use it to view PDF files.

Thank you for your understanding and support.
