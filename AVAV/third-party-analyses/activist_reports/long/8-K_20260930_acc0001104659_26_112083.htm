Item 5.07. Submission of Matters to a Vote of Security Holders.

 

On September 24, 2026, AeroVironment, Inc. (the “Company”) held its 2026 Annual Meeting of Stockholders (the “Annual Meeting”). Below is a brief description of each matter submitted to a vote at the Annual Meeting, as well as the final voting results with respect to each matter. For more information about these proposals, please refer to the Company’s 2026 Proxy Statement.

 

Proposal 1 — Election of Directors

 

The Company’s stockholders elected the five persons nominated by the Board of Directors as Class I and Class II directors for a one-year term as follows:
