Item 8.01. Other Events.

 

On June 12, 2023, MergerMarket published an article containing certain statements attributed to an officer of AST SpaceMobile, Inc. (“we” “us” or the “Company”) with respect to which we are providing certain clarifications and corrections.

 

In particular:

 

  ●

The MergerMarket article incorrectly stated that we have a burn rate for operational expenses of around $40 million annually. As we have previously disclosed, we are currently using cash at the rate of approximately $40 million per quarter for adjusted operating expenses. We understand that MergerMarket attempted to correct their error in later versions of the article by changing “annually” to “quarterly” but did not change “operational expenses” to “adjusted operating expenses”, which is the correct line item.

  ● The MergerMarket article stated that the company’s audit opinion validated our projections of more than 12 months in cash. That statement was intended to state that our independent auditors had issued their audit report with respect to our financial statements as of and for the fiscal years ended December 31, 2021 and 2022 on March 31, 2023 without a going concern paragraph, and we issued our interim financial statements for the quarter ended March 31, 2023 without a going concern qualification. Our need for capital in the future may result in our independent auditors or management expressing substantial doubt about our ability to continue as a going concern in future financial statements.
  ● The MergerMarket article indicated that we are “engaging with strategics to fund future launches,” “engaging with potential strategic partners…through non-dilutive sources as well as equity,” “racing forward” with potential strategic partners and “exploring a range of options for bringing in revenue.” As previously disclosed, we require a substantial amount of capital, and we expect such requirements will continue to increase in the future. We are regularly in discussions with various financing sources to enhance liquidity, including strategic partners, and have considered and will continue to consider numerous options to raise capital. We may raise additional funds through the issuance of equity, equity-linked or debt securities (secured or unsecured) and/or incurrence of secured or unsecured loans or other debt facilities. However, we have not obtained any commitments or entered into any agreement with respect to financing from strategic partners and no such transaction with a strategic partner is imminent. Although four of our six strategic partners who are investors in the Company have invested on two occasions, there can be no assurance that any of these investors will continue to invest in the Company and we have no current agreements with any of these parties, or any other strategic partner, to invest in the Company.

Non-GAAP Financial Measures

 

Adjusted operating expense is an alternative financial measure used by management to evaluate our operating performance as a supplement to our most directly comparable U.S. GAAP financial measure. We define Adjusted operating expenses as Total operating expenses adjusted to exclude amounts of stock-based compensation expense and depreciation and amortization expense. We believe Adjusted operating expense is a useful measure across time in evaluating the Company’s operating performance as we use Adjusted operating expenses to manage the business, including in preparing our annual operating budget and financial projections. Adjusted operating expenses is a non-GAAP financial measure that has no standardized meaning prescribed by GAAP, and therefore has limits in its usefulness to investors. Because of the nonstandardized definition, it may not be comparable to the calculation of similar measures of other companies and are presented solely to provide investors with useful information to more fully understand how management assesses performance. This measure is not, and should not be viewed as, a substitute for its most directly comparable GAAP measure of Total operating expenses.
