Item 8.01. Other Events.

 

On September 8, 2022, AST SpaceMobile, Inc. (“AST SpaceMobile” or the “Company”) announced that its BlueWalker 3 (BW3) test satellite is planned to reach orbit on September 10, 2022 from Kennedy Space Center in Cape Canaveral, Florida.

 

The actual date BW3 reaches orbit remains subject to change and is contingent on a number of factors including, but not limited to, timely readiness of the launch vehicle, weather conditions, technical problems and other unknowns.
