Item 5.02. Departure of Directors or Certain Officers; Election of Directors;

On June 9, 2023, Mr. Dennis J. Scott, Senior Vice President, General Counsel, Chief Compliance Officer and Corporate Secretary of Centrus Energy Corp. (the “Company”) provided notice that he will retire from his positions at the Company, effective August 11, 2023. Mr. Scott’s decision to retire is not the result of any dispute or disagreement with the Company, the Company’s management or the Board of Directors of the Company on any matter relating to the Company’s operations, policies or practices. Effective upon Mr. Scott’s retirement, Mr. Shahram Ghasemian will be appointed as Acting General Counsel, Chief Compliance Officer & Corporate Secretary of the Company. Mr. Ghasemian currently serves as the Senior Assistant General Counsel and Director, Legal Affairs and Corporate Compliance.
