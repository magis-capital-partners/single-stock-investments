Item 7.01 Regulation FD Disclosure.

 

On October 20, 2021, Centrus Energy Corp. announced the commencement of a tender offer to purchase all of its issued and outstanding Series B Senior Preferred Stock, par value $1.00 per share (the “Series B Preferred Shares”), at a price of $1,145.20 per Series B Preferred Share (inclusive of any rights to accrued but unpaid dividends), to each holder in cash, less any applicable withholding taxes (the “Offer”), and the related consent solicitation (the “Consent Solicitation”).
