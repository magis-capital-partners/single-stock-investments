Item 8.01 Other Events.

Wyoming Acquisition

On August 1, 2024, CleanSpark, Inc. (the "Company") announced the execution of 75 MW of power contracts and the completion of the acquisition of its first Bitcoin mining site in Wyoming. On July 26, 2024, the Company acquired a Bitcoin mining site in Cheyenne, Wyoming with an initial power capacity of 30 MW. This site is expected to add over 2 exahashes per second (EH/s) to the Company's hashrate once operational. Groundbreaking is planned to start immediately with an expected operational date prior to the end of 2024. The Company has also executed power contracts for an additional 45 MW at a second site in Wyoming, expected to contribute an additional 3 EH/s to the Company's hashrate.

 

A copy of the press release is attached as Exhibit 99.1 to this Current Report on Form 8-K and is incorporated by reference herein.
