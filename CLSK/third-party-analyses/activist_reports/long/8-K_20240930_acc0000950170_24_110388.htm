Item 7.01 Regulation FD Disclosure.

On September 30, 2024, CleanSpark Inc. (Nasdaq: CLSK) (referred to herein as "Company" or "CleanSpark") issued a press release providing an update following Hurricane Helene. A copy of this press release is attached as Exhibit 99.1 and is being furnished with this Current Report on Form 8-K.

Item 8.01 Other Events.

On September 30, 2024, the Company issued a press release providing updates on the impact of Hurricane Helene. The Company confirmed that all employees and infrastructure are safe, with no material damage reported at its sites. CleanSpark temporarily shut down approximately 365 MW of capacity in Southeast Georgia as a precautionary measure during the hurricane. Through coordinated efforts with local utilities, approximately 200 MW has been brought back online, and full restoration is expected by October 4, 2024. The Company’s hashrate currently stands at approximately 22 EH/s, with an expected return to full operations at approximately 28 EH/s once all sites are fully re-energized.
