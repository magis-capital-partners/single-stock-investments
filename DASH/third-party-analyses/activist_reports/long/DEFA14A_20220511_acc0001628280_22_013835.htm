Following the filing of its proxy statement on Schedule 14A (the “Proxy Statement”) for the 2022 annual meeting of stockholders (the “Annual Meeting”) with the Securities and Exchange Commission on May 2, 2022, DoorDash, Inc. (“DoorDash”) discovered a typo in the date of the Annual Meeting. Accordingly, DoorDash is amending the Proxy Statement solely in order to correct the day of the week on which the meeting will occur from “Tuesday, June 23, 2022” to “Thursday, June 23, 2022.” There are no other revisions to the Proxy Statement.
