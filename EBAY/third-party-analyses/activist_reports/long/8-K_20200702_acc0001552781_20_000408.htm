Item 5.02.

Departure of Directors or Certain Officers; Election of Directors; Appointment of Certain Officers; Compensatory Arrangements of Certain Officers.

 

eBay Inc. (“eBay”) has entered into a letter agreement with Andy Cring, Interim Chief Financial Officer, modifying Mr. Cring’s existing severance benefits (“Letter Agreement”). Under the terms of the Letter Agreement, for purposes of Mr. Cring’s participation in eBay’s standard severance plan for vice presidents, if he is terminated without cause and signs and does not revoke a waiver of claims against eBay, then he will be entitled to: (1) cash severance equal to one times base salary plus target annual bonus, (2) a prorated annual bonus for the year of termination based on actual eBay performance and target individual performance, (3) the value of the equity awards that would have vested in the 12-month period after the termination date and (4) an amount equal to 8 months of COBRA premiums. In addition, the Letter Agreement provides for a $2,000,000 cash payment to Mr. Cring if he remains employed with eBay through June 1, 2021 or his employment is terminated without cause prior to that date and signs and does not revoke a waiver of claims against eBay.
