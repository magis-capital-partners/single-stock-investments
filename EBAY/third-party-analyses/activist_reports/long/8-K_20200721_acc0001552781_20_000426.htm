Item 7.01.Regulation FD Disclosure.

On July 21, 2020, eBay Inc., a Delaware corporation (“eBay”), issued a press release announcing the entry into a Transaction Agreement with Adevinta ASA (“Adevinta”), pursuant to which eBay will transfer its Classifieds business to Adevinta in exchange for $2.5 billion in cash and 540 million Adevinta shares (the “Transaction”). A copy of the press release is attached as Exhibit 99.1 to this Current Report on Form 8-K and is incorporated in this Item 7.01 by reference herein.

This information that is furnished shall not be deemed “filed” for purposes of Section 18 of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as amended (the “Exchange Act”), or otherwise subject to the liabilities of that Section. Such information shall not be deemed incorporated by reference into any filing under the Securities Act of 1933, as amended, or the Exchange Act, except as shall be expressly set forth by specific reference in such filing.
