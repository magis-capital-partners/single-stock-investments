Item 7.01. Regulation FD Disclosure.
On December 15, 2021, eBay Inc., a Delaware corporation (“eBay”) announced it has changed its definition of Gross Merchandise Volume ("GMV") to align with customer money flows on its platforms. Historical metrics were retrospectively recast to reflect the change in definition. A copy of the company's press release announcing this change is attached as Exhibit 99.1 to this report.

The information in this report, including Exhibit 99.1, is furnished and shall not be deemed “filed” for purposes of Section 18 of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as amended, or otherwise subject to the liabilities of that Section, and shall not be deemed subject to the requirements of Item 10 of Regulation S-K or incorporated by reference into any filing under the Securities Act of 1933, as amended, regardless of any general incorporation.
