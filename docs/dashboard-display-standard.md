# Dashboard display and evidence contract

The six dashboard workspaces share `display-system.css`, `display-system.js` and the route inventory in `display-registry.json`. Feature modules retain their task-specific layouts.

- DM Sans prose and controls; JetBrains Mono numbers. Title 24 px, section 18 px, body 14 px, table 13 px, secondary text at least 12 px.
- Shared dark/light tokens, text-labelled evidence states, visible keyboard focus, reduced-motion support, and comfortable/compact table density. Settings persist across workspaces and the standalone letter reader.
- Shared numeric boundaries reject blank values, booleans and nonfinite numbers; preserve observed zero, minor currency units, small prices, percentage fractions versus percentage-valued inputs, and percentage-point changes. Month-only observations retain month precision. Calendar dates do not move between time zones.
- Coverage tables paginate at 50 rows, use real company links, keep missing values last, and retain page navigation in the URL. User navigation adds browser history; canonical aliases replace their existing entry.
- Each page links to source observations, publication freshness, evidence completeness, responsible lane and next action. API success alone never establishes freshness. A private placeholder is unavailable evidence, not an empty research universe. Frozen strategy studies identify their historical data window.
