# Company assumptions and consistent valuations

Judgments run only through the owner's signed-in local Codex CLI: GPT-6.1 Sol
with High reasoning for the author and a separate ephemeral challenger session.
There is no OpenAI API-key execution, Batch execution or Cursor builder call.
The computer and desktop automation must be available for scheduled judgments.
GitHub performs deterministic compilation, shared PV/IRR checks and publication.

## Evidence before judgment

The full-universe financial refresh discovers reports from SEC submissions,
independently of whether the operating-cash-flow XBRL tag has updated. Annual
financials and dated current/interim observations are stored separately in the
same company fundamentals record. Quarterly or year-to-date observations never
silently replace annual cash-flow inputs. The underwriting packet and shared
forecast consume the same report marker and semantic source fingerprint.

Between formal reports, newly filed SEC item 2.02 earnings releases are also
collected with their financial supplements. The financial period comes from an
explicit completed-period heading in the release, never the 8-K event date.
An unresolved release blocks judgment and current PV/IRR until collection repairs
it. A new accession or revised primary facts invalidate the accepted source
fingerprint even when a filing's date predates the model. Quote and collection
clock changes leave that fingerprint unchanged.

Primary financial evidence, verified share units, a current annual baseline and
the latest discovered SEC report plus annual disclosures are checked before any
judgment call. Financial companies do not have a compulsory industrial capex/FCF
template. Funds and special instruments need their own primary claim evidence;
missing disclosures are gaps, not zero values or permission to invent inputs.
International primary coverage remains explicit where an issuer's source adapter
has not supplied verifiable financial observations.

Evidence requests expand bounded contexts from independently discovered primary
reports. Model output cannot choose arbitrary destinations or execute tools. A
repaired evidence packet gets a new hash and review binding. Requests, incomplete
attempts and unresolved critiques persist privately and retry automatically
within the existing call limits; unchanged cached outputs are reused.
Requests naming a financial note retain bounded note sections, including adjoining
tables and footnotes. A material acquisition can add SEC target evidence only
after the parent disclosure identifies the transaction and the SEC identity matches.
Target monetary observations retain issuer scope and their own reporting calendar;
target share counts never inherit the parent listing basis. Linked primary source
hashes participate in the parent's freshness checks. Unreported future quantities
are bounded judgments when the evidence supports them, not demands for perfect
facts; genuinely unbounded material claims remain blocked.

## Author, challenge and compile

Initial underwriting excludes old classifications, targets and market prices.
Every economic assumption has evidence, counterevidence, a falsifier, a review
trigger and explicit scenarios. Framework selection, financing scope, working
capital, reinvestment, dilution, terminal proceeds and all material claims must
be reconciled. The independent challenger reviews the entire contract; any major
or critical unresolved finding prevents approval.

Only a previously validated, independently approved contract can be supplied as
an update baseline. Changed evidence is identified explicitly. A new report
requires reconsidering every assumption and the framework. Price changes and
collection clocks alone do not trigger authoring. Expired reviews still require
renewal. Unverified legacy targets never become an assumption baseline.

The model prompt uses a lossless table/dictionary transport. Code round-trips it
and verifies its original hash before launch. Evidence IDs and complete source
contexts survive compression; the challenger receives primary evidence, not an
author's summary of it. Provider receipts preserve actual session IDs and usage.

The approved immutable handoff goes to GitHub. The existing compiler creates the
model directly, records its exact hash and validates it against the approved
contract. No builder inference is needed. Source updates during underwriting,
split/share inconsistencies, stale data, changed proof or failed PV/IRR replay
block replacement of the active model. Publication uses checked PRs and automatic
merge. No human economic review or capital authority is introduced.

## Economic validation (October 2026)

New production underwriting requires `underwriting-contract/2` and an independently
bound `underwriting-review/2`. Version 1 remains replayable for historical audit;
an old arithmetic receipt cannot grant current economic approval. Existing models
without the economic process are queued for fresh authoring and their research
returns are withheld during the normal shared-forecast refresh. Old handoffs cannot
skip this migration, and changed processing instructions invalidate waiting state.

The process implements lessons from Constellation (2017), RV Capital (2018),
Fundsmith (2024), Berkshire (2023), Hosking (Q2 2023), Horizon Kinetics (Q2 2023),
Howard Marks's Risk Revisited and SVB memos, Klarman's Margin of Safety, Heuer's
Psychology of Intelligence Analysis and the royalty/bank primers in the private
research vault. These teach analytical questions, never company forecast inputs.

Eight required mechanisms cover earning power, reinvestment, competitive response,
customer value, capital allocation, financing survival, inflation and realization.
Each references frozen primary evidence and actual graph nodes, or explains why it
does not apply. Every annual component bridge reconciles cash sources to investment,
outside claims, distributions, buybacks and retention, with positive shares and
adequate modeled liquidity, in all cases. A coherent financing response must replace
a cash deficit; a discount-rate change cannot repair one. Distribution cash must
reconcile to the same share denominator used in the bridge.
Maintenance, organic growth, acquisitions, compensation funding and residual
claims reconcile separately. Operating businesses, banks and royalties also have
a quantitative revenue-to-earnings-to-cash bridge; asset and event claims explain
why that bridge does not apply. The dashboard exposes the annual allocation.
Opening shares roll from the preceding closing balance. Net issuance and paid
buybacks at an explicit repurchase price must explain the closing denominator;
share reductions cannot create free per-share growth.

The holding horizon and competitive runway are separate. For a continuing equity
claim, trusted code projects the declared subsequent growth path, charges growth
investment using the declared incremental return on parent equity, and discounts
later distributions and mature continuation back to the holding date. Earnings,
investment return and capitalization must use the same financing basis. Maintenance,
interest, compensation and minority claims belong before that boundary; growth
investment is charged once. Finite-life and asset-realization claims have separate
terminal types. A compounder label grants no runway, and past market multiples are
not valuation floors. Enterprise returns cannot substitute for equity returns.

At least three competing explanations must state counterevidence and observations
that distinguish them. At least two structural alternatives must include a combined
assumption or runway test. Alternative calculations must pass their allocation and
liquidity checks. The reviewer binds the exact diagnostics hash, assesses all eight
mechanisms and the three largest sensitivity drivers, covers all alternatives and
resolves every diagnostic flag. A driver rated weak cannot be approved. Approval
records reproducibility and economic validation separately; it is not a guarantee
of forecast accuracy or permission to deploy capital.

Trusted code introduces the quote after authoring. `underwriting_diagnostics.json`
contains structural values, bounded reverse expectations and a sequential return
bridge: flat cash at unchanged valuation, forecast cash at unchanged valuation,
then the modeled exit valuation. The differences reconcile to the calculated IRR;
they are order-dependent counterfactual contributions, not an approximate additive
yield/growth formula. Optional scenario probabilities weight present values, never
an arithmetic average of IRRs. A reverse solution outside the declared bounds is
reported as unbracketed rather than manufactured by extrapolation.

At least two forward operating commitments freeze their metric, unit, period,
scenario predictions and model identity. `underwriting_outcomes.json` retains model
vintages and grades exact-period, matching-unit primary observations on later
underwriting updates. Missing or ambiguous observations remain unscored. Investment
performance is not a substitute for operating forecast accuracy. The dashboard
detail shows return contributions, alternatives, competing explanations and the
operating forecasts to review. Forecast data never increases authority by itself.

## Progress and usage

The policy contains a 16-name pilot spanning operating businesses, a cyclical
with captive finance, a loss-making balance-sheet business, a bank, holding
companies, royalties, international listings and a fund. A fair attempt order
prevents one priority name from permanently monopolizing work. Cohort targets,
actual approvals, completed models, gaps and measured usage are separate fields
in each batch result. Existing call budgets are unchanged.

The installed runtime's `last-run.json` records the actual result. The shared
forecast summary's `data_collection` reports primary/provider coverage and
collection failures separately from available valuations. A successful job is
not a claim that every company has complete evidence or approved assumptions.

Inspect saved local usage without launching a model:

```powershell
python _system/scripts/underwriting_pipeline.py usage --work PATH_TO_RUNTIME/work
```

Unknown provider usage is counted as unknown, not zero. Reasoning is included in
the provider's output count; an actual per-approved-company estimate is withheld
when receipts lack usage. Subscription allowance and credits are not inferred
from API token prices.
# Liquidity in the economic bridge

Every annual path rolls opening usable liquidity through retained cash and an
explicit adjustment for facilities, restrictions or collateral to closing usable
liquidity. Closing resources carry to the next opening and must cover the stated
minimum. Independent structural alternatives repeat this check; a constant
liquidity assumption cannot conceal an unfunded cash deficit.
