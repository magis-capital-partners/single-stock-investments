IMPORTANT NOTICE REGARDING THE AVAILABILITY OF PROXY MATERIALS FOR THE WARRIOR MET COAL, INC. ANNUAL MEETING OF STOCKHOLDERS TO BE HELD ON APRIL 20, 2026 Notice of Annual Meeting of Stockholders WHEN AND WHERE IS THE STOCKHOLDERS MEETING? The 2026 Annual Meeting of Stockholders of Warrior Met Coal, Inc. will be held on Monday, April 20, 2026, at 9:00 a.m. Central Time, in an online-only format, at www.cesonlineservices.com/hcc26_vm. You will not be able to attend the meeting in person. To attend the virtual annual meeting, please register at www.cesonlineservices.com/hcc26_vm no later than 9:00 a.m. Central Time on April 19, 2026. WHAT IS BEING VOTED ON AT THE STOCKHOLDERS MEETING? 1. the election of six director nominees named in the proxy statement; 2. to approve the adoption of the Warrior Met Coal, Inc. 2026 Equity Incentive Plan (the “2026 Equity Plan”); 3. an advisory resolution to approve the executive officer compensation; 4. ratification of the appointment of our independent registered public accounting firm for 2026; and 5. such other business as may properly come before the meeting or any adjournment or postponement thereof. WHAT DOES THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS RECOMMEND? The Warrior Met Coal, Inc. Board of Directors recommends a vote FOR the election of the six director nominees, FOR Proposal 2, FOR Proposal 3, and FOR Proposal 4. None of the proposals are conditioned on the approval of any other proposal. HOW CAN I GET A COMPLETE SET OF PROXY MATERIALS? This is not a proxy card.
