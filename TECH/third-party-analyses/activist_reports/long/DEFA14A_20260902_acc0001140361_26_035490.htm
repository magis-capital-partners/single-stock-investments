The following email was sent to the employees of Bio-Techne Corporation (“Bio-Techne”) on September 2, 2026.

Team,

Following our All-Hands Meeting last week, I wanted to share a recap of key milestones and the integration planning work underway for our planned acquisition by Merck KGaA, Darmstadt, Germany.

Where We Are: We recently received approval of the proposed transaction in Germany from the German Federal Cartel Office. Regulatory approvals in other countries are still in progress and, while we do not expect to announce every time we receive an approval, we will continue to share periodic updates as additional key milestones are reached.

What’s Next: The next major milestone is the Bio-Techne shareholder vote on September 23, when shareholders will vote on whether to approve the transaction. We will communicate the outcome shortly after the vote.

What’s Underway: Led by the Integration Planning Office (IPO), workstreams across the business are beginning planning activities and working with their counterparts at Merck KGaA, Darmstadt, Germany. For example, our Finance team has begun initial conversations focused on understanding key processes and differences in areas such as fiscal year calendars, reporting requirements and financial systems. In addition, our Change & Communications teams have been connecting regularly to plan how information will be shared internally and externally and how we can thoughtfully support employees throughout this process.

What Hasn’t Changed: As a reminder, the work underway is focused on planning and readiness as we prepare for close. It does not mean that operational integration has begun; that will only happen once the transaction closes. Until then, Bio-Techne and Merck KGaA, Darmstadt, Germany will continue to operate as independent companies.

We know many of you have questions about what happens after close. While we are committed to keeping you informed, it is important to remember that we are still early in the integration planning process and are not able to respond to every question at this stage. We will continue to share updates as planning progresses and additional milestones are reached.

We Want to Hear from You:  We’d like your feedback on what’s working well and what would be most helpful as we prepare for our exciting next chapter. We encourage you to take a few minutes to share your feedback through this short survey.

Visit the Integration Hub to view the transaction milestones slide from our All-Hands meeting for reference on where we are on the timeline, and keep an eye out for our September newsletter, where we’ll be sharing additional resources.

Thank you for your continued focus and dedication to Bio-Techne.

Laurie Gilman
Integration Lead, Bio-Techne
