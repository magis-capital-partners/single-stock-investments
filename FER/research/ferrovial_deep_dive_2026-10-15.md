## 3. Cash flow and capital allocation
- **Project dividends to the parent:** €968M in FY25 (€947M in FY24), of which Highways was €880M (20-F). 1H26: €378M (Highways €372M) against €323M in 1H25. Over 10 years the projects paid €6.5B in dividends while Ferrovial invested €5.3B of equity and returned €5.3B through buybacks and cash dividends (Factbook, Dec-2025).
- **Parent net cash:** €1,307M ex-infrastructure at 2026-06-30 (€1,341M at Dec-25). Liquidity €4.7bn (1H26 PR). Ferrovial issued a €400M non-dilutive cash-settled convertible due 2031, with conversion at €68.94 (6-K, 2025-11-28). It issued a €500M bond in Mar-26 and repaid a €780M bond in May-26.
- **Buybacks:** €972M in 2024 and €501M in 2025 (11.0M shares) (20-F). The current programme allows up to €800M or 15M shares. By 2026-09-11 it had bought 6,180,574 shares for €357.9M, and it has been **extended to 2027-03-03** (6-K, 2026-09-14). Cancellations: 23.25M shares in 2024 and 4.2M in Mar-2026.
- **Dividends:** scrip plus cash. Cash dividends were €130M in 2024 and €156M in 2025 (20-F). The interim scrip in May-2026 was €400M in total, or **€0.5578 per share** (6-K, 2026-05-15); 24.45% was taken in cash (€98M). Dutch withholding tax is 15%.

- **What the 13F shows.** The 13F covers only the US-line shares: 19.10M (2024Q2, the first quarter after the US listing), 19.33M (2024Q4), 19.47M (2025Q2), 20.74M (2025Q4) and **20.94M (2026Q2, $1.43bn, 2.7% of the 13F)** (`hohn/out/tci_13f_positions.csv`). - **Thesis (Hohn, 2025 talks [secondary transcripts]):** "Ferrovial toll roads with long concessions"; "family ~25% stake; good capital allocators (sold Heathrow… reinvest in monopoly toll roads)". TCI also sent a climate (Say on Climate) engagement letter around 2020–21 (TCI ESG page).

## 5. Risks
1. **Capital discipline on new greenfield lanes.** The I-24 bid was about **3x higher** than rival bids [secondary: JPM via Investing.com]. UBS sees a project IRR of about 7% [secondary]. I-285 follows straight after. Both need very large financing (financial close for I-24 is expected around mid-2027 and for I-285 in Oct-2027 [secondary]). The stock fell 4.95% on 2026-08-26 and 4.3% on 2026-10-15.
2. **Traffic.** Volumes fell in 1H26 on NTE (-2.0%) and I-77 (-5.2%). Value depends on price per transaction, which grew 9–19%. Political or tolling backlash is the tail risk. Revenue sharing with the DOTs also caps upside (Factbook).
3. **JFK NTO delay.** The Phase A milestone was missed and slipped to Mar-2027, and the ratings are on negative outlook or watch.
4. **Construction losses.** Ferrovial Construction's margin is only 1.5%. Its history includes legacy losses. There is litigation over the Turów settlement and Centella, Chile (1H26 6-K).
5. **Redomicile and tax.** The 20-F flags a risk that Spain or another country claims tax residence, which could trigger a **Dutch exit tax** and limits on using tax losses after the merger. There is also an open Spanish tax dispute for 2002–05 (€44M, no provision).
6. **Index.** Ferrovial joined the **Nasdaq-100 in Dec-2025** (20-F). The Amsterdam delisting removes AEX-type flows. Ferrovial is not in the S&P 500; as a Dutch-domiciled foreign private issuer it is likely ineligible [Magis view]. A Nasdaq-100 deletion if the market cap falls would bring forced selling.
7. **Leverage.** Infrastructure project debt is €7.8bn (Jun-26) and is non-recourse. FX: the CAD and USD weakened in 2025 (20-F).

## 6. Catalysts (next 12 months)
- **Q3-26 results**; the date was announced in a 6-K on 2026-10-15. Watch the 407 CAD 550M distribution and managed-lane pricing.
- **Q1-27:** NTO Phase A DBO in Mar-2027 and the start of airline moves.
- **I-24 financing (2027).** Disclosure of equity commitments and IRR could reverse or confirm the derating. The I-285 commercial close is expected around Dec-2026 [secondary].
- **Buyback:** about €442M remaining to 2027-03-03, roughly 1.4% of the market cap [derived].
- **Possible asset rotation:** a stake sale in a mature US lane would put a market mark on the lanes, which is the key unknown in this SOTP.

## 8. Verdict
Ferrovial owns some of the best toll assets in the world. The 407 ETR alone (€11.9bn on the 2025 transaction mark) is worth about 38% of today's €31.1bn market cap, and the US Managed Lanes are raising price per transaction at double digits. The stock has fallen to €43.22 (from €62.65 in Feb-2026 [secondary]) on doubts about bid discipline at I-24 and I-285. My base SOTP is €36.7 per share (€26.4bn). It deliberately values the lanes at 22x EBITDA, below the ~29x implied for 407, and puts zero on the new wins. On that basis the stock still trades at a **~18% premium**. Only at 407-like multiples for the lanes (about €42 per share) or in the bull case (€44.4) is today's price covered. **For Magis this is Watch, not Buy:** a high-quality business that TCI is still adding to, but with no margin of safety until either the I-24 economics are disclosed and credible or the price nears about €37 per share (base SOTP). Re-underwrite at Q3-26 results and after I-24 financial close.
