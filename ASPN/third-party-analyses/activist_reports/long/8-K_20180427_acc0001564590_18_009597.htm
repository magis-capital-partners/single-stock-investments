Item 1.01 Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement.

On April 25, 2018, Aspen Aerogels, Inc. (the "Company") amended the Amended and Restated Loan and Security Agreement with Silicon Valley Bank, which was originally effective from August 31, 2014 to August 31, 2016 and subsequently extended through April 28, 2018 (the "Credit Facility"). Pursuant to the present seventh amendment (the "Amendment"), the Credit Facility’s maturity date is extended to April 28, 2019 along with agreement on certain financial covenants applicable to the extended term. The summary of the Amendment is qualified in its entirety by reference to the full text of the Amendment, a copy of which will be attached as an exhibit to the Company’s Quarterly Report on Form 10-Q for the period ending June 30, 2018.
