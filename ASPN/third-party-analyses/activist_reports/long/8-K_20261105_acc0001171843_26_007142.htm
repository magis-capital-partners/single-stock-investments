Item 5.02. Departure of Directors or Certain Officers; Election of Directors; Appointment of Certain Officers; Compensatory Arrangements of Certain Officers.

 

On November 2, 2026, the Board of Directors (the “Board”) of the Company appointed Stacy D. Nieuwoudt to join the Board as a Class III director, effective November 4, 2026, to serve until the Company’s 2029 Annual Meeting of Stockholders. The Board also appointed Ms. Nieuwoudt to the Audit Committee and the Compensation & Leadership Development Committee of the Board. In connection with Ms. Nieuwoudt’s appointment to the Board, the Board also increased the size of the Board from six to seven members.

 

Ms. Nieuwoudt, age 46, has been a private investor since 2019. Previously, she served as a Senior Energy and Industrials Analyst at Aptigon Capital, a Citadel Company, from 2017 to 2019, and as an Energy Equities Analyst at Surveyor Capital, also a Citadel Company, from September 2010 to November 2017. Ms. Nieuwoudt currently serves on the board of directors of ProFrac Holding Corp. (Nasdaq: ACDC), a vertically integrated energy services and manufacturing company. At ProFrac Holding Corp., Ms. Nieuwoudt also Chairs the Compensation Committee and serves on the Audit Committee. Her previous board experience includes serving as a director of enCore Energy Corp. (Nasdaq: EU) from October 2024 to September 2025, where she served on the Audit and Compensation Committees, and as a director of Independence Contract Drilling, Inc. from January 2021 to August 2024, where she chaired both the Audit and Nominating & Governance Committees and served on the Compensation Committee. Ms. Nieuwoudt holds a B.A. from Rice University. The Company believes Ms. Nieuwoudt is qualified to serve on the Board due to her extensive energy industry expertise, deep knowledge of capital markets, and decades of experience analyzing energy and industrial sectors.

 

The Board has determined that Ms. Nieuwoudt is an independent director for purposes of serving on the Board and its committees. There are no arrangements or understandings between Ms. Nieuwoudt and any other person pursuant to which Ms. Nieuwoudt was appointed as a director. There are no transactions in which the Company is a participant and in which Ms. Nieuwoudt has a material interest that are required to be disclosed under Item 404(a) of Regulation S-K.

 

Ms. Nieuwoudt will be entitled to the compensation paid by the Company to its non-employee directors under the Company’s Non-Employee Director Compensation Policy (the “Policy”), including an annual cash retainer of $45,000 for service on the Board, an annual cash retainer of $7,500 for service on the Audit Committee and an annual cash retainer of $5,000 for service on the Compensation & Leadership Development Committee (pro-rated for the portion of the year in which she serves as a member of the Board, Audit Committee and Compensation & Leadership Development Committee). In accordance with the Policy and pursuant to the Aspen Aerogels Amended and Restated 2023 Equity Incentive Plan (the “Plan”), Ms. Nieuwoudt was also awarded a pro-rated equity grant of restricted stock and stock options on the date of her appointment to the Board, vesting on the earlier of the day prior to the Company’s 2027 annual meeting of stockholders, or the occurrence of a Change in Control (as defined in the Plan). The Policy is filed as Exhibit 10.1 to the Company’s Quarterly Report on Form 10-Q, filed by the Company on October 8, 2024, and is incorporated herein by reference.

 

Ms. Nieuwoudt has also entered into an indemnification agreement in the form the Company has entered into with its other non-employee directors, which form is filed as Exhibit 10.18 to the Company’s Amendment No. 1 to its Registration Statement on Form S-1, filed by the Company on May 14, 2014, and is incorporated herein by reference.

 

On November 5, 2026, the Company issued a press release announcing the appointment of Ms. Nieuwoudt to the Board, which is attached hereto as Exhibit 99.2 and incorporated herein by reference.
