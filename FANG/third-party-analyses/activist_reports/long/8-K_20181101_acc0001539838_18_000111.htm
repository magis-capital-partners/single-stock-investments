Item 1.01. Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement.
On October 26, 2018, Diamondback Energy, Inc. (“Diamondback”), as parent guarantor, Diamondback O&G LLC, as borrower (the “Borrower”), and certain other subsidiaries of Diamondback, as guarantors, entered into an eighth amendment (the “Eighth Amendment”) to the Second Amended and Restated Credit Agreement, dated as of November 1, 2013, with Wells Fargo Bank, National Association, as administrative agent, and the lenders party thereto.
 In the Eighth Amendment, the Borrower elected a commitment amount of $2.0 billion, and the borrowing base was increased to $2.5 billion, which will increase to $2.65 billion upon Diamondback’s closing of the pending acquisition of assets from Ajax Resources, LLC, which occurred on October 31, 2018, and satisfaction of certain other conditions set forth in the Eighth Amendment. The Eighth Amendment also increased Diamondback’s flexibility to make restricted payments and redeem senior unsecured notes.
The preceding summary of the Eighth Amendment is qualified in its entirety by reference to the full text of such amendment, a copy of which is attached as Exhibit 10.1 to this Current Report on Form 8-K and incorporated herein by reference.
