Item 2.01 Completion of Acquisition or Disposition of Assets.

On November 3, 2026, IonQ, Inc. (the “Company”) completed its previously announced acquisition (the “Acquisition”) of all of the issued and outstanding shares of common stock of Elisity, Inc., a Delaware corporation (“Elisity”), pursuant to the terms of the Agreement and Plan of Merger, dated as of October 9, 2026, by and among the Company, Elisity, Espresso Acquisition Sub, Inc., a Delaware corporation and a wholly owned subsidiary of the Company, and Fortis Advisors LLC, solely in its capacity as the representative of the securityholders (the “Holder Representative”). The consideration (the “Closing Consideration”) delivered in connection with the closing of the Acquisition consists of 32,233,274 shares of the Company’s common stock, par value $0.0001 per share (each, a “Share”). In addition to the Closing Consideration, Elisity’s securityholders will be eligible to receive up to 4,265,934 Shares in earn-out consideration, depending on the achievement of financial metrics through January 2028, which, together with the Closing Consideration, constitutes the merger consideration of up to 36,499,208 Shares (the “Merger Consideration”).

In connection with the closing of the Acquisition and the issuance of the Merger Consideration, the Company and the Holder Representative entered into a Registration Rights Agreement, dated as of November 3, 2026 (the “Registration Rights Agreement”), pursuant to which recipients of the Closing Consideration have certain registration rights with respect thereto.

The foregoing description of the Registration Rights Agreement does not purport to be complete and is qualified in its entirety by reference to the full text of the Registration Rights Agreement, a copy of which is filed herewith as Exhibit 10.1 to this Current Report on Form 8-K and is incorporated by reference herein.
