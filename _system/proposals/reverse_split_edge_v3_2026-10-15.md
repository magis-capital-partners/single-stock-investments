# RSI-3.0: announcement evidence and position economics

User-authorized research version, 2026-10-15. Implementation: `reverse_splits/net_edge_v3.py`, `net_edge_evidence.py`, `net_edge_study.py`; dashboard: Shorts backtest → RSI-3.0. RSI-1.0 rules, production fundamental thresholds, decisions, orders and capital authority are unchanged. `research_only=true`, `authority=none`.

## What changes

The earlier edge study had 331 numerical ratings among 691 priced events, no complete framework reviews, no reconciled financing packages and no approved historical locates. Tiny orders faced a $2 commission minimum; the original sample also included 417 SSR-restricted entries, rather than 417 halts. This version repairs the data contracts and the research engine without assuming those gaps have been filled.

1. **Filing reconstruction.** US GAAP and IFRS aliases, one explicit reporting currency, annual statements, matched fiscal YTD bridges and four contiguous reported quarters. Preserve fiscal starts, accession, tag, unit, filing date and source values. Same-day date-only filings are excluded. Latest relevant period must be within 200 days; three measured signals remain necessary. Missing debt is never a zero-debt observation. The final extraction produces 375 ratings in the original 691 events and 847 among 4,304 dated records with a usable split ratio. Some former ratings are lost under stricter fiscal reconciliation. Current SEC responses omitting CIK are bound through an independently fetched submissions identity and entity-name match; raw response bytes remain unchanged.
2. **Framework evidence.** All 27 routes retain supported, contradicted, unresolved or N/A states. N/A never supports a thesis. A contradiction vetoes a selected mechanism; an unrelated contradiction does not veto every short. Mandatory cited identity, instrument, borrow, funding or falsification vetoes still exclude a name. Complete reviewed conviction is separately supplied by a dated, reasoned analyst review; numerical deterioration is not a probability or full conviction.
3. **Financing.** Reconcile common shares and mutually exclusive ATM/warrant/convert scenarios onto the same split basis. Include net proceeds and value available to common. Ownership dilution and per-share value change are separate. A reverse split alone has zero mechanical dilution. Financing is a matching feature when both target and donor have evidence; it is never added to the historical return a second time.
4. **Forecast.** At announcement, select at most 60 earlier completed other issuers, at least 20, for the identical entry/exit rule. Match deterioration, split ratio, observed numerical/framework features and reconciled financing when available. Keep one completed observation per issuer; exclude the target issuer. Shrink the empirical mean toward zero with 20 pseudo-issuers. Seed the issuer bootstrap deterministically; use a one-sided 90% lower bound on gross mean. Sample uncertainty does not measure rare squeeze probability.
5. **Separate clocks.** Freeze gross payoff, expected holding scenarios and the announcement planning calculation using only prior source availability. Recheck price, costs, inventory and approved locate at entry. Imported historical reviews must be outcome-blinded and are labelled reconstructions; prospective review creation must precede the announcement cutoff. Imported actual loan charges never become an entry-rate indication.
6. **Carry.** Project every prior exit-duration scenario onto the new trade's actual exchange/settlement calendar. Use T+2 before 2024-05-28, T+1 thereafter, and charge the settled calendar interval through return settlement, including weekends. Daily USD collateral is nominal shares × ceil(102% × prior closing nominal price), charged at daily APR / 360. Known splits change nominal quantity and price inversely; rounding stays on the nominal security. Minimum billed days and pre-borrow per-share fees are explicit. Same-day ordinary settled carry can be zero. Actual accrual records, fee-per-share records and dated recalls are distinct inputs.
7. **Execution.** Daily OHLC establishes conditional benchmarks only. Stops win ambiguous same-bar brackets; adverse opening gaps are not capped at the stop. SSR replay requires verified national BBO plus eligible aggressive buy prints, consumes an explicit queue ahead, cancels if the bid reaches the limit, and times out after 15 minutes. Sampled quote paths alone never prove fills. The complete-evidence case accepts policy-bound, compliance-verified observed execution reports with order references; future cover evidence cannot gate an earlier entry. Missing cover evidence leaves an admitted liability unresolved.
8. **Joint sizing.** Search whole-share quantities, evaluating depth proxies and actual commission minima/caps at each size. Use nominal cover quantities after known splits for expected and realized fees. Both expected net edge and the lower confidence bound after costs must be positive. Quarter fractional sizing, deterioration/reviewed conviction, prior ADV, inventory, concurrent names, maintenance margin and name/sleeve squeeze budgets constrain size. Costs and stressed carry also consume squeeze budget. Net distribution, skew, expected gain/loss and calendar carry accompany each selected or rejected trial.
9. **Portfolio accounting.** Short proceeds do not create NAV. Existing liabilities consume capital before new entries. Daily margin uses nominal split-adjusted quantity, including maintenance floors. Return-settlement charges can persist after cover. Missing future price paths retain admitted positions and suppress performance; future data availability is never an entry filter. Joint daily highs are a conservative funding diagnostic; a breach suppresses performance. Imported dividends and fractional settlement adjustments reconcile as incremental cash costs, with their basis explicit. Coverage of these histories is still incomplete.
10. **Validation.** Compare announcement-next-open and post-split-next-open, 1/5/10/20 sessions, brackets versus time, and $50k/$250k/$1m account scenarios. The account profiles and fees are research assumptions, not actual account statements. Previously explored history remains diagnostic. Prospective forecasts and explicit abstentions are immutable, hash-chained records archived before scheduled entry; outcomes append separately. The scheduled public disclosure/borrow archive begins collecting this evidence. No missing historical rate or approval is backfilled from today's public quote.

## Inputs still needed

The changes make the filter operable with dated evidence, but do not manufacture the evidence. Historical approved locates/order reports require the owner’s broker records or an authorized licensed archive. Full framework, identity, valuation, catalyst and financing reviews require source-specific annotations; unresolved reviews remain excluded. Additional raw filings, identity histories, cancelled announcements, nominal paths, national quotes/trades, dividends and fractional settlements remain explicit collection gaps. The public companyfacts fetcher is bounded and stops on 403/429; an identity-mismatched response is rejected.

The broader source catalogue retains 7,069 events, including unpriced and unverified records. Discovery accessions are a separate denominator. No complete announcement universe, observed executable performance or profitable edge is asserted.
