# 2026-10-30 Activism desk lessons [PROPOSED]

## L1 [PROPOSED] Short reports need their own scan; the 13D feed does not carry them
- Evidence: activist_feed.json (build 2026-10-27T13:55:22Z) has 0 short-side rows dated after 2026-10-10. It missed Muddy Waters EIX (2026-10-13), Culper CBRS (2026-10-28) and J Capital IRM (2026-10-28). The Oct 29 run did not catch CBRS either; found today via shortreport.fyi and culperresearch.com.
- Seen: 2026-10-30 (CBRS caught one morning late; same failure as the EIX test case).
- Harmful: a held name (CBRS) got a public short a day before the desk saw it.
- Scope: Activism desk, every morning run, whatever the feed's freshness.
- Fix: each run, read the latest-research page of the publishers in activist_records plus one aggregator (shortreport.fyi), then match tickers to registry.json.
- Review after: 2026-11-13.

## L2 [PROPOSED] Check aggregator dates against the publisher's own date
- Evidence: shortreport.fyi lists "NVIDIA (NVDA): Short Report – Culper Research, Oct 27, 2026". Culper's own site dates the NVDA report 2026-05-13 (Culper_NVDA_5-13-2026.pdf).
- Seen: 2026-10-30.
- Harmful: a five-month-old report would have gone out as new.
- Scope: any short report found through an aggregator or news re-post.
- Fix: use the publisher's PDF or post date as as_of_date. If they disagree, use the publisher's date and drop the item as stale.
- Review after: 2026-11-30.

## L3 [PROPOSED] Read the forward-contract maturity dates from the filing; do not carry dates over
- Evidence: catalysts.json had the CIFR Bitfury tranches on 2026-10-30 and 2026-12-07. CIFR Form 4 0001123292-26-001436 (filed 2026-10-27) gives maturities 2026-09-25, 2026-10-23 and 2026-11-30, with delivery within one business day. Tranche 2 settled 2026-10-26.
- Seen: 2026-10-30.
- Harmful: a "due today" catalyst had already happened four days earlier, and the final tranche was a week late on our calendar.
- Scope: any catalyst taken from a 13D/A or Form 4 derivative footnote.
- Fix: put the exact footnote dates in the catalyst, cite the accession, and re-check the issuer's Form 4s within two business days of each maturity.
- Review after: 2026-12-02.
