The Math Behind the T1-Trina US Tax Credit China Hustle:

We learned that Trina Solar gets paid out T1 Energy’s US 45X Tax Credits when Trina Solar is specifically the customer. In that situation, T1 appears to pay out 90% of the total US Tax Credits directly to Trina, but they relabel it as a “Revenue Discount” to pretend US Taxpayer money isn’t directly sent to China.

Anyways, the math from T1’s internal “45X JEQ4 2025” spreadsheet is simple:

  • T1 sold 970,286,040 watts worth of modules to Trina Solar US Inc in FY 2025
  • 970,286,040 watts * $0.0624/watt = $60.5 million revenue discount

This shows that T1 only records this “revenue discount” on modules sold to Trina Solar US Inc. Sales to T1’s few other customers and sales where Trina acted as the sales agent did NOT receive this discount.

For simplicity, the 2 bullets below summarize what is happening here:

  • T1 generated $67.9 million worth of US 45X tax credits from modules sold to Trina Solar US Inc (970,286,040 watts * $0.07/watt)
  • T1 paid $60.5 million of those tax credits back to Trina Solar (970,286,040 watts * $0.0624/watt)

So, Trina received ~90% of the US tax credits T1 generated from the modules Trina purchased off of T1 Energy.

How to Execute a China Hustle of US Tax Credits – A Short Story by T1 Energy & Trina Solar:

Documents Show T1 Energy’s Payment of 45X US Tax Credits to Trina Solar Occur:

  1. T1 Energy produces solar modules at G1
  2. T1 earned (in FY 2025) a $0.07/watt in Section 45X Tax Credits from the US government
  3. Trina Solar buys modules from T1 Energy
  4. T1 accrues a “trade discount” of $0.0624/watt payable back to Trina
    1. The $0.0624/watt rate is 89% of the value of the full $0.07/watt tax credit
    2. Trina captures ~90% of the US 45X Tax credit value on modules purchased from T1
  5. The discount is booked as a contra-revenue adjustment, reducing T1’s reported net sales
  6. The discount flows directly back to Trina as a “45X tax credit trade discount due to Trina US”
