Prices are Yahoo Finance daily bars. The machine-readable copy is `Fugazi_Research.csv`. How to add the next publisher, or the next print, is in `README.md`.

## How a row is measured

Pre-report close is the last regular-session close before the publication date, in unadjusted dollars, the print on the tape.

Day 1 % is that session's close versus the pre-report close when the publication date is a trading session, and the next session when it is not. The percent uses the split-adjusted and dividend-adjusted close.

Low after report is the lowest regular-session trade from Day 1 through the latest close, same adjustment, and the date of that low, versus the pre-report close.

Total to date is the latest close versus the pre-report close. In this file the latest close is 6 October 2026.

## Caveats

- Advent and Davis. Both left Nasdaq and the later tape is OTC. Advent's low and total sit on sub-penny Expert Market prints from July 2026 (a 27 July 2026 print of $0.0003 after a prior close near $0.35). Those two totals are bad prints, not a clean exchange close. Davis was suspended on 25 March 2026. Its total uses the OTC series under DTCKF.
- Avis Budget. The pre-report session, 22 April 2026, traded as high as $847.70 and closed at $443.94. The prior close was $713.97. Day 1 of −48.4% is measured from that squeeze close, not from a quiet price.
- T3 Defense (DFNS). Day 1 was +122.2% (3 August 2026 close of $61.50 versus the 31 July close of $27.68). The later low was −77.6% on 1 October 2026. The up day is a path fact.
- 2025 release times are not on the archive page. Day 1 for those rows is the publication-date close. If a note went out after the close, the first tradable session was the next day.
- The list is micro-cap heavy, and many names had already reverse-split before the report. The medians describe this publisher's targets. They are a poor base rate for a large-cap short.
- Sound Group paid cash distributions of $1.00 on 1 October 2025 and $1.20 on 22 April 2026. Its total uses the dividend-adjusted close and is less negative than the raw price change.

## Official outcomes

- Advent Technologies. Nasdaq suspended the shares on 30 October 2025. Removal from listing was effective 2 February 2026, after a delisting determination dated 13 January 2026 (failure of the Nasdaq Capital Market equity tests, Listing Rules 5550(b)(1), (b)(2), and (b)(3)). On 20 July 2026 OTC Markets moved the common stock to the Expert Market, unsolicited quotes only. The quote symbol is ADNH. Company 8-K filed after that notice.
- Davis Commodities. Nasdaq suspended trading on 25 March 2026. On 21 September 2026 the company said Nasdaq would file a Form 25 and delist the Class A shares. Quotation continued on OTC Markets as DTCKF.
- Akanda. On 13 August 2026 Nasdaq granted an extension through 13 December 2026 to regain compliance with Listing Rule 5550(b)(1), the $2.5 million stockholders' equity test. The deficiency letter was 16 June 2026. Source is the company Form 6-K describing the extension notice. Missing the date can lead to delisting. It is a deadline, not a completed delisting.
- No SEC, DOJ, or exchange fraud finding tied to these reports turned up in the listing record checked for this seed. A plaintiff-firm investigation notice on Astrotech, dated 2 July 2026, is not a finding.
