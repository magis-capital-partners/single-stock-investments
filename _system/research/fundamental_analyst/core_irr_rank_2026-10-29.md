## Method
- **Model:** the Magis 7-year owner-cash IRR, same convention as repo `valuation.json` and `exports/GOOGL_valuation_Q3_2026_calc.py`. CF0 = −price. CF1–6 = owner cash per share. CF7 = owner cash × (1 + exit multiple). Growth uses g1 for years 1–5 and g2 for years 6–7. Owner cash stands in for dividends plus buybacks, so dividends are included. The 15% price is the same cash flows discounted at 15%.
- **Owner cash [F inputs, A choices]:**
  - **Default:** TTM OCF − capex − SBC on diluted shares. Source: Yahoo statements, cross-checked against SEC XBRL for 21 US filers. They tie for MA, MCO, MSCI, CME, ICE, CPRT, TPL, MIAX, COST, GOOGL, CBOE, WCN, HEI, AXP and RGLD.
  - **Serial acquirers:** company FCFA2S (CSU, TOI, LMN).
  - **Royalties/streams:** OCF − SBC. Stream purchases count as growth.
  - **LSEG:** company equity FCF less SBC.
  - **AI capex names:** capex normalized as a % of revenue. GOOGL 20% (unchanged from the Oct 28 export). AMZN 12%. META 22%.
  - **COST:** net income (growth-warehouse capex added back).
  - **ASML:** about net income, because prepayments inflate FCF.
  - **AXP:** banks method. EPS × (1 − g/ROE) = $17.75 × (1 − 9%/33%).
  - **BN:** LTM distributable earnings, cut 10% to strip out realizations.
  - **BRK.B, MKL, FRMO:** book value per share grown 8%/9%/8% a year, exiting at 1.45x/1.3x/0.9x P/B.
- **Exit multiples [A]:** set conservatively, at or below today's multiple and repo exits. They are below my estimate of each name's 10-yr median (for example MA/V ~26–28x vs ~35x+ historically [I]). **Gap:** I did not machine-compute 10-yr median multiples for every name. Only the royalties have 5-yr medians (from `royalties/watchlist.json`). Where the exit is *above* today's multiple (B3, CPRT, LSEG, ICE, TFPM, RMS, MKL, FRMO), the bracketed figure shows the IRR with no re-rating.
