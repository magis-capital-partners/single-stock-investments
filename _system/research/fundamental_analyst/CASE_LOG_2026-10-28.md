# Lens case log
Purpose: record each lens call when it is made, then score it at the review date, so the rules in PRINCIPLES.md are tested against outcomes. **Never edit a past row's call or reason.** Add the outcome only, and add a new row if the view changes.
Conventions: date = call date (ET). Price = the price the call was made at, in listing currency. IRR figures are Drew's repo model base case (the HK/Stahl lens never feeds base IRR). Review date defaults to 6 months after the call. Outcome is left blank until review: fill in price at review, thesis status (intact, broken or unresolved), and which PRINCIPLES item the outcome supports or refutes.

Calibration (added 2026-10-29; PRINCIPLES.md §G): every new call carries a **Probability** that it is right on its own terms at the review date. At review, **Brier** = (probability − outcome)², outcome 1 if right, 0 if wrong. Existing rows get a probability only where the original write-up stated a confidence; none did, so they read "not stated" and are not Brier-scored.

Use as the falsifier test at review: is FRMI's underlying value stable/growing or eroding?
- 2026-10-28, B3 — Klarman 2021 draft: "differentiate between what is secular and what is cyclical". At review, record whether B3's volume weakness proved cyclical (supports waiting for price) or secular share loss (supports pass).

At review, check whether B3 has (a) realised fee increases and (b) a fee that is small relative to the value it delivers to users. Both are needed before the Hohn "pricing power unproven" objection lapses.
