## Unified Data Architecture & Multi-Agent Blueprint (2026-10-16)

- Conducted empirical audit of `magis-capital-partners/single-stock-investments` (303,687 files, 51.71 GB working tree, 15.22 GB `.git` packfile), `Single Stock Research PDF Store` (Google Drive Shared Drive `0AFpaOm4iTLqjUk9PVA`), and `magis-capital-partners/research-vault`.
- Identified four structural bottlenecks: (1) 47.79 GB of binary `.pdf` (14.12 GB) and raw `.htm` (33.67 GB) files tracked in Git; (2) 152,467 dated output files (`*_YYYY-MM-DD.md`) with 70.4%–85.8% semantic duplication across runs; (3) monolithic JSON feeds (`portfolio_view.json` at 4.02 MB, `dashboard_meta.json` at 3.85 MB, `research_index.json` at 4.66 MB) inflating agent context consumption; (4) `build_document_registry.py` scanning only `ROOT.rglob("*.pdf")` and skipping `_external`, leaving `research-vault` corpora invisible to the unified document registry.
