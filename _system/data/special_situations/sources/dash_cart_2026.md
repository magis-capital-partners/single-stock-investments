# DASH agreement to acquire Maplebear

Read from EDGAR on 2026-10-29. DoorDash CIK 0001792789. Our words.

Rule 425 filing, accession 0001792789-26-000065, filed October 29, 2026. The combined document includes the October 29, 2026 earnings release. That release says DoorDash announced a definitive agreement to acquire Maplebear Inc. ("Instacart") for $65.00 per share, payable in at least 75 percent cash and up to 25 percent DoorDash common stock, subject to election and proration. This filing does not state a closing date, a shareholder vote, or a CVR. It is not cited here as a no-vote deal.
