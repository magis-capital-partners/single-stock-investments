# KBR Trinzic spin-off

Read from EDGAR on 2026-10-29. KBR CIK 0001357615. Our words.

Form 8-K, accession 0001357615-26-000194, filed October 29, 2026. Item 7.01 says Trinzic, Inc., which KBR expects to spin off as an independent public company in January 2027, plans fixed-income investor education meetings. Exhibit 99.1, the third-quarter earnings release, says the Mission Technology Solutions separation, which will become Trinzic, is targeted to be completed on January 4, 2027, subject to final approval by KBR's board and other customary conditions, including effectiveness of the Form 10 filed with the SEC on September 30, 2026. This note does not take a ratio or a share count from that Form 10.
