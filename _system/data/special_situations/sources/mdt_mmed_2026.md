Rule 425, accession 0001628280-26-065819, October 14, 2026. The exchange offer and withdrawal rights expired at 12:00 midnight, New York City time, at the end of October 9, 2026. The offer was oversubscribed. Medtronic elected to exchange all of its remaining MiniMed interest, 252,813,348 MiniMed shares, for 55,032,401 Medtronic ordinary shares, and said it will not retain any MiniMed shares. Holders of fewer than 100 Medtronic shares who tendered all of them are not prorated. The final count: 297,521,390 Medtronic shares validly tendered and not withdrawn; 296,763,500 subject to proration; 757,890 odd-lot shares not prorated; 55,032,401 shares accepted. The final proration factor is 18.289133 percent.
