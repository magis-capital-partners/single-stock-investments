# FDX retained FedEx Freight stake

Read from EDGAR on 2026-10-29. FedEx CIK 0001048911. Our words.

Form 10-Q, accession 0001048911-26-000131, filed October 28, 2026, for the quarter ended September 30, 2026. The filing says FedEx retained a 19.9 percent ownership interest in FedEx Freight common stock. It records that equity security at a carrying value of $3.3 billion at September 30, 2026, inside short-term investment, after a $1.2 billion unrealized loss. It says FedEx expects to fully monetize the remaining FedEx Freight stake within 12 months of the balance sheet date, subject to market conditions. The sentence is an expectation. The 10-Q does not say FedEx must sell.
