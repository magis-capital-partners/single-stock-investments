# SKYA issuer tender

Read from EDGAR on 2026-10-29. Issuer CIK 0001737995. Our words. Not a price target.

The October 16, 2026 Schedule TO-I, accession 0000930413-26-003076, and the offer to purchase filed with it (dated October 15, 2026) offer $3.00 cash per share, less $0.0001 for pre-funded warrants and strategic warrants, for up to 48,311,489 shares. The offer says that count is about 95 percent of the shares issued and outstanding on October 15, 2026. The original cover said the offer would expire one minute after 11:59 p.m. New York City time on October 29, 2026, unless extended. The same offer states an aggregate purchase price of up to $201,260,220 and an asset value of about $223.8 million in cash. It also says a non-U.S. holder is subject to 30 percent withholding on gross proceeds unless a lower rate is established.

The offer's summary of the August 14, 2026 Schedule 13G/A says the Bastion reporting persons' position includes 1,345,417 common shares, pre-funded warrants for up to 4,234,615 shares, and stapled warrants for up to 5,384,615 shares held by Bastion Trading, plus 2,940,075 common shares held by Lucio Holding. Those warrants have a blocker that stops exercise above 9.99 percent of the common stock, and the offer says Bastion Trading cannot exercise warrants that would put Mr. Zhu above 4,294,891 shares. That 9.99 percent figure is a warrant blocker in the offer's summary of the 13G/A. It is not a statement that Bastion owns 9.9 percent.

Amendment No. 2, accession 0001493152-26-048786, filed October 28, 2026. Exhibit (a)(1)(J) is the company's October 27, 2026 release. It says the company will extend expiration to one minute after 11:59 p.m. New York City time on November 12, 2026, unless further extended or ended sooner. It says pre-funded warrants of each class rise from 95 percent to 100 percent, still at $3.00 less the $0.0001 exercise price. It adds cash and cryptocurrency stapled warrants, exercise price $9.75, purchase price $0.17, up to 100 percent of each class. Common stock and strategic warrants stay at up to 95 percent and $3.00. Those classes, and the pre-funded classes, keep a 50 percent minimum inside the class. If every security sought is tendered, the company says it will pay about $214.3 million. It says it holds about $223 million in cash. Bastion agreed to tender all of its common shares, pre-funded warrants, and stapled warrants, and to a three-year standstill.

Amendment No. 3, accession 0001493152-26-049114, filed October 29, 2026. It adds the October 27, 2026 tender support and settlement agreement with Bastion Trading Limited and certain affiliates, and it incorporates that agreement by reference to Exhibit 10.1 of the Form 8-K filed October 28, 2026. The amendment says Bastion will tender the securities it beneficially owns that the offer accepts, and that the standstill runs three years from signing.
