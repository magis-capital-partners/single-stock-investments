# ARI delisting and liquidating trust

Read from EDGAR on 2026-10-29. Apollo Commercial Real Estate Finance CIK 0001467760. Our words.

Form 8-K, accession 0001193125-26-424253, filed October 16, 2026, Item 3.01, and Exhibit 99.1. The company says it expects to file a Form 25 on or about October 27, 2026, and that it expects the last day of trading on the NYSE will be November 6, 2026. The release says that after delisting the company will transfer remaining assets and liabilities to a Maryland liquidating trust, expects to deregister on November 9, 2026, and will dissolve. Beneficial interests are to be distributed one-for-one. Subject to limited exceptions, those interests will not be transferable and will not trade on the NYSE or any other exchange.

Form 25, accession 0001193125-26-434293, filed October 27, 2026, is the notification of removal from NYSE listing for the common stock, under 17 CFR 240.12d2-2(c). The Form 25 does not restate November 6.
