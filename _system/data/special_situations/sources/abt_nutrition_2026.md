# ABT Nutrition separation

Read from EDGAR on 2026-10-29. Abbott CIK 0000001800. Our words.

Form 8-K, accession 0001104659-26-117587, filed October 21, 2026, Item 8.01. Exhibit 99.1 is the October 21, 2026 release. Abbott says it intends to separate its global Nutrition business into an independent public company by a tax-free distribution to Abbott shareholders, and that it expects to complete the separation by the end of 2027. The release says the Nutrition company would have about $8.5 billion in annual sales. The 8-K does not state a distribution ratio, a record date, a when-issued window, a new ticker, or a holder who must sell.
