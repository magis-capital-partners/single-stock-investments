## Approved beliefs — company-specific

Company facts age fastest, so every row carries a status and the date it was last checked
against its cited source. Add ` · agent` to the status when an agent, not the human, made
that check — the same marker the belief bullets carry, and for the same reason: a status
date with no marker reads as a human affirmation.

Most sources here are filings. A row sourced to a **deep dive** is a judgement, not a fact
read off a filing, so re-reading a filing cannot re-affirm it; such a row keeps the date the
judgement was made and says what it is. A superseded row keeps its original figures — the
point is to see what moved.
