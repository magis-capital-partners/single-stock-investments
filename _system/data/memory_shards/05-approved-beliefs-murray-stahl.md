## Approved beliefs — Murray Stahl

### Croupier business model

- **Croupiers** facilitate pecuniary transactions (exchanges, custodians, processors) without putting principal capital at risk — distinct from balance-sheet financials like banks. — `_system/reference/investment-wisdom/stahl/Stahl-Croupier-Business-Model-2008.pdf` `[active 2026-05-21]`
- Modern regulation pushes OTC/exotic instruments toward organized exchanges and clearing — expanding croupier TAM over time. — `_system/reference/investment-wisdom/stahl/Stahl-Croupier-Business-Model-2008.pdf` `[active 2026-05-21]`
- Liquidity demand can **rise** in crises (ETF turnover, futures hedging) even when credit markets freeze — do not assume croupier volumes collapse with recession. — `_system/reference/investment-wisdom/stahl/Stahl-Croupier-Business-Model-2008.pdf` `[active 2026-05-21]`
- Toll-like contract economics do **not** make a croupier. On-site industrial gas plants, fab equipment, HDD, pipelines and regulated wires all put principal capital and asset risk to work — they are infrastructure or compounder archetypes. The croupier test is the *absence of principal at risk*, not the shape of the revenue. — `_system/memory/daily/2026-07-10.md` `[active 2026-08-09 · agent]`

- A ticker onboarded from an 8-K alone, before its 424B4/S-1 is in evidence, must default to archetype `pending`/`unknown`. ### Exchanges & market infrastructure

- For exchanges, analyze **handled volume** alongside market share — share loss and volume growth are not mutually exclusive. — `_system/reference/investment-wisdom/stahl/Stahl-Exchanges-Less-Talk-More-Figures-2009.pdf` `[active 2026-05-21]`
- Exchange operating margins can remain extraordinarily high (20–75%+) through severe market stress — durability matters more than one-year EPS dips. — `_system/reference/investment-wisdom/stahl/Stahl-Exchanges-Less-Talk-More-Figures-2009.pdf` `[active 2026-05-21]`
- Fee model matters: ad valorem pricing ties revenue to market levels; per-trade/volume pricing can be more resilient in drawdowns. — `_system/reference/investment-wisdom/stahl/Stahl-Exchanges-Less-Talk-More-Figures-2009.pdf` `[active 2026-05-21]`
- For an exchange croupier trading at peak average daily turnover, size a peak-cycle reserve as an **explicit negative component** before treating spot EPS as a floor; volume mean-reversion is the binding bear case, not a tail. — `_system/memory/daily/2026-07-24.md` `[active 2026-08-09 · agent]`
- LSEG is a croupier: data, index and clearing tolls on a recurring income base — **"Over 70% of our income is recurring in nature and benefits from long-term customer relationships"** (73% of total income including recoveries), at a 50.3% adjusted EBITDA margin in FY2025 — **not organic**: p. 4 carries footnote 4 on that figure, "Includes benefit from the Post Trade Solutions transaction", so it is not comparable to the 48.8% prior year without backing that benefit out. — `LSEG/investor-documents/ir-lseg/annual_report_2025.pdf` (p. 5 "Our investment case" for the >70% and 73% figures; p. 4 "Group highlights" for the margin and its footnote 4) `[active 2026-08-09 · agent]`

### Real diversification

- ~50% of S&P 500 names are statistically irrelevant to index performance (≤7 bp weight each); index "diversification" is narrower than it appears. — `_system/reference/investment-wisdom/stahl/Stahl-Achievement-of-Diversification-2020.pdf` `[active 2026-05-21]`
- Low index weight ≠ low economic importance — compare employees, revenue, and real-economy footprint, not cap-weight alone. — `_system/reference/investment-wisdom/stahl/Stahl-Achievement-of-Diversification-2020.pdf` `[active 2026-05-21]`
- Where outcomes are near-independent and idiosyncratic failure rates are high (biotech), diversified factor exposure is more coherent than concentrated event bets — base rates beat conviction when internal correlation is low. — `_system/memory/daily/2026-07-09.md` `[active 2026-08-09 · agent]`

### Spinoffs & corporate simplification

- Spinoffs and separations can unlock value when conglomerate complexity hides segment economics — screen for forced simplification events. — `_system/reference/investment-wisdom/stahl/Stahl-Spinoffs-Going-Separate-Ways-2015.pdf` `[active 2026-05-21]`

### Structural rhymes

- A land-grant checkerboard rhyme is structural, not commodity-identical. A synthetic substitute (soda ash) caps mineral pricing power in a way oil and gas royalties are not capped — do not clone a TPL valuation onto a different mineral because the land pattern matches. — `_system/memory/daily/2026-07-27.md`; `_system/memory/daily/2026-07-31.md` `[active 2026-08-09 · agent]`
