### Normalize before you claim a floor

- Trailing FCF near zero during a capex cycle is a **trough illustration**, not an owner-cash floor. Normalize the cycle before stance, and exclude warrant and mark-to-market gains from owner cash. — `_system/memory/daily/2026-07-18.md` `[active 2026-08-09 · agent]`
- Under milestone billing, operating cash flow can run ahead of GAAP earnings quality during a backlog build. Normalize before stance rather than reading the cash conversion as durable. — `_system/memory/daily/2026-07-18.md` `[active 2026-08-09 · agent]`

### What is not net asset value

- A PEA net present value is not equity NAV for a pre-reserve developer until probability of permitting and financing, and expected dilution, are haircut out. — `_system/memory/daily/2026-07-18.md` `[active 2026-08-09 · agent]`
- RPO or backlog per share is not NAV. Levered equity written against a backlog requires an interest-cost and customer-concentration stress before any per-share claim. — `_system/memory/daily/2026-07-18.md` `[active 2026-08-09 · agent]`

### Where the inefficiency actually is

- The classic S&P 500 index-inclusion premium fell to ~0.8% in the 2010s (Greenwood & Sammon 2022). The residual edge is in *anticipating* membership changes and in smaller, less-liquid, less-substitutable names with steeper demand curves (Wurgler & Zhuravskaya; Petajisto). Rank index alerts by demand shock versus ADV and calendar proximity — never by the mere fact of a change. — `_system/memory/daily/2026-07-01.md`; `_system/memory/daily/2026-07-09.md` `[active 2026-08-09 · agent]`
- In biotech, traditional P/E and own-price momentum are inverted signals; use spend-based value and clinical peer momentum instead, and treat multi-specialist ownership (>50% of biotech funds) as the primary public quality screen. — `_system/memory/daily/2026-07-09.md` `[active 2026-08-09 · agent]`
- When ranking specialist 13F names, separate **issuer** size (where the small-cap edge lives) from **position** liquidity. A diversified short book is high short interest × weak consensus — a single high-SI name is never a conviction short. — `_system/memory/daily/2026-07-09.md` `[active 2026-08-09 · agent]`
