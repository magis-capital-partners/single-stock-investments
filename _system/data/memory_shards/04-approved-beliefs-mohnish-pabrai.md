### Dhando & asymmetric payoffs

- Seek **heads I win, tails I don't lose much** — downside protection matters more than upside precision. — Dhando framework; `_system/reference/investment-wisdom/pabrai/` letters 2005–2020 `[active 2026-05-21]`
- Understandable, durable, regulated businesses can still fail the 15% bar at fair bond-proxy multiples. **Watch** is the correct stance absent a price dislocation — quality is never a reason to pay any price. — `_system/memory/daily/2026-07-10.md` `[active 2026-08-09 · agent]`
- A high mechanical IRR computed on filing FCF at a depressed price stays **partial** dhando until the bear case explicitly models an FCF step-down. — `_system/memory/daily/2026-07-10.md` `[active 2026-08-09 · agent]`
- High cash yield backed by oligopoly pricing gives partial dhando only; a binary regulatory outcome prevents **full** dhando at any price implying a high-single-digit IRR or less. — `_system/memory/daily/2026-07-10.md` `[active 2026-08-09 · agent]`
- Engineered-in stickiness (auto tier-one, spec-locked components) limits permanent loss, but OEM pricing and the cycle cap upside asymmetry. Bounded downside on its own is not dhando. — `_system/memory/daily/2026-07-10.md` `[active 2026-08-09 · agent]`

### Bet sizing & concentration

- Run a concentrated portfolio of high-conviction ideas; redemptions are a feature when partners leave capital with fewer, better ideas. — `_system/reference/investment-wisdom/pabrai/Pabrai-Letter-l_010112.pdf` (2012) `[active 2026-05-21]`
- When subscriptions lag redemptions but idea quality is high, treat cash scarcity as a signal to prioritize — not to dilute into marginal names. — `_system/reference/investment-wisdom/pabrai/Pabrai-Letter-l_010124.pdf` (Jan 2024) `[active 2026-05-21]`

### Business selection

- Invest only in simple, understandable businesses with durable economics and honest management — skip complex stories requiring heroic assumptions. — `_system/reference/investment-wisdom/pabrai/` partner letters 2005–2025 `[active 2026-05-21]`
- Pre-revenue asset plays stay **partial** dhando until an SEC-filed contract defines the downside (a tenant lease, or non-recourse project financing). A resource estimate is not a floor. — `_system/memory/daily/2026-06-17.md` `[active 2026-08-09 · agent]`
