## Approved beliefs — Horizon Kinetics

### Equity yield curve & time arbitrage

- When future value and timing are reasonably knowable, institutional 12-month horizons create steep discounts (~35% annualized at ~3 years in HK case studies); patient capital captures **time arbitrage**. — `_system/reference/investment-wisdom/horizon-kinetics/Stahl-Equity-Yield-Curve-extract.txt` `[active 2026-05-22]`
- A dormant-asset discount is not an equity yield curve without a **filed catalyst date**. Absent a disclosed transaction, the curve is an analyst assumption dressed as a schedule. — `_system/memory/daily/2026-07-24.md` `[active 2026-08-09 · agent]`
- A dated strategic-alternatives process is what converts a dormant-asset discount into a plottable curve; dormancy on its own is not a catalyst. — `_system/memory/daily/2026-06-10.md` `[active 2026-08-09 · agent]`

### Predictive attributes

- Index screeners use **descriptive** (backward-looking) attributes; forward **predictive** predispositions — dormant assets, dated recovery, NPI deficit paydown — are more useful for prospective returns. — `_system/reference/investment-wisdom/horizon-kinetics/HK-Q1-2025-Commentary-extract.txt` `[active 2026-05-22]`

### Market structure discounts

- Royalty trusts, K-1 LPs, and yield-screen exclusions can persistently misprice otherwise debt-free, high-margin pass-through securities. — `_system/reference/investment-wisdom/horizon-kinetics/HK-Q1-2026-Commentary-extract.txt` `[active 2026-05-22]`
- In non-reporting OTC limited partnerships, GAAP book can understate a royalty franchise by multiples, because land and reserves sit at historical cost. Price-to-book is not the market-structure test — the franchise cash flow is. — `_system/memory/daily/2026-06-10.md` `[active 2026-08-09 · agent]`
  - The BVERS price pair originally carried here (~$595 book against ~$2,400) was removed 2026-08-09: the cited daily log labels the $2,400 an `[Assumption]`, not a quote, so promoting it both froze a dated number in a belief and contradicted the illiquid-venue rule above (`On illiquid venues (TSE PRO, OTC Pink), treat vendor price feeds as discovery evidence only`). — `_system/memory/daily/2026-06-10.md`; `BVERS/research/deep_dive_2026-06-10.md`
- A market structure discount (no 10-K, thin OTC quote) can hide land-heavy or royalty economics, but **minority realization** through nonprofit or family-controlled governance is the binding constraint — the discount may be entirely correct. — `_system/memory/daily/2026-07-24.md`; `_system/memory/daily/2026-06-10.md` `[active 2026-08-09 · agent]`

### Scarce assets

- For scarce-asset land owners with off-balance-sheet legacy estates, compare price to the proof-first component sum and to Lawrence owner cash — not to GAAP book. A premium over the component base embeds milestone timing and scarcity; it is not evidence of a filing error. — `_system/memory/daily/2026-07-24.md` `[active 2026-08-09 · agent]`

### Transitory problems

- Temporary dividend suspension from recoverable operator charges (Mesabi, SJT, HE) can be an equity yield curve setup when deficit and timeline are bounded — but capex overruns extend the curve. — `_system/reference/investment-wisdom/horizon-kinetics/HK-Q1-2025-Commentary-extract.txt` `[active 2026-05-22]`
