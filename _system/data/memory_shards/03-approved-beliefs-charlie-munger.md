## Approved beliefs — Charlie Munger

### Mental models & latticework

- Build a latticework of mental models from multiple disciplines before specializing in stock picking; isolated facts without models produce "confusion and folly." — `_system/reference/investment-wisdom/munger/Munger-1994-Elementary-Worldly-Wisdom.pdf` `[active 2026-05-21]`

### Inversion & failure avoidance

- Before asking "why is this a good investment," ask "what would make this fail?" and whether we can survive being wrong. — `_system/reference/investment-wisdom/munger/Munger-1994-Elementary-Worldly-Wisdom.pdf` `[active 2026-05-21]`
- Invert an implausible distribution claim before trusting a data aggregator's share count. When $5.3M of net income appears to fund ~$157M of dividends on 3.46M shares, the share count or the yield is wrong — not the arithmetic. — `_system/memory/daily/2026-07-24.md` `[active 2026-08-09 · agent]`

### Psychology of misjudgment

- Maintain a checklist of ~25 standard causes of human misjudgment; watch for **lollapalooza** effects when several biases combine. — `_system/reference/investment-wisdom/munger/Psychology-of-Human-Misjudgment.pdf` `[active 2026-05-21]`
- Incentive-caused bias is among the most reliable forces in human behavior — always map who gets paid for what before trusting management narrative. — `_system/reference/investment-wisdom/munger/Psychology-of-Human-Misjudgment.pdf` `[active 2026-05-21]`
- Incentive-caused bias has a repeatable public-markets fingerprint: a **recently internalized manager**, plus an **earnout tied to a self-reported metric** (Adjusted EBITDA), plus a **restatement in that same accounting area**. Track the combination, not any single leg. — `WHK/investor-documents/sec-edgar/424B4_20260609_rpt_acc0001193125_26_264014.htm`; `_system/memory/daily/2026-08-05.md` `[active 2026-08-09 · agent]`

### Moats & competitive advantage

- Prefer businesses where rational managers can defend pricing power over long periods; moat quality matters more than near-term EPS noise. — `_system/reference/investment-wisdom/munger/Munger-1994-Elementary-Worldly-Wisdom.pdf` `[active 2026-05-21]`
- A moat that has been stable for decades still produces sub-15% returns when price outruns per-share cash growth. Moat quality answers "will it survive," not "what will I earn" — the two questions need separate answers. — `_system/memory/daily/2026-07-10.md` `[active 2026-08-09 · agent]`
- Goodwill impairment on acquired software is a signal that management paid for optionality the market no longer credits. Either re-underwrite that optionality inside segment growth, or write its terminal value to zero explicitly — do not leave it implicit in the multiple. — `_system/memory/daily/2026-07-10.md` `[active 2026-08-09 · agent]`

### Circle of competence & simplicity

- If we cannot explain the business in plain language within five sentences, we are outside circle of competence — pass or defer. — `_system/reference/investment-wisdom/munger/Munger-1994-Elementary-Worldly-Wisdom.pdf` `[active 2026-05-21]`

### Structure before price

- For externally managed vehicles (mortgage REITs, advised trusts), capitalize the manager fee drag as an explicit liability component before comparing price to book. Filing book alone is not a dhando floor. — `_system/memory/daily/2026-07-24.md` `[active 2026-08-09 · agent]`
- For asset-backed financing holdcos, separate filing book equity from the economic overhang of margin loans and prepaid forwards before treating price-to-book as dhando; each pledge is its own proof row. — `_system/memory/daily/2026-07-24.md` `[active 2026-08-09 · agent]`
- For single-segment equipment suppliers with disclosed customer concentration, treat the peak fiscal year as an upper bound and trough operating cash flow as the stress case before any stance above **watch**. — `_system/memory/daily/2026-07-24.md` `[active 2026-08-09 · agent]`
- On illiquid venues (TSE PRO, OTC Pink), treat vendor price feeds as discovery evidence only; confirm a live broker quote before sizing, however attractive the mechanical return looks. — `_system/memory/daily/2026-07-24.md` `[active 2026-08-09 · agent]`
