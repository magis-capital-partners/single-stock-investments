Notice of 2026 Annual Meeting of Shareholders

 

Verizon Communications Inc.’s Annual Meeting of Shareholders will be held on Thursday, May 21, 2026 at 10:00 a.m. Eastern Daylight Time, virtually via the internet at meetnow.global/VZ2026.

 

Proposals to be voted on at the meeting are listed below along with the Board of Directors’ recommendations.

 

The Board of Directors recommend a vote FOR all the nominees listed and FOR Proposals 2 - 4:

1.Election of Directors
2.Advisory vote to approve executive compensation
3.Approval of Verizon’s 2026 Long-Term Incentive Plan
 4.Ratification of appointment of independent registered public accounting firm

 

The Board of Directors recommends a vote AGAINST the following proposals:

5.Board oversight of material issues related to climate change
6.Independent board chair
7.Risks of non-fiduciary executive compensation metrics
