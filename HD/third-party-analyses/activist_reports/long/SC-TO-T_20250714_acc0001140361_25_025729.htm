This Tender Offer Statement on Schedule TO (this “Schedule TO”) relates to the tender offer by Gold Acquisition Sub, Inc., a Delaware corporation (“Purchaser”) and an indirect, wholly-owned subsidiary of The Home Depot, Inc., a Delaware corporation (“The Home Depot”), for all of the outstanding shares of common stock, par value $0.01 per share (the “Shares”), of GMS Inc., a Delaware corporation (“GMS”), at a price of $110.00 per share in cash, without interest and subject to any required withholding of taxes, upon the terms and conditions set forth in the offer to purchase, dated July 14, 2025 (the “Offer to Purchase”), a copy of which is attached as Exhibit (a)(1)(A), and in the related letter of transmittal (the “Letter of Transmittal”), a copy of which is attached as Exhibit (a)(1)(B), which, as each may be amended or supplemented from time to time, collectively constitute the “Offer.”
All the information set forth in the Offer to Purchase, including Schedule I thereto, is incorporated by reference herein in response to Items 1 through 9 and Item 11 of this Schedule TO, and is supplemented by the information specifically provided in this Schedule TO.
