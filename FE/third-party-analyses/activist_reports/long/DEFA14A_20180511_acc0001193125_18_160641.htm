Annual Shareholder Meeting is May 15

Get ready to attend this year’s Annual Meeting of Shareholders – the Company’s Board of Directors recommends voting “FOR” the election of the nominees in Item 1 and the proposals in Items 2 through 6.

 

 

The Annual Meeting of Shareholders is Tues., May 15 at 8 a.m. at the John S. Knight Center in Akron.

Company shareholders as of the March 16 record date should have received the FirstEnergy proxy materials to vote via the Internet, phone, or by completing and returning their proxy card/voting instruction form through the United States Postal Service in the envelope provided. The Company’s Board of Directors recommends voting “FOR” the election of the nominees in Item 1 and the proposals in Items 2 through 6, and “AGAINST” the shareholder proposal in Item 7.

This will be George Smart’s final meeting as Chairman of the Board. As previously reported, he and Board member William T. Cottle will retire as of the Annual Meeting. Also, Donald T. Misheff will assume his role as George’s successor as Chairman of the Board, and this will be Sandra Pianalto’s first Annual Meeting since joining the Board in February.
