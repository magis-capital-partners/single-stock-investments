Michael Saylor

So what we've done is we've created a product - STRC. It pays a dividend. Right now it's twice a month. It'll eventually be - it'll probably be - every day after the vote at the end of the month.

***

And right now we're in the process of taking it to daily. And the idea behind daily is we're moving toward a world where everything trades 365 days a year, any minute and anywhere. So why not create a fixed income instrument that pays you every single day with 365 dividend events or income events? This would be the ideal thing to tokenize, but if you want to cross trade or you want to arbitrage between the UAE markets, the European markets, the Chinese markets, the Hong Kong markets, the US markets, and do it on the weekend and do it in a tokenized economy, then you'd like an instrument where it's in your interest to buy it on Saturday morning, sell it on Sunday morning, buy it back on Sunday night, right, in whatever currency. And so that's why we're going to daily. We expect it'll increase liquidity and strip volatility and increase optionality, attract new pools of capital, allow a lot of higher speed trading strategies. That makes it better for the credit, that makes it better for the equity, it makes it better for the investor.

***

Jeff Park

But the feature that I think is really interesting, at least for crypto native investors who've long thought about what is the actual benefit of going to crypto, is this aspect of payment frequency. ***

Mr. Saylor

And so Strive did it. We're like, okay, well, let's see if something breaks and what happens. And what we found is their turnover was 3X ours. Like clearly they tapped into a new pool of capital. And you could imagine what it might be. I mean, look, you could sell our product, buy their product, pick up 10 days worth of dividends, trade back into our product and just do high frequency trading. Or maybe you might sell their product in the morning, do a bunch of trading for six hours and buy their product back in the evening. So, there was a reason to buy and sell, and you could go and capture and ARB this. And we found there are a lot of pools of capital that they could classify a daily dividend as a different money instrument. And so, there are pools of capital that will buy it that would not buy a monthly or quarterly instrument. So, after their success, we thought, well, we should do something similar.

And then we considered, the entire world's going to 365-day tokenized, so rather than every business day, we might as well make it every calendar day because that way you have an incentive to buy it on Saturday morning or buy it on Sunday morning or disincentive to sell it on Sunday morning. And it just seemed like that was just a better way to do it. So, we're just pushing the envelope. As we joke, if we could pay hourly, we'd pay hourly. But right now, what we want to do is we want to increase liquidity. And so when there's 365 times when you might want to buy it or you might want to sell it, that's better than four times a year. So, we want to increase the liquidity. We want to strip the volatility. If you pay a 50 cent dividend, the thing will trade down 40 cents or 50 cents. Well, if we pay a one penny dividend, it might trade down one penny. I'd much rather have it trade down one penny on $100 base than trade down 50. So this is all just about perfecting the credit instrument. And partly it's just the right thing to do in the TradFi markets. And then also it's the right thing to do to get ready for the DeFi markets and the digital tokenized markets. And we've seen this explosion in tokenized Stretch.

***

And so we see that tokenizing taking off, and we feel like after talking to all of our partners, people that are doing it like Apex and Saturn, it's pretty clear it's beneficial for them if we go to daily, because we strip that vol.
