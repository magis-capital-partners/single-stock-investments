Item 7.01Regulation FD Disclosure.

 

Transocean Ltd. (“Transocean”) announced today that Petrobras awarded two of the Company’s ultra-deepwater drillships, the Ocean Rig Corcovado and Ocean Rig Mykonos, with a 629 day contract and 550 day contract, respectively. The estimated firm contract backlog, excluding mobilization, is approximately $123 million and $118 million, respectively. The rigs are expected to commence work in Brazil in November 2019 and the contracts include priced options for 680 days and 815 days, respectively.

 

A copy of the press release announcing the contracts for the Ocean Rig Corcovado and the Ocean Rig Mykonos is attached hereto and is incorporated herein by reference as Exhibit 99.1.
