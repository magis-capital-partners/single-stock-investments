A MESSAGE FROM PRESIDENT AND CEO KEELAN ADAMSON

 

Following our announcement earlier this week of Transocean’s plans to acquire Valaris, I wanted to follow up with some key information and direction.

 

·Until this transaction closes, which is anticipated to be in the second half of 2026, Transocean and Valaris will continue to operate as two independent companies. Our priority remains to deliver safe, reliable, and efficient operations to our customers around the world. It is business as usual.

 

·An integration planning team will be established to engage with Valaris counterparts under strict legal guidelines. Outside of normal industry events, no one else is authorized to engage with Valaris employees on any business matter related to this transaction.

 

·For the avoidance of doubt, and in accordance with normal business practices, you should not discuss any active or expected commercial opportunities for either company with them. This includes bids, contracts, negotiations, and market intelligence with or about any business partner, including customers and vendors.

 

·Any communication that has the appearance of attempting to coordinate our commercial activities—even if unintended—may be unlawful and could compromise our ability to obtain regulatory approval.

 

·Additionally, be mindful of ALL written communications regarding the transaction to your staff and to customers and vendors, as they may be subject to review by governmental-approval authorities.

 

1

 

 

If you have any questions about specific interactions with Valaris personnel or other third parties, contact Simone Ramcharitar or Kyle Vento in the Legal Department.

 

I will communicate regularly on our progress as we move toward closing the transaction. If you have any general questions or concerns, please raise them with your supervisor or manager and we will address them in due course.

 

This is a very exciting time for our company, but the transaction cannot distract us from our “day job.” Your continued support and focus are crucial, and I truly appreciate the time, talent, and dedication that keep Transocean moving in the right direction every day.

 

Keelan

The transaction relates to the proposed business combination of Transocean and Valaris pursuant to the terms of the Business Combination Agreement, dated as of February 9, 2026, and is being made by way of a scheme of arrangement pursuant to section 99 of the Companies Act 1981, as amended, under the laws of Bermuda.
