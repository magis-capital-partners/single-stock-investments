Item 7.01Regulation FD

​

On January 8, 2020, Transocean Ltd. announced that Transocean Inc., its wholly-owned subsidiary, has priced its previously-announced offering (the “Offering”) of U.S. $750 million aggregate principal amount of senior unsecured notes due 2027 (the “Notes”) to eligible purchasers pursuant to Rule 144A/Regulation S. The Notes will be guaranteed by Transocean Ltd. and certain of Transocean Inc.’s subsidiaries.

 

The notes will bear interest at the rate of 8.0% per annum. The Offering is expected to close on or about January 17, 2020, subject to customary closing conditions. The company intends to use the net proceeds from the Offering to refinance, repurchase and/or redeem certain of its existing indebtedness in one or more transactions, and/or for general corporate purposes.

 

A copy of the press release announcing the pricing of the Offering is furnished herewith as Exhibit 99.1 and is incorporated herein by reference.
