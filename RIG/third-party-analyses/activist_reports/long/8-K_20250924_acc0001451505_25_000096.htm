Item 7.01Regulation FD Disclosure.

​

Transocean Ltd. (the “Company”) announced today that it intends to offer and sell 100,000,000 Company shares, par value $0.10, in an underwritten public offering. The Company expects to grant the underwriters a 30-day option to purchase up to an additional 15,000,000 shares in the offering at the public offering price, less underwriting discounts and commissions.

​

The foregoing description does not purport to be complete and is qualified in its entirety by the full text of the press release announcing the offering, a copy of which is attached hereto and incorporated by reference as Exhibit 99.1.
