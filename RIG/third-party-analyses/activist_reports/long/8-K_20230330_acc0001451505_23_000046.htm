Item 5.03Amendments to Articles of Incorporation or Bylaws; Change in Fiscal Year.

​

On March 28, 2023, the Articles of Association of Transocean Ltd. the “Company”) were amended (as amended, the “Articles of Association”) to reflect changes in the Company’s total issued share capital resulting from the previous issuance of 681 Transocean Ltd. shares out of conditional capital in connection with the exercise of certain 0.50% exchangeable senior bonds due 2023 issued by Transocean Inc., a wholly-owned subsidiary of the Company, prior to their maturity on January 30, 2023. Following the amendments, the Articles of Association reflect a share capital of CHF 79,724,475.30 divided into 797,244,753 fully paid registered shares.

​

The foregoing description of the Articles of Association does not purport to be complete and is qualified in its entirety by reference to the full text of the Articles of Association, a copy of which is filed herewith as Exhibit 3.1 and is incorporated herein by reference.
