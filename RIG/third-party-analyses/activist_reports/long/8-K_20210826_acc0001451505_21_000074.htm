Item 7.01Regulation FD Disclosure.

​

Transocean Ltd. announced today that BOE Exploration & Production LLC (“BOE”) awarded Transocean a $252 million firm contract for its newbuild ultra-deepwater drillship, the Deepwater Atlas, including a mobilization fee of $30 million.

​

This award results from the final investment decision of BOE and its Shenandoah project working interest owners to sanction the previously announced Shenandoah project in the U.S. Gulf of Mexico.

​

A copy of the press release announcing the contract for the Deepwater Atlas is incorporated herein by reference as Exhibit 99.1.
