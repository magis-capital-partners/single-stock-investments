The below letter (or a substantially similar form) has been sent and will be sent by or on behalf of Transocean Ltd. to its shareholders prior to the extraordinary general meeting of Shareholders of Transocean to be held on November 24, 2026 in connection with the proposed business combination with Valaris Limited.

 

 

Dear Transocean Shareholder:

 

We previously sent you proxy materials for the Extraordinary General Meeting of Transocean Ltd. to be held on November 24, 2026 at 3:00 p.m. Swiss time at Transocean’s offices, Turmstrasse 30, 6312 Steinhausen, Switzerland.

 

At the meeting, you will be asked to approve, among other things, the issuance of Transocean Shares needed to complete Transocean’s business combination with Valaris Limited.

 

The Transocean Board UNANIMOUSLY recommends that you vote “FOR” each of the Transocean Resolutions.

 

Your vote is important, no matter how many or how few shares you own.

 

If you have not already done so, PLEASE VOTE TODAY by submitting your voting instructions electronically over the internet, by telephone or return the proxy card by mail as soon as possible. All electronic voting instructions or proxy cards must be received no later than 4:00 p.m. Eastern Time on November 23, 2026 (10:00 p.m. Swiss time on November 23, 2026).

 

Why the Transocean Board supports the business combination:

 

·A multi-year, deliberate evaluation. The Transocean Board and management exercised discipline before ultimately determining that a business combination with Valaris, at the Exchange Ratio of 15.235 Transocean Shares for each Valaris Share and on the negotiated terms, represented the most compelling strategic opportunity to enhance long-term shareholder value. The fixed Exchange Ratio provides certainty as to the relative ownership economics of the combined company and ensures that the value of the consideration payable to the Valaris Shareholders will not increase in the event that the market price of Valaris Shares increases prior to the completion of the business combination.

 

·An expanded fleet with greater customer reach. The combined fleet would position the company to serve additional customers with diverse project requirements across more geographies, increasing the long-term reliability and durability of cash flows.

 

·Revenue visibility. The combined company’s pro forma backlog of more than $12.7 billion as of November 2, 2026 provides clear visibility into future cash flows into future cash flows and supports the Transocean Board’s expectation of industry-leading free cash flow generation across the industry cycle.

 

 

 

 

·A stronger balance sheet. The all-stock structure requires no new cash financing and is expected to accelerate deleveraging, targeting a leverage ratio of approximately 1.5x within 24 months after closing.

 

·Cost synergies. More than $200 million of expected annual cost synergies have been identified, mainly from operational efficiencies.

 

·Tax-free reorganization. For United States federal income tax purposes, the business combination is expected to be a tax-free “reorganization” pursuant to section 368(a) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, as amended.

 

We thank you for your consideration and continued support.

 

Sincerely,

 

   
  Keelan I. Adamson   Jeremy D. Thigpen
  President and Chief Executive Officer of Transocean Ltd.   Executive Chair of the Board of Directors of Transocean Ltd.
