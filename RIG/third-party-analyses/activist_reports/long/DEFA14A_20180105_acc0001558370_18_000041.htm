To:  Certain Employees of Transocean holding shares of Transocean Ltd.

Subject:  Reminder to Vote your Transocean Shares

 

REMINDER TO VOTE YOUR TRANSOCEAN SHARES

An extraordinary general meeting of Transocean shareholders is scheduled for Tuesday, January 16, 2018, to vote on proposals to approve the Songa Offshore transaction. The Transocean Board of Directors unanimously recommends that you vote “FOR” each of the proposals that are being submitted to shareholders at the meeting.

Please complete and return your proxy card or voting instruction form, or submit your voting instructions electronically over the Internet, as soon as possible. All proxy cards, voting instruction forms and electronic voting instructions must be received no later than 2:00 p.m. (CET) on January 16, 2018. Employees with Transocean shares eligible to vote at the meeting may have received correspondence from E*Trade on or about December 30th with instructions on how to electronically vote.

More information is included in the proxy statement and other documents, which can be found on our website at: www.deepwater.com.
