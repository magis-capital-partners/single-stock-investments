Item 7.01. Regulation FD Disclosure

Following a decision by Pacific Dental Services (“PDS”) to select a competitor as its distributor effective January 2020, Henry Schein notes that the revenue associated with the loss of the supplies, equipment and services business with PDS represents less than one-half of one percent of projected total 2019 worldwide Henry Schein sales and is immaterial to our results of operations.
