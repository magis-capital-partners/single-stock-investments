The following is a press release issued by NOVAGOLD Resources Inc. (“NOVAGOLD”) announcing its 2026 third quarter results on October 8, 2026:

 

NEWS RELEASE

 

NOVAGOLD Files THIRD Quarter 2026 Report
UNLOCKING DONLIN GOLD’S full POTENTIAL

and ADVANCING THE PATH TO DEVELOPMENT

 

►Donlin Gold Transaction announced: NOVAGOLD and Paulson Advisers LLC and its affiliates entered into a series of definitive agreements on July 21, 2026, pursuant to which NOVAGOLD’s ownership interest in Donlin Gold LLC will increase from 60% to 100% by acquiring Paulson’s 40% ownership interest in an all-share transaction that is expected to close by year-end.

 

►Financial Advisors selected: Donlin Gold appointed Endeavour Financial and Macquarie Capital to provide financial advisory services for the development of the Donlin Gold project in Alaska and to support the arrangement of project and infrastructure financing.

 

►Robust treasury: Ended the quarter with approximately $343.4 million in cash and term deposits — a strong financial position to advance the Donlin Gold project.

 

October 8, 2026 – Vancouver, British Columbia – NOVAGOLD RESOURCES INC. (“NOVAGOLD” or the “Company”) (NYSE American, TSX: NG) today filed its 2026 third quarter report and provided an update on its Tier One1 gold development project held by Donlin Gold LLC (“Donlin Gold”), which is owned 60% by NOVAGOLD through NOVAGOLD Resources Alaska, Inc., a wholly-owned subsidiary of the Company, and 40% by Paulson Advisers LLC and certain of its affiliates (collectively “Paulson”) through Donlin Gold Holdings LLC (“Donlin Gold Holdings”).

 

Details of the financial results for the quarter ended August 31, 2026 are presented in the consolidated financial statements and quarterly report on Form 10-Q filed on October 8, 2026, that is available on the Company’s website at www.novagold.com, on SEDAR+ at www.sedarplus.ca, and on EDGAR, the United States Securities and Exchange Commission’s (“the SEC”) website, at www.sec.gov.

 

All amounts are in thousands of U.S. dollars unless otherwise stated.

 

As detailed in the above-mentioned filings, NOVAGOLD held approximately $343.4 million in cash and term deposits as of August 31, 2026, and reported net third quarter operational cash expenditures of $28.1 million — reflecting $24.8 million to fund NOVAGOLD’s share of the Donlin Gold project and $3.3 million in corporate general and administrative costs.

 

As NOVAGOLD is a development-stage company with no production, the Company reported earnings of ($36.0) million and earnings per share of ($0.08) for the third quarter of 2026. NOVAGOLD’s results for this quarter reflect incremental expenditures at Donlin Gold due to ongoing Bankable Feasibility Study (BFS) activities and higher general and administrative expenses at NOVAGOLD primarily driven by ongoing professional fees related to the pending transactions announced on July 22, 2026 (the “Transactions”), partially offset by higher interest income. Professional fees are expected to remain elevated for the remainder of fiscal 2026 and into the early part of fiscal 2027 as we work toward the completion of the Transactions, currently expected to occur in the fourth calendar quarter of 2026.

 

President’s Message

 

Unlocking Donlin Gold’s Full Potential Through Strategic Ownership and Development

 

NOVAGOLD is strengthening its position as a “go-to” U.S. gold development company, having entered into a series of definitive agreements on July 21, 2026 with Paulson and NovaGold Corporation (“New NOVAGOLD”), pursuant to which, subject to the satisfaction of certain closing conditions and upon the completion of the Transactions, New NOVAGOLD would become the ultimate parent company of NOVAGOLD and its subsidiaries and would own, directly and indirectly, 100% of Donlin Gold. As per the release titled “NOVAGOLD Enters into Definitive Agreements to Acquire 100% of Donlin Gold”, the all-share transaction to acquire Paulson’s 40% interest would give NOVAGOLD shareholders equity participation in a well-capitalized gold developer with 100% ownership of a differentiated, strategic asset projected to be the largest single gold mine in the United States, with an expected market capitalization of approximately $4.9 billion.2 With projected average annual production of 1.3 million ounces during its first 10 full years of production3 and 1.1 million ounces annually over the 27-year mine life,4 Donlin Gold has the potential to become America’s largest single gold mine and a cornerstone asset of the U.S. gold industry.

These transformative Transactions will deliver substantial benefits to NOVAGOLD stakeholders, including generating immediate accretion across net asset value per share as well as gold reserves and resources per share through the addition of approximately 16 million ounces of Measured and Indicated Mineral Resources (inclusive of 13 million ounces in Proven and Probable Mineral Reserves within a safe, stable, and supportive jurisdiction). Furthermore, the Transactions increase projected attributable production metrics by over 520,000 ounces of annual gold production during the first 10 full years.5 Beyond these metrics, the Transactions streamline corporate decision-making and increase operational and capital efficiency by consolidating 100% ownership of Donlin Gold under a single corporate parent, while preserving NOVAGOLD’s independent governance through specific lock-up, standstill, and voting restrictions. The Transactions will also establish a single point of contact for engagement with key stakeholders, including longstanding Donlin Gold landowners, Calista Corporation (“Calista”) and The Kuskokwim Corporation (TKC), and create a better-aligned structure via a new U.S.-domiciled parent company, that will be listed on the New York Stock Exchange.

 

The Notice of Meeting and the Management Information Circular and Proxy Statement for the Special Meeting of Shareholders were filed with Canadian securities regulatory authorities and with the SEC and first mailed to shareholders on or about October 5, 2026. The Special Meeting of Shareholders will be held on November 3, 2026. The Transactions are expected to close by year-end, subject to shareholder approval, court approval, regulatory and stock exchange approval and the satisfaction of customary closing conditions.

 

Bankable Feasibility Study Engineering Integration Advances & Financial Advisors Appointed to Support the Arrangement of Project and Infrastructure Financing

 

Parallel to this strategic development, the Company continues to advance the Donlin Gold BFS, which remains on schedule for completion in 2027. A team led by Fluor Corporation (“Fluor”) continues to integrate major work packages and coordinate technical workstreams with specialist contractors WSP USA, Inc. (WSP), Worley Alaska, Inc. (“Worley”), and Hatch Ltd. (“Hatch”). This season’s geotechnical work for the BFS will conclude this fall with drill holes and test pits at Jungjuk Port and access corridor, borrow sites and plant site, and for Engineering, Procurement, and Construction Management (EPCM) infrastructure. Resource modelling, mine planning, and Front-End Engineering Design (FEED) activities are progressing as planned, while EPCM coordination on a Class 3 estimate is well under way. This disciplined approach to key infrastructure and unit operations is designed to support a high-quality BFS.

 

Additionally, Donlin Gold appointed Endeavour Financial and Macquarie Capital as financial advisors to provide advisory services for the development of the project in Alaska. Under this mandate, the advisors will assist Donlin Gold in evaluating funding alternatives — including conventional project finance, infrastructure financing, and potential support from governmental agencies and/or sovereign wealth funds — and executing a structured project financing strategy. By coordinating engagement with financiers and advancing due diligence in tandem with the BFS, NOVAGOLD aims to optimize its funding mix while preserving long-term shareholder value. The advisors will provide tailored guidance as the project progresses toward a final investment decision, following the completion of the BFS and related workstreams.

To further support the BFS and prepare for subsequent phases of development and eventual commercial operation, Donlin Gold has continued to build its team across key technical, operational, workforce development, and support functions. Since the beginning of 2026, the team has grown from approximately 35 to 77 employees, currently based in Anchorage and Vancouver, to support ongoing site, BFS, and contractor activities. Management continues to assess organizational requirements and recruit for critical positions as project activities advance.

 

Looking ahead, Donlin Gold’s combination of scale, grade, and exploration potential provides a strong foundation as the project advances through the BFS and subsequent stages of development. With approximately 40 million ounces of Measured and Indicated Mineral Resources (inclusive of Mineral Reserves), comprising 560 million tonnes grading 2.22 grams per tonne6 — more than twice the global industry average grade7 — Donlin Gold combines large-scale, high-grade open-pit mineralization with a long mine life and low estimated operating costs8. Expected average annual gold production of more than one million ounces further underscores the project’s sheer scale, while less than 7.5% of the broader land package has been systematically explored, providing significant potential for further exploration.

 

A Legacy of Collaboration Supports the Project’s Continued Advancement

 

Engagement with local communities, stakeholders and government representatives remains an important part of Donlin Gold’s approach. Built over decades of dialogue with more than 60 communities across the region, these relationships provide an ongoing forum for sharing information, understanding local perspectives, and incorporating community input as the project progresses. This sustained engagement and collaboration continues to reinforce the long-standing relationships that Donlin Gold has established throughout the region and in Alaska more broadly.

 

During the third quarter, in coordination with Calista, the Company conducted a series of community visits to six villages, alongside a dedicated community meeting in Crooked Creek — the village closest to the Donlin Gold project. These efforts focused specifically on the Subsistence Plan and a general project update, and fostered ongoing dialogue on subsistence-related matters and project activities. This commitment to regional partnership was further reinforced through participation in Calista’s Annual Meeting in Bethel, TKC’s Annual Meeting in Crooked Creek, and the Calista Shareholder Relations Committee meeting in Anchorage, ensuring sustained engagement with Alaska Native Corporation leadership and shareholders.

 

Donlin Gold also facilitated several key initiatives for the Subsistence Community Advisory Committee (SCAC), including in-person and virtual meetings to gather feedback on the Subsistence Plan and Barge Communication Plan. SCAC members also toured the Red Dog Mine, providing an opportunity to observe a large-scale mining operation firsthand and discuss considerations relevant to communities in the region. At Donlin Gold, site tours provided Alaska Native Corporation landowners Calista and TKC with further insight into ongoing fieldwork and project activities. Additional tours were hosted for representatives of the Alaska Native Village Corporation Association, Congressman Nick Begich’s resource staff, and trade association leaders from the Alaska Chamber, the Alaska Support Industry Alliance, the Alaska Oil and Gas Association, the Resource Development Council, and the Alaska Miners Association.

A Long-Term Commitment to the Region and its People

 

NOVAGOLD’s approach to responsible development extends across education, community wellness, cultural preservation and environmental stewardship — with initiatives designed to contribute to the economic, health and social well-being of our employees, Donlin Gold employees and contractors, as well as communities throughout the Yukon-Kuskokwim (Y-K) region. Donlin Gold supports these efforts through fisheries studies, environmental activities, subsistence initiatives and grants, while recognizing the importance of the region’s traditions and subsistence way of life, where environmental health is paramount. Year to date, Donlin Gold has conducted over 25 community visits and briefings throughout the Y-K region, made ongoing social investments through financial and in-kind contributions as well as significant employee involvement, and participated in more than 150 engagements with government representatives, agencies, and candidates.

 

During the third quarter, Donlin Gold participated in educational and community initiatives across the Y-K region, including student engagement in local communities and continued support for Alaska EXCEL, which provides educational and professional opportunities for rural Alaska students and young adults. Donlin Gold also continued to support regional safety initiatives, including the distribution of life vests in communities along the Kuskokwim River and its sponsorship of the Iron Dog helmet safety program through Alaska Safe Riders.

 

Environmental monitoring and stewardship activities also carried on during the quarter, including aquatic monitoring near the proposed mine site and at Snow Gulch, where monitoring of fish populations and newly developed habitat continued to demonstrate species diversity consistent with previous surveys, including the presence of salmon. The 2026 George River salmon smolt monitoring program was also completed in coordination with the Native Village of Napaimute, with more than 7,000 fish sampled over 30 days. These activities contribute to Donlin Gold’s ongoing environmental baseline data collection and broader understanding of conditions in and around the project area.

 

Preserving Donlin Gold’s Regulatory Approvals and Permitting Position

 

Donlin Gold is a federally permitted project. The permitting framework in Alaska involves extensive review and coordination among federal and state agencies, regional stakeholders, and communities throughout the Y-K region.

 

Permitting activities in the third quarter of 2026 advanced as expected. Work continues on the dam safety permitting process for the project’s seven dams, with submission of Detailed Design Packages anticipated by end of 2026 or early 2027 as previously expected and with potential issuance of the Dam Safety Certificates expected in 2028.

After the quarter end, on September 23, 2026, the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, in consultation with the Bureau of Land Management, published Donlin Gold’s Supplemental Environmental Impact Statement (SEIS), which analyzes a larger theoretical tailings dam release, while leaving Donlin Gold’s federal permits in place. The SEIS is open for public comment until October 23, 2026, with the Final SEIS anticipated in April 2027.

 

All federal and state permits and approvals granted to Donlin Gold remain in effect as the legal appeals proceed. Donlin Gold and its owners continue to support the federal and state agencies in defending the extensive permitting process undertaken for the project, while working with the relevant federal agencies and stakeholders toward an appropriate remedy in response to the Federal District Court’s remand decision. Donlin Gold continues to advance permitting activities in a diligent and responsible manner, and to engage with communities and stakeholders as the project progresses.

Supporting technical and site activities are also progressing, including geotechnical drilling and materials assessments along the planned upriver port and access road, plant site, and proposed dam locations.

2026 Outlook

 

NOVAGOLD’s operating expenditures for fiscal year 2026 reflect additional incurred legal and other professional fees associated with the Transactions.

About NOVAGOLD

 

NOVAGOLD is a well-financed precious metals company focused on the development of the Donlin Gold project in Alaska, one of the safest mining jurisdictions in the world. With approximately 40 million ounces of gold in Measured and Indicated Mineral Resources (approximately 560 million tonnes at an average grade of 2.22 grams per tonne on a 100% basis)10 inclusive of Proven and Probable Mineral Reserves, the Donlin Gold project is regarded to be one of the largest, highest-grade, and most prospective known open-pit gold deposits in the world. According to the 2025 Technical Report and the 2025 Technical Report Summary, the Donlin Gold project is expected to produce an average of more than one million ounces per year over a 27-year mine life on a 100% basis once in production.11

 

About Donlin Gold Holdings

 

Donlin Gold Holdings, 100% wholly-owned by Paulson Advisers LLC and its affiliates, is the 40% owner of the Donlin Gold project. Donlin Gold Holdings, together with NOVAGOLD, owns 100% of Donlin Gold and shares equal voting and operating control with NOVAGOLD through its operating agreement.
