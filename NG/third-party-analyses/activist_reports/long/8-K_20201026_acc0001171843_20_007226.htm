Item 7.01 Regulation FD

 

On October 26, 2020, NOVAGOLD RESOURCES INC. (“NOVAGOLD”) issued a joint press release with Donlin Gold LLC and Barrick Gold Corporation (“Barrick”) reporting more results from a drill program that NOVAGOLD and Barrick, co-owners of Donlin Gold LLC, commenced in February 2020 to validate recent geological and resource modeling controls developed by the co-owners and to test potential extensions of high-grade zones at the Donlin Gold project. A copy of the press release is furnished as Exhibit 99.1 to this report and incorporated herein by reference.

 

The information contained in the press release attached hereto is being furnished and shall not be deemed filed for purposes of Section 18 of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as amended (the “Exchange Act”), or otherwise subject to the liability of that section, and shall not be incorporated by reference into any registration statement or other document filed under the Securities Act of 1933, as amended, or the Exchange Act, except as shall be expressly set forth by specific reference in such filing.
