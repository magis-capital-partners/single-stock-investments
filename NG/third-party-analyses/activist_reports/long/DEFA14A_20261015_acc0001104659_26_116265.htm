NOVAGOLD Social Content

 

Wednesday, October 14

 

LinkedIn

 

NOVAGOLD Files Its Third Quarter 2026 Report — Unlocking Donlin Gold’s Path to Development

 

NOVAGOLD has filed its Q3 2026 report, highlighting major milestones that strengthen the Company’s position as a leading U.S. gold development company.

 

Key updates include:

 

Donlin Gold transaction — NOVAGOLD and Paulson entered into definitive agreements on July 21, 2026, pursuant to which NOVAGOLD would acquire Paulson’s 40% interest in Donlin Gold in an all share-transaction that is expected to close by year-end and create a consolidated, streamlined structure.

 

Financial Advisors selected — Donlin Gold has appointed Endeavour Financial and Macquarie Capital to provide financial advisory services and support project and infrastructure financing.

 

Robust treasury — ~$343.4M in cash and term deposits at quarter-end, a strong financial position to advance the Donlin Gold project.

 

BFS progress — Engineering integration led by Fluor continues on schedule for completion in 2027, with extensive geotechnical, FEED, and EPCM work underway.

 

Community & permitting momentum — Over 25 community visits year-to-date and 150 engagements with government representatives, agencies, and candidates, continued environmental stewardship, and advancement of dam safety and SEIS processes.

 

With large scale, high grade, and strong exploration potential, Donlin Gold remains one of the most significant gold development opportunities in the United States.
