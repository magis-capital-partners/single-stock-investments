Item 7.01 Regulation FD

 

On August 13, 2018, NOVAGOLD RESOURCES INC. (“NOVAGOLD”) issued a press release reporting the issuance of the Donlin Gold Record of Decision by the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers (“Corps”) and U.S. Bureau of Land Management (“BLM”). A copy of the press release is furnished as Exhibit 99.1 to this report and incorporated herein by reference.

 

The information contained in the press release attached hereto is being furnished and shall not be deemed filed for purposes of Section 18 of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as amended (the “Exchange Act”), or otherwise subject to the liability of that section, and shall not be incorporated by reference into any registration statement or other document filed under the Securities Act of 1933, as amended, or the Exchange Act, except as shall be expressly set forth by specific reference in such filing.

 

Item 8.01 Other Events

 

On August 13, 2018, the Corps and the BLM issued a favorable joint Record of Decision for the Donlin Gold project. The Record of Decision approves Donlin Gold LLC’s permit application filed under Section 404 of the Clean Water Act and Section 10 of the River and Harbors Act of 1899, and issuance of an offer to lease federal lands administered by the BLM in response to Donlin Gold LLC’s application for a right-of-way.
