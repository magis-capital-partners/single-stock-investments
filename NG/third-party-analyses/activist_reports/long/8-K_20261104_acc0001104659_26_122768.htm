Item 5.07. Submission of Matters to a Vote of Security Holders.

 

On November 3, 2026, NOVAGOLD Resources Inc. (“NOVAGOLD” or the “Company”) held a special meeting of shareholders (the “Special Meeting”) to consider certain proposals related to (i) the Arrangement Agreement, dated as of July 21, 2026 (the “Arrangement Agreement”), by and among the Company, NovaGold Corporation, a Delaware corporation (“New NOVAGOLD”) and Paulson Advisers LLC, a Delaware limited liability company (“Paulson”), (ii) the Master Implementation Agreement, dated as of July 21, 2026, by and among the Company, New NOVAGOLD, Paulson and the other parties thereto (the “Master Implementation Agreement”), (iii) the Contribution Agreement, dated as of July 21, 2026, by and between New NOVAGOLD and Paulson (the “Contribution Agreement”), and (iv) the Investor Rights Agreement, dated as of July 21, 2026, by and between New NOVAGOLD and Paulson (the “Investor Rights Agreement” and together with the Arrangement Agreement, the Master Implementation Agreement and the Contribution Agreement, the “Transaction Agreements”).

 

The Transaction Agreements provide that, among other things, upon the terms and subject to the conditions set forth therein, New NOVAGOLD will acquire all of the issued and outstanding common shares of NOVAGOLD, without par value (the “NOVAGOLD Shares”), by way of an arrangement under the Business Corporations Act (British Columbia) in accordance with a plan of arrangement (the “Arrangement”), and each holder of NOVAGOLD Shares (other than those who duly and validly exercised their dissent rights in accordance with the Arrangement) will receive on the closing of the Arrangement one share of voting common stock of New NOVAGOLD (each, a “New NOVAGOLD Voting Share”) for each NOVAGOLD Share. Substantially concurrently with (but immediately prior to) the consummation of the Arrangement, Paulson will cause certain of its affiliates to contribute all of their issued and outstanding equity interests in Donlin Gold Holdings LLC, a Delaware limited liability company, and Donlin Gold Holdings II LLC, a Delaware limited liability company, to New NOVAGOLD in exchange for New NOVAGOLD Voting Shares (with Paulson's beneficial ownership, together with its affiliates, of New NOVAGOLD Voting Shares capped at 19.99% of the issued and outstanding New NOVAGOLD Voting Shares) and shares of non-voting common stock of New NOVAGOLD (the “New NOVAGOLD Non-Voting Shares” and together with the New NOVAGOLD Voting Shares, the “New NOVAGOLD Shares”), which number of New NOVAGOLD Shares will be determined based on a 10% discount to the equity value of Paulson’s 40% ownership interest in Donlin Gold LLC, a Delaware limited liability company (“Donlin Gold”), implied by the equity value of NOVAGOLD based on the 10-day volume weighted average price of the NOVAGOLD Shares as of July 21, 2026.

 

Upon the consummation of the transactions contemplated by the Transaction Agreements (the “Transactions”), New NOVAGOLD will become the ultimate parent of NOVAGOLD and its subsidiaries, will be listed on the NYSE, will carry on the business currently conducted by NOVAGOLD and its subsidiaries and will own, directly and indirectly, 100% of Donlin Gold.

 

Upon the consummation of the Transactions, holders of NOVAGOLD Shares will become holders of New NOVAGOLD Voting Shares and own approximately 65% of New NOVAGOLD. Inclusive of its existing equity ownership in NOVAGOLD, Paulson (together with its affiliates) will own approximately 39.7% of New NOVAGOLD Shares, consisting of both New NOVAGOLD Voting Shares and New NOVAGOLD Non-Voting Shares, while its voting interest will be capped at 19.99% of the New NOVAGOLD Voting Shares.

 

At the close of business on September 23, 2026, the record date of the Special Meeting, there were 438,815,994 NOVAGOLD Shares outstanding, each of which was entitled to one vote with respect to each proposal voted on at the Special Meeting. A total of 303,446,751 NOVAGOLD Shares, representing approximately 69.15% of the outstanding NOVAGOLD Shares entitled to vote at the Special Meeting, were in attendance in person or by proxy, constituting a quorum for the transaction of business at the Special Meeting pursuant to the Company’s articles.
