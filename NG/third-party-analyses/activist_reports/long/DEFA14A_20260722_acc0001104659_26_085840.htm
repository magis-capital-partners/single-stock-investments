NOVAGOLD RESOURCES INC. 400 Burrard Street, Suite 1860, Vancouver, BC V6C 3A6 T 604 669 6227 TF 1 866 669 6227 F 604 669 6272 www.novagold.com NYSE-American, TSX: NG MEMO Dear Team, We are pleased to share a major turning point for NOVAGOLD Resources Inc. (“NOVAGOLD”). NOVAGOLD, Paulson Advisers LLC and their affiliates (“Paulson”) have entered into a series of definitive agreements (the “Transaction Agreements”), pursuant to which NOVAGOLD’s ownership interest in Donlin Gold LLC (“Donlin Gold”) will be increased from 60% to 100%, as a result of NOVAGOLD’s acquisition of Paulson’s 40% ownership interest in Donlin Gold in an all-share transaction. This transaction reflects our continued commitment to Donlin Gold and its long-term success. For example, NOVAGOLD plans to advance the Transaction Agreements alongside the ongoing Donlin Gold workstreams, which includes supporting a fully integrated Bankable Feasibility Study and therefore positioning the Donlin Gold project for its next stage of development once the study and project financing are complete. With Paulson’s continued active role on NovaGold Corporation’s (the new company) board of directors, this new structure will facilitate and expand access to capital, including from governmental agencies and sovereign wealth funds, to support Donlin Gold’s next phase of project development. You can view today’s announcement here. We have included below a list of employee questions and answers for your reference. Key Highlights: • Transaction overview: Creates a leading U.S. gold developer, with an equity value of approximately U.S.$4.2 billion in cash and 100% ownership of Donlin Gold. • Co-Chair governance structure: The NovaGold Corporation Board of Directors will be Co-Chaired by Dr. Thomas Kaplan and John Paulson and expanded from 10 to 11 directors. • Immediate accretion: Delivers immediate value enhancement for NOVAGOLD’s shareholders across several key measures, including net asset value per share, gold resources per share through the addition of over 16 million ounces of measured and indicated resources (with 13 million ounces in proven and probable reserves) 1 in an excellent jurisdiction with projected increases of more than 520,000 ounces in attributable annual gold production over the first 10 years2 . To: All NOVAGOLD Staff From: Greg Lang, President & CEO Date: 7/22/2026 Re: Partnership Update and Expansion of NOVAGOLD’s Stake in Donlin Gold

The new company, NovaGold Corporation, would be a Delaware corporation intended to be listed on NYSE. • Future strategic potential: Enhances NOVAGOLD’s ability to pursue future strategic opportunities. This transaction represents a significant step forward for NOVAGOLD - one that strengthens our position in the gold mining industry and reinforces our commitment to long-term growth and success. Page 3 NOVAGOLD Employee Questions & Answers 1.) What does this mean for NOVAGOLD employees? Overall, it means greater stability, clearer direction, and increased confidence that the project can continue moving forward in a coordinated way. • A single ownership structure can help provide clearer leadership and more efficient decision-making – reducing delays to keep project planning on track. • A unified structure can also make it easier to attract investment and secure the funding needed for the next phases of work – Providing future employment and workforce development opportunities. 2.) Will this affect my current role and pay? • No, we expect business as usual. • All existing employment agreements will be honored. 3.) Will we keep the Company’s Canadian Office? • Yes, it is anticipated to be business as usual. 4.) How will this affect the benefits for me and my family? • We don’t expect any changes to the benefit plans because of the transaction. 5.) Will this affect my accrued vacation? • We don’t anticipate any changes to your accrued vacation. 6.) Once New NG is formed, how can people buy/trade shares? Will we be able to continue to participate in the employee share-purchase program? • Once the transaction has closed, NovaGold Corporation will be listed on NYSE. • The Company’s symbol NG will not change. Trading shares will be the same. • Existing plans remain in place. 7.) What will be tax implications on share ownership for Canadian employees? • The transaction is expected to be a taxable disposition for NOVAGOLD shareholders for Canadian income tax purposes. • The transaction is not expected to be a taxable event for Canadian tax purposes with respect to existing stock options, performance share units or other equity awards. • Existing equity awards will remain outstanding but will become awards to acquire, or receive the value of, NovaGold Corporation shares.
