Item 7.01 Regulation FD

 

On June 28, 2021, we issued a press release addressing an appeal filed in Alaska Superior Court by Earthjustice of the decision by the Commissioner of the Alaska Department of Environmental Conservation (ADEC) to uphold the state’s Clean Water Act 401 Certification (the “401 Certification”). Earthjustice filed the appeal on behalf of Orutsararmiut Native Council (ONC) on June 28, 2021. The 401 Certification was originally granted for the Donlin Gold project on August 10, 2018. A copy of the press release is furnished as Exhibit 99.1 to this report and incorporated herein by reference.

 

The information contained in the press release attached hereto is being furnished and shall not be deemed filed for purposes of Section 18 of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as amended (the “Exchange Act”), or otherwise subject to the liability of that section, and shall not be incorporated by reference into any registration statement or other document filed under the Securities Act of 1933, as amended, or the Exchange Act, except as shall be expressly set forth by specific reference in such filing.
