# FICO KPI plan

**As of:** 2026-10-06
**Question this plan answers:** Is the mortgage-score toll losing price, losing volume, or both, and are the three bureaus steering lenders toward the score they own?
**Stance today:** watch. Full note: `research/deep_dive_2026-09-29.md`.
**Third party:** Quality Stocks, "Stock of the Week: Fair Isaac" (2026-10-05), is **[PENDING APPROVAL]**. Their $672 base value, 11.6% a year return build, and buy zones are not inputs to `valuation.json`. The useful overlap is the question they pose: the market may be pricing lost pricing power even if FICO keeps the customers.

These rows are on the dashboard. The nightly build and the Sunday world-model job run `build_fico_watch.py`, which writes `dashboard/data/fico_watch.json` and `research/kpi_ledger.json`. Debt, cash, revenue, operating income, buybacks, and interest come from the SEC companyfacts feed. The Scores split, bureau share, margin, unit-price sentence, lenders, and the policy grid stay pinned to the last filing a person read. A newer 10-Q or 10-K turns the panel red. None of the gates go into the base return.

## What would change the watch

| Reading | Evidence | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| Price break | Mortgage origination score unit price flat or down, units stable | The growth engine in the June quarter is contested. Quality Stocks' comment is this case. |
| Isolated switch | Unit price holds, units hold, no second large lender names VantageScore as preferred | Rocket is one originator, not the industry. |
| Steering | Bureau agreements rise as a share of revenue while Scores margin falls | Experian, TransUnion, and Equifax own VantageScore and distribute FICO. |
| No offset | Software revenue still around 2% growth and Software operating income does not rise | Software cannot fill a hole in Scores. |
| Balance sheet | Net debt / EBITDA stays above 3x while the toll is contested | The debt-funded buybacks are a separate problem, not a footnote. |

## 1. Fair Isaac, every quarter

Source is the 10-Q or 10-K and the earnings release. Fiscal year ends September 30. The next print is the year just ended.

| ID | KPI | Why it is on the list | Gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| `scores_b2b_revenue` | Business-to-business Scores revenue | The toll. Fiscal 2025 was $949 million, 81% of Scores. | Direction versus the prior year, and whether management still attributes the change to mortgage unit price. |
| `scores_mortgage_unit_price` | Mortgage origination score unit price | The June quarter said the B2B increase was "primarily attributable to a higher mortgage origination scores unit price." The September 29 grid attacks that line. | Disclose the price if they give it. If they only describe it, record the sentence. A flat or lower price with stable units is a fail. |
| `scores_mortgage_units` | Mortgage origination score units | Separates a price cut from a volume loss. The filing does not isolate this today. | Record "not disclosed" until it is. Do not invent a share of profit. |
| `scores_operating_margin` | Scores operating income / Scores revenue | Fiscal 2025 was 88%. A monopoly price cut shows up here before it shows up in the story. | Hold near the last reported level. A step down is a fail. |
| `scores_b2c_revenue` | myFICO / direct-to-consumer Scores | Fiscal 2025 was $220 million. The mortgage grid does not set this price. | Context. Confirms the non-mortgage book is still there. |
| `bureau_revenue_share` | Revenue from agreements with the three bureaus | June quarter: 63% of revenue, up from 54% a year earlier, and 51% for all of fiscal 2025. | A further rise while Scores margin falls is the steering gate. |
| `software_revenue_growth` | Software revenue growth | Fiscal 2025 Software was $822 million, up 3%, and operating income fell $9.8 million. Quality Stocks uses about 2%. | If this stays low single digits, it is not an offset to Scores. |
| `software_operating_income` | Software operating income | Growth that does not drop to profit does not fill the hole. | Year-over-year change. |
| `net_debt_to_ebitda` | Net debt / EBITDA | Long-term debt was $5.28 billion at June 30, including the $1.5 billion term loan used to repurchase stock, plus $1.0 billion of 6.25% notes in March 2026. Quality Stocks puts the ratio above 3x. | Recompute from the filing each quarter. Above 3x while mortgage price is contested stays a fail. |
| `interest_expense` | Interest expense, net | Nine months to June 30: $146 million, up from $94 million. | Tracks the buyback debt in dollars. |
| `buyback_price` | Dollars spent on buybacks and the average price paid | The poor buyback is a claim until the quarterly share-repurchase table is checked against the price that quarter. | Average price paid versus the quarter's low. Buying above the post-September price is the fact to record. |
| `free_cash_flow` | Free cash flow | The September 29 base path assumes 4% growth for five years. | The path fails if cash flow steps down because mortgage fees were cut, not because originations were slow. |

Card, auto, and account-management scores stay inside B2B Scores until the company splits them. They are not a separate KPI. The deep dive already says those books are outside the mortgage grid.

## 2. The three bureaus, owners of VantageScore

VantageScore is not a public company. Equifax (EFX), TransUnion (TRU), and Experian (EXPN.L) own it and sell FICO. Track the same three rows at each.

| ID | KPI | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `mortgage_inquiries` | US mortgage credit inquiries or mortgage credit-pull volume | Shared volume. If originations are down, FICO Scores can fall without anyone switching. |
| `vantagescore_disclosure` | Any dollar figure, royalty, or volume for VantageScore or "alternative mortgage score" | A number here is the first hard evidence of share shift. Absence is itself a reading: the rival is still too small to break out. |
| `mortgage_revenue_growth` | Their mortgage, credit-marketing, or verification revenue growth | If their mortgage line jumps while FICO's mortgage unit price falls, the fee is moving to the score they own. |

## 3. Lenders, adoption rather than FICO's books

Start with the names already on the record, then the rest of the top originators by volume.

| Name | What to record |
|---|---|
| Rocket Mortgage (RKT) | Preferred score for eligible loans, the quarter it starts, and whether they repeat the claim that borrowers saved about $1,600. That savings figure is Rocket's, not a FICO filing. |
| United Wholesale Mortgage (UWMC) | Share of borrowers who score better on VantageScore. They have said about a quarter, and that it might reach 40%. Update only when they publish a new share. |
| Next originators | A count: how many of the top 10 mortgage originators have named VantageScore 4.0 as preferred or default for GSE delivery. One name is an anecdote. Three is a pattern. |

## 4. Policy and volume, shared context

These are not Fair Isaac cash. They explain the other half of the fee.

| ID | KPI | Source | Gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| `llpa_single_grid` | One loan-level price adjustment grid for Classic FICO and VantageScore 4.0, and the 20-point mark-down on VantageScore still gone | FHFA, Fannie Mae, Freddie Mac | A reversal would put mortgage exclusivity back on the table. Silence means the September 28 change still stands. |
| `manual_underwrite_rule` | Classic FICO still required when a human underwrites the loan | Same | If this requirement goes, the remaining franchise in mortgages is habit and price, not a rule. |
| `fha_10t` | FHA use of FICO 10T, described as planned for January 2027 | FHA | Intent until loans are delivered on 10T. Not revenue. |
| `mortgage_origination_volume` | US mortgage origination volume | MBA or the GSE monthly summaries | Separates a slow housing market from a lost score. |

## Cadence

- FICO rows: the next annual report, then each 10-Q.
- Bureau rows: the same week those three report.
- Lender count: when a top-10 originator speaks, not on a timer.
- Policy rows: when FHFA, Fannie Mae, Freddie Mac, or FHA publishes. Recheck on each FICO earnings date so a quiet reversal is not missed.

## What not to track

- A percent of profit "at risk" in mortgages. The filing does not isolate that book. The September deep dive keeps the guess out of the base path.
- Quality Stocks' fair value, buy zones, or 11.6% return build.
- Daily price. The drawdown is the setup, not a KPI.
