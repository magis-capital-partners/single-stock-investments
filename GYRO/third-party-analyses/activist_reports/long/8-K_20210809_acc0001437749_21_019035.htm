Item 5.07         Submissions of Matters to a Vote of Security Holders
 
On July 5, 2021, Gyrodyne, LLC (the “Company”) held its 2021 annual meeting of shareholders (the “Annual Meeting”). The shareholders considered three proposals. Of the 1,482,680 shares of the Company’s common stock outstanding and eligible to vote at the Annual Meeting, 1,192,960 shares, or 84.49% of the eligible common stock, were present either in person or represented by proxy. Set forth below are the results of the matters submitted for a vote at the Annual Meeting.
