Item 1.01    Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement
 
GSD Cortlandt, LLC, a New York limited liability company (“GSD Cortlandt”) and wholly-owned subsidiary of Gyrodyne, LLC, a New York limited liability company (the “Company”), and the Company, as guarantor, entered into a letter agreement as of October 19, 2026, effective as of October 10, 2026 (the “Extension Agreement”) with SIG CRE 2023 Venture LLC (the “Lender”), as lender, holder and servicer, with respect to that certain loan in the original principal amount of $4,950,000 (the “Loan”) originally made by Signature Bank to GSD Cortlandt and secured by a mortgage on GSD Cortlandt’s property located at 1985 Crompond Road, Cortlandt Manor, New York and supported by a limited guaranty executed by the Company.
 
The Extension Agreement extends the maturity date of the Loan for five years to October 10, 2031, resets the interest rate and monthly payments in accordance with the terms of the existing note, and provides for a principal paydown of the Loan as a condition to the extension. Under the Extension Agreement, the interest rate on the Loan reset to 7.28% per annum and GSD Cortlandt made a principal paydown of $431,411.38, following which, after giving effect to the scheduled payment of principal and interest, the outstanding principal balance of the Loan is $4,041,482.21. The Company, as guarantor, reaffirmed its obligations under its limited guaranty and environmental indemnity agreement, and GSD Cortlandt and the Company provided a customary release of claims in favor of the Lender and related parties.
 
Except as modified by the Extension Agreement, all terms of the existing loan documents remain in full force and effect.
 
The foregoing description of the Extension Agreement is qualified in its entirety by reference to the full text of the Extension Agreement, which is attached hereto as Exhibit 10.1 to this Current Report on Form 8-K and is incorporated herein by reference.
