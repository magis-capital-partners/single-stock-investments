Item 8.01.

Other Events.

On July 25, 2019, II-VI Incorporated (“II-VI”) and Finisar Corporation (“Finisar”) issued a joint press release announcing the results of the elections made by holders of Finisar’s common stock and certain restricted stock units issued by Finisar (collectively, the “Voting Securities”) regarding the form of merger consideration they wish to receive in the pending acquisition of Finisar by II-VI. II-VI and Finisar also announced that they are permitting holders of Voting Securities who made an election to withdraw some or all of the Voting Securities as to which they made an election. A copy of the joint press release is attached hereto as Exhibit 99.1 and incorporated herein by reference.
