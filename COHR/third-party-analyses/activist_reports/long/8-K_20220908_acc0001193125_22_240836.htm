Item 5.03. Amendments to Articles of Incorporation or Bylaws; Change in Fiscal Year.

II-VI Incorporated (the “Company”) filed with the Pennsylvania Department of State an amendment (the “Amendment”) to its Articles of Incorporation in order to change its name to “Coherent Corp.” effective on September 8, 2022, as previously announced. A copy of the Amendment is filed as Exhibit 3.1 to this Current Report on Form 8-K and incorporated herein by reference.

Additionally, the Company adopted Amended and Restated Bylaws effective on September 8, 2022 (the “Amended and Restated Bylaws”) in order to reflect the change in the Company’s name as set forth in the Amendment. A copy of the Amended and Restated Bylaws is filed as Exhibit 3.2 to this Current Report on Form 8-K and incorporated herein by reference.

Item 8.01. Other Events

Effective September 8, 2022, the Company’s common stock, no par value (the “Common Stock”), began trading under the ticker symbol “COHR” on the Nasdaq Global Select Market.

Also on September 8, 2022, the Company issued a press release announcing the effectiveness of the previously announced change in the Company’s name to “Coherent Corp.” and the change in the ticker symbol for the Common Stock on the Nasdaq Global Select Market to “COHR”. A copy of the press release is filed as Exhibit 99.1 to this Current Report on Form 8-K and incorporated herein by reference.
