Item 7.01 Regulation FD Disclosure.

On May 10, 2023, the Company announced the following.

In light of macro conditions and ongoing work to make the Company more efficient, the Company plans to take certain restructuring actions including site consolidation, movement, and closure, as well as the relocation of certain manufacturing facilities to increase its resiliency, and global head-count reductions.

In addition, the Company has accelerated some of the actions planned as part of its multi-year synergy, integration, and transformation efforts following the acquisition of Coherent, Inc., including site consolidations and relocations to lower cost sites. These relocations and other actions are expected to result in the Company achieving its previously announced $250 million synergy plan.

The Company has commenced a review of strategic alternatives for its Silicon Carbide Business. The strategic alternatives under consideration include a minority investment in the Silicon Carbide Business by a strategic or financial partner, joint venture, and/or the sale of the Silicon Carbide Business.

The information in this Item 7.01 of this Current Report on Form 8-K shall not be deemed “filed” for the purposes of Section 18 of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 or otherwise subject to the liabilities under that Section. Furthermore, the information in this Item 7.01 of this Current Report on Form 8-K shall not be deemed to be incorporated by reference into the filings of the Company under the Securities Act of 1933.
