Item 8.01. Other Events.

On March 8, 2021, II-VI Incorporated (“II-VI”) issued a press release announcing that it delivered a revised proposal to the board of directors of Coherent, Inc. (“Coherent”) to acquire all of the outstanding shares of Coherent common stock for per share consideration of $170.00 in cash and 1.0981 shares of II-VI common stock, and that the Coherent board has informed II-VI that it deems II-VI’s revised proposal superior to Coherent’s merger agreement with Lumentum Holdings Inc. A copy of the press release is attached hereto as Exhibit 99.1 and incorporated herein by reference.
