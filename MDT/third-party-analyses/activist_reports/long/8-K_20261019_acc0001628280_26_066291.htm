Item 5.02.Departure of Directors or Certain Officers; Election of Directors; Appointment of Certain Officers; Compensatory Arrangements of Certain Officers
On October 15, 2026, the Board of Directors (the "Board") of Medtronic plc (the “Company”) approved the appointment of Michael Marinaro, Executive Vice President and President, Medical Surgical Portfolio, Americas, and Global Commercial Operations to the role of President, Medtronic, effective October 14, 2026. In this expanded role, Mr. Marinaro will continue with his current responsibilities, in addition to exercising oversight responsibility for the Company’s Greater China operations, which will continue to be managed locally by Alex Gu, Senior Vice President and President, Greater China. Mr. Marinaro will continue to report to Geoffrey S. Martha, who will continue to serve as the Company’s Chairman of the Board of Directors and Chief Executive Officer.
In connection with Mr. Marinaro’s appointment as President, Medtronic, the Compensation and Talent Committee (the "Committee") of the Board approved changes to his compensation, including an increase in Mr. Marinaro's annual base salary to $1,000,000 for all of fiscal year 2027. Mr. Marinaro's target annual incentive opportunity under the Medtronic Incentive Plan will continue to equal 100% of his annual base salary. In addition, the Committee approved supplemental fiscal year 2027 long-term incentive awards with an aggregate target value of approximately $3.25 million, consisting of (i) a performance share unit award with a target value of $1.625 million, (ii) a stock option award with a grant date value of $975,000 and (iii) a restricted stock unit award with a value of $650,000, each to be granted on November 2, 2026 pursuant to the Company’s 2021 Medtronic plc Long-Term Incentive Plan (the "Plan"). The performance share unit award will vest on April 27, 2029 based on the Company’s fiscal year 2027 through fiscal year 2029 performance. The stock option award will vest in four equal annual installments beginning August 3, 2027, and will have a ten-year term. The restricted stock unit award will vest in full on August 3, 2029. The awards are subject to the terms and conditions of the applicable award agreements and the Plan.
Item 5.07.Submission of Matters to a Vote of Security Holders
On October 15, 2026, the Company held its Annual General Meeting in order to: (1) elect, by separate resolutions, twelve directors, each to hold office until the 2027 Annual General Meeting of the Company and until his or her successor is elected; (2) ratify, in a non-binding vote, the appointment of PricewaterhouseCoopers LLP as the Company’s independent auditor for fiscal year 2027 and authorize, in a binding vote, the Company’s Board of Directors, acting through the Audit Committee, to set the auditor’s remuneration; (3) approve, in a non-binding advisory vote, the compensation of the Company's named executive officers; (4) renew the Board’s authority to issue shares; (5) renew the Board’s authority to opt out of pre-emption rights; and (6) authorize the Company and any subsidiary of the Company to make overseas market purchases of Medtronic ordinary shares.
At the close of business on August 21, 2026, the record date for the Annual General Meeting, 1,279,980,861 Company ordinary shares, par value $0.0001 per share (“ordinary shares”), were outstanding and entitled to vote. The holders of a total of 1,116,484,432 ordinary shares were present at the Annual General Meeting, either in person or by proxy, constituting a quorum.
