Item 5.07
Submission of Matters to a Vote of Security Holders.

A special meeting of stockholders (the “Special Meeting”) of Bristol-Myers Squibb Company (“Bristol-Myers Squibb”) was held on April 12, 2019. A total of 1,230,609,196 shares of Bristol-Myers Squibb common stock, out of a total of 1,633,957,568 shares of Bristol-Myers Squibb common stock outstanding and entitled to vote as of the close of business on March 1, 2019 (the record date for the Special Meeting), and a total of 1,064 shares of Bristol-Myers Squibb convertible preferred stock, out of a total of 3,584 shares of Bristol-Myers Squibb convertible preferred stock outstanding and entitled to vote as of the close of business on March 1, 2019, were present in person or represented by proxy. A summary of the voting results for the following proposals, each of which is described in detail in the joint proxy statement/prospectus of Bristol-Myers Squibb and Celgene Corporation (“Celgene”) dated February 22, 2019, which was first mailed to Bristol-Myers Squibb’s stockholders on or about February 22, 2019, is set forth below:

Proposal 1. Approval of the Stock Issuance

Bristol-Myers Squibb’s stockholders approved the issuance of shares of Bristol-Myers Squibb common stock (the “Stock Issuance”) to stockholders of Celgene in the merger between Celgene and Burgundy Merger Sub, Inc. (“Merger Sub”), a wholly-owned subsidiary of Bristol-Myers Squibb, contemplated by the Agreement and Plan of Merger, dated as of January 2, 2019, as it may be amended from time to time, among Bristol-Myers Squibb, Merger Sub and Celgene.
