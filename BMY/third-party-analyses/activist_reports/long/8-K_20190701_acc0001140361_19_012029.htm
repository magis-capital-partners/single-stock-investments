Item 8.01
Other Events.

On July 1, 2019, Bristol-Myers Squibb Company (“Bristol-Myers Squibb”) issued a press release announcing the consummation of its sale of Bristol-Myers Squibb’s UPSA consumer health business (the “transaction”) to Taisho Pharmaceutical Holdings Co., Ltd. (“Taisho”).  Bristol-Myers Squibb and Taisho entered into related transaction agreements at the closing of the transaction, including a transitional services agreement.  As reported in Bristol-Myers Squibb’s Current Report on Form 8-K dated and filed on December 19, 2018, Bristol-Myers Squibb estimates the transaction will be approximately ($0.04) dilutive to 2019 earnings.

A copy of the press release is attached hereto as Exhibit 99.1 to this Current Report on Form 8-K and incorporated herein in its entirety.
