SECTION 8 - OTHER EVENTS

Item 8.01 Other Events

On April 4, 2019, NextEra Energy Capital Holdings, Inc. (NEECH), a wholly-owned subsidiary of NextEra Energy, Inc. (NEE), sold $1.0 billion principal amount of its 2.90% Debentures, Series due April 1, 2022, $900 million principal amount of its 3.15% Debentures, Series due April 1, 2024, $300 million principal amount of its 3.25% Debentures, Series due April 1, 2026 and $500 million principal amount of its 3.50% Debentures, Series due April 1, 2029 (collectively, the Debentures), which are guaranteed by NEE. The Debentures were registered under the Securities Act of 1933 pursuant to Registration Statement Nos. 333-226056, 333-226056-01 and 333-226056-02. This Current Report on Form 8-K is being filed to report as exhibits certain documents in connection with the sale of the Debentures.

In addition, on April 4, 2019, NEECH sold $500 million principal amount of its Series O Junior Subordinated Debentures due May 1, 2079 (Junior Subordinated Debentures). The Junior Subordinated Debentures bear interest at a rate of 5.65%, payable semi-annually, until May 1, 2029 and thereafter will bear interest at a rate equal to three-month LIBOR plus 3.156%, reset quarterly, payable on February 1, May 1, August 1 and November 1 of each year. The Junior Subordinated Debentures are guaranteed on a subordinated basis by NEE. The Junior Subordinated Debentures were registered under the Securities Act of 1933 pursuant to Registration Statement Nos. 333-226056, 333-226056-01 and 333-226056-02. This Current Report on Form 8‑K is being filed to report as exhibits certain documents in connection with the sale of the Junior Subordinated Debentures.
