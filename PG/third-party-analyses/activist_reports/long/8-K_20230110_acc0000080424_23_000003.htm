 ITEM 7.01  REGULATION FD DISCLOSURE

On January 10, 2023, The Procter & Gamble Company (the “Company”) announced that its Board of Directors declared a quarterly dividend of $0.9133 per share on the Common Stock and on the Series A and Series B ESOP Convertible Class A Preferred Stock of the Company, payable on or after February 15, 2023, to Common Stock shareholders of record at the close of business on January 20, 2023, and to Series A and Series B ESOP Convertible Class A Preferred Stock shareholders of record at the start of business on January 20, 2023.
