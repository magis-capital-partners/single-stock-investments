Item 7.01. Regulation FD Disclosure.

Today, March 25, 2020, Dollar Tree, Inc. issued a press release announcing certain developments in its response to COVID-19, including rewarding hourly store and distribution center associates with wage premiums totaling approximately $30.0 million and dedicating its stores' first operational hours each day to at-risk customers. A copy of the press release is attached to this Form 8-K as Exhibit 99.1 and is incorporated herein by this reference.

The information contained in this item is being furnished to the Securities and Exchange Commission. Such information shall not be deemed "filed" for purposes of Section 18 of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 or otherwise subject to the liabilities of that Section. The information shall not be deemed incorporated by reference into any registration statement or other document filed pursuant to the Securities Act of 1933, except as expressly set forth by specific reference in such filing.
