Item 7.01. Regulation FD Disclosure.

Dollar Tree, Inc. (the "Company") has previously announced that it was rewarding hourly-paid store and distribution associates with a $2.00 per hour premium for all hours worked during the 19-week period from March 8, 2020 through July 18, 2020 at an estimated total cost of $142.5 million. The Company will be extending the $2.00 per hour premium for hourly-paid store and distribution associates for hours worked over the one-week period ending July 25, 2020 at an estimated incremental cost of $7.5 million.
