Item 8.01. Other Events.

On September 23, 2020, Dollar Tree, Inc. (the “Company”) was authorized to resume the Company’s share repurchase program, which was temporarily suspended in March 2020 in response to the uncertainties surrounding the COVID-19 pandemic. The Company currently has $800.0 million remaining under the Board’s repurchase authorization. The shares will be purchased from time to time at prevailing market prices, through open market or privately negotiated transactions, depending upon market conditions.
