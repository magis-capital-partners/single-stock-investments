This Schedule 14A filing consists of the following communication relating to the proposed acquisition (the “Acquisition”) of NeoPhotonics Corporation (“NeoPhotonics”) by Lumentum (“Lumentum”), a Delaware corporation and Neptune Merger Sub, Inc., a Delaware corporation and a wholly-owned subsidiary of Lumentum (“Merger Sub”), pursuant to the terms of an Agreement and Plan of Merger, dated November 3, 2021, by and among NeoPhotonics, Lumentum and Merger Sub:

Letter to Suppliers of Lumentum by Alan Lowe

The communication listed above was first used or made available on November 4, 2021.

From: Alan Lowe, President and CEO

To: INSERT

Subject: Lumentum to Acquire NeoPhotonics

Dear Valued Supplier and Partner,

I am reaching out to share some exciting news about Lumentum. We announced that we have agreed to acquire NeoPhotonics Corporation.

This transaction will enable us to better serve our customers, and in turn, be an even better partner to you as we expand our opportunity in the fast-growing market for high-speed optical components used in cloud and telecom network infrastructure.

As we continue to accelerate our investments in key areas, we’re confident in our ability to utilize Lumentum’s scale, strategy and talented team to build on NeoPhotonics’ success. NeoPhotonics has a differentiated portfolio of photonic technology and products as well as an innovative R&D team. Together, we will better meet the growing need for next generation optical networking solutions, expand our photonics capabilities at a time when photonics are at the forefront of favorable long-term market trends and help our customers compete and win in their respective markets.

While we are excited about the opportunities ahead, please keep in mind that this announcement is just the first step. Until the transaction closes, which we expect to occur in the second half of calendar year 2022, Lumentum and NeoPhotonics will continue to operate as two independent companies and it remains business as usual. You can expect no changes to the way we work with you. If you have any questions or concerns, please don’t hesitate to reach out to your usual Lumentum contact.

Thank you – as always – for your continued support and partnership.

Best Regards,

Alan S. Lowe

President and CEO
