This Schedule 14A filing consists of the following communication relating to the proposed acquisition (the “Acquisition”) of NeoPhotonics Corporation (“NeoPhotonics”) by Lumentum (“Lumentum”), a Delaware corporation and Neptune Merger Sub, Inc., a Delaware corporation and a wholly-owned subsidiary of Lumentum (“Merger Sub”), pursuant to the terms of an Agreement and Plan of Merger, dated November 3, 2021, by and among NeoPhotonics, Lumentum and Merger Sub:

Letter to Employees of Lumentum by Alan Lowe

The communication listed above was first used or made available on November 4, 2021.

To: All Lumentum Employees

From: Alan Lowe, President and CEO

Subject Line: Lumentum to Acquire NeoPhotonics / Q1 FY 2022 Results

Dear Lumentum Team,

I wanted to share with you some very exciting news for our company. Just a few moments ago, we announced that Lumentum has agreed to acquire NeoPhotonics Corporation.

This transaction will allow us to be an even better partner to our customers and expands our opportunity in the fast-growing market for high-speed optical components used in cloud and telecom network infrastructure. The press release we issued can be found here https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/lumentum-to-acquire-neophotonics-to-accelerate-optical-network-speed-and-scalability-301416398.html.

Importantly, at the center of our strategy has always been a relentless focus on developing a differentiated portfolio with the most innovative products and technology in our industry – so that we can help our customers compete and win in their respective markets. The acquisition of NeoPhotonics is consistent with this strategy and will enable us to better meet the growing need for next generation optical networking solutions.

I hope you all share my enthusiasm for the exciting new opportunities ahead with NeoPhotonics and I am eager to see the creativity and innovation that the combined team will bring to the market.

Q1 Fiscal Year 2022 Results

Today’s announcement, as well as the strong financial results we reported for Q1 FY 2022, are a testament to your hard work. Thank you.

We’re pleased to have delivered another great quarter, which had financial results that exceeded our prior guidance on all metrics. Notably, we achieved a new record company gross margin at 55%.

Next Steps

As we move forward, please keep in mind that today is just the first step toward completing our transaction with NeoPhotonics, which we expect to occur in the second half of calendar year 2022.

Until then, Lumentum and NeoPhotonics will continue to operate as two independent companies, and it remains business as usual. While we have a dedicated team working on closing the transaction, the best thing the rest of us can do is stay focused on our day-to-day responsibilities and serving our customers.

To share more details on today’s news, we will be hosting an all-hands for NA / APAC employees at 5:00 pm PT today and for NA / Europe employees at 8:00 am PT tomorrow. In addition to discussing this exciting transaction, we will also walk through our quarterly results and recent business updates. In the meantime, we have prepared the attached FAQ to address some of your immediate questions.

Thank you for your dedication to Lumentum and unwavering commitment to meeting our customers’ evolving needs. Let’s keep up the great work. I look forward to sharing more with you soon.

Best Regards,

Alan
