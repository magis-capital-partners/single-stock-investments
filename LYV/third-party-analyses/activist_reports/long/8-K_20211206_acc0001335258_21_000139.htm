Item 8.01    Other Events.

On December 6, 2021, Live Nation Entertainment, Inc. (the “Company”) issued a press release announcing the closing of its previously-announced acquisition of an aggregate of 51% of the capital stock of OCESA Entretenimiento, S.A. de C.V. In connection therewith, the arbitration proceedings related to the transaction before the International Court of Arbitration of the International Chamber of Commerce were dismissed. A copy of the press release issued by the Company is filed as Exhibit 99.1 to this Current Report on Form 8-K and is incorporated into this Item 8.01 by reference.
