A Special Meeting of Shareholders of the Invesco QQQ TrustSM, Series 1 (the “Trust” or “QQQ”) is scheduled to be held on October 24, 2025.

VOTE TO LOWER YOUR FEES!

As a shareholder of record, you are being asked to elect 9 Trustees to serve on a newly established Board of Trustees of the Trust and to approve an investment advisory agreement between the Trust and Invesco Capital Management LLC (“Invesco”).
