Item 5.07.    Submission of Matters to a Vote of Security Holders.
Post Holdings, Inc. (the “Company”) held its 2022 annual meeting of shareholders (the “Annual Meeting”) entirely virtually, conducted via a live audio-only webcast on Thursday, January 27, 2022. At the Annual Meeting, of the 62,563,659 shares outstanding and entitled to vote, 57,460,512 shares were represented, constituting a 91.84% quorum. The final results for each of the matters submitted to a vote of shareholders at the Annual Meeting are as follows:
