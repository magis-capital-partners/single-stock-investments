Item 7.01. Regulation FD Disclosure.
On February 8, 2023, Post Holdings, Inc. (“Post”) issued a press release announcing the execution of an asset purchase agreement with The J. M. Smucker Company (“Smucker”) pursuant to which Post agreed to acquire from Smucker certain assets related to Smucker’s pet food business. In addition, Post will host a conference call on February 8, 2023 at 5:00 p.m. Eastern Time to discuss the acquisition and to respond to questions. The press release is attached hereto as Exhibit 99.1 and the investor presentation is attached hereto as Exhibit 99.2, and each is incorporated herein by reference.
The information contained in this Item 7.01 and Exhibit 99.1 and Exhibit 99.2 attached hereto shall not be deemed “filed” for purposes of Section 18 of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as amended (the “Exchange Act”), or otherwise subject to the liabilities of that Section, nor shall it be deemed incorporated by reference in any filing under the Securities Act of 1933, as amended, or the Exchange Act, except as expressly set forth by specific reference in such filing.
