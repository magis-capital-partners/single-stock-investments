Item 2.02.    Results of Operations and Financial Condition.

("Post" or the "Company") issued a press release announcing certain preliminary results for its fourth fiscal quarter ended September 30, 2023, as well as its preliminary outlook for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2024. Item 5.02.    Departure of Directors or Certain Officers; Election of Directors; Appointment of Certain Officers; Compensatory Arrangements of Certain Officers.
On November 6, 2023, Post announced that Robert V. Vitale, President and Chief Executive Officer of the Company, is taking a medical leave of absence. In connection with Mr. Vitale's leave of absence, on November 2, 2023, the Company's Board of Directors appointed Jeff A. Zadoks, Executive Vice President and Chief Operating Officer, as interim President and Chief Executive Officer. Mr. Zadoks has served as the Company's Executive Vice President since 2017 and as the Company's Chief Operating Officer since December 2022. Prior to this, he served as the Company's Chief Financial Officer starting in 2014. He joined the Company in 2011 as Corporate Controller.
A copy of the press release announcing these matters is attached hereto as Exhibit 99.2 and is incorporated herein by reference.
