Item 8.01. Other Events.

 

The information provided pursuant to this Item 8.01 is to be considered “filed” under the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 (“Exchange Act”) and incorporated by reference into those filings of Rollins, Inc. (the “Company”) that provide for the incorporation of all reports and documents filed by the Company under the Exchange Act.

 

On April 3, 2019, Rollins, Inc. (NYSE: ROL), a premier global consumer and commercial services company, today announced that it will release its unaudited first quarter financial results for the period ended March 31, 2019 on Wednesday, April 24, 2019. In conjunction with its release, the Company will host a conference call to review the Company’s financial and operating results on Wednesday, April 24, 2019 at 10:00 a.m. Eastern Time.
