SUPPLEMENT TO PROXY STATEMENT

RELATING TO 2022 ANNUAL MEETING OF SHAREHOLDERS

This supplement to the proxy statement should be read together with the proxy statement (the “Proxy Statement”) of Expeditors International of Washington, Inc. (the “Company”), filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission on March 22, 2022 in connection with the Company’s 2022 Annual Meeting of Shareholders to be held at the Company’s corporate office located at 1015 Third Avenue, Seattle, WA 98104 on Tuesday, May 3, 2022 at 8:00 a.m. (Pacific time).

The purpose of this filing is to update information contained in the Proxy Statement relating to Shareholder Proposal No 4: Political Spending Disclosure. Shareholder Proposal No. 4 was submitted by John Chevedden, 2215 Nelson Ave., No. 205, Redondo Beach, Calif. 90278, who has stated that he owns 50 shares.
