## What this business is

Ecora Royalties PLC owns royalties and metal streams, mostly on other companies' mines. **[Fact]** For 2025, royalty and metal stream revenue was $55.9 million and portfolio contribution was $57.0 million. **[Fact]** Base metals contributed $28.5 million. Kestrel steelmaking coal contributed $17.5 million, down from $41.4 million, and the company says that coal contribution is expected to end in 2030. **[Fact]** Company-defined free cash flow was $27.4 million. Net debt was $85.5 million. This is a royalty company with a shrinking coal royalty and a larger base-metals book.

## Why the market might be wrong

The listed price is in pence. The cash flow is in dollars. Mixing them makes the multiple meaningless. **[Fact]** The London price was 151.2 pence on 27 October 2026. At 1.3327 dollars per pound, that is $2.015. Company free cash flow per weighted average share is about $0.11. **[Inference]** A buyer who uses the pence print as if it were dollars will think the shares are wildly expensive or wildly cheap, and both readings are wrong. The real argument is whether base metals replace Kestrel. The base case does not forecast that replacement. It holds cash flat.

## Business & moat

Portfolio contribution in 2025 was $57.0 million, versus $63.2 million in 2024. **[Fact]** Base metals were $28.5 million, up from $11.4 million. Kestrel was $17.5 million on 2.2 million tonnes, versus 2.1 million tonnes the year before, at a realized price of $143 a tonne versus $223. **[Fact]** The 2026 royalty-area guide is 1.0 to 1.2 million tonnes, mostly in the second half. Profit attributable to equity holders was $22.183 million, against a loss of $9.827 million in 2024. That swing includes a Voisey's Bay impairment reversal and a Kestrel revaluation loss of $27.106 million. **[Fact]** Operating profit before those items is not the same thing as the $22.2 million.

Company free cash flow is defined in the release. Start with net cash from operating activities of $35.105 million. Subtract finance costs of $9.9 million. Add Denison principal repayments of $2.5 million and finance income of $0.3 million. Subtract lease payments of $0.6 million. The release states the result as $27.4 million. **[Fact]** Interest is already out. Net debt of $85.5 million, and total borrowings of $93.3 million, are not deducted a second time. Leverage was 2.0 times, against a 3.5 times cap. The dividend for the year is 2.00 US cents, final 1.40 cents subject to the annual meeting. Dividends paid were $6.9 million.

### Mental models

A royalty book is a stack of tolls with different end dates. Kestrel is a toll with a published end. Base metals are the replacement toll. The accounts already charge interest. Adding the debt balance on top of an after-interest cash figure would punish the equity twice.

### Business mechanics

Operators mine. Ecora invoices a royalty or takes metal under a stream and pays a contractual cost. Overhead was about $12.2 million. The company also buys and sells royalties. In 2025 it spent $50 million on the Mimbula copper stream and received proceeds from the Dugbe sale. Those are investing choices. They are not inside the $27.4 million figure as growth capex, and they are not a forecast.

### Moat

The contracts last longer than a single coal price year. **[Inference]** Kestrel's end date means part of the book is finite. Narrow fits a contract file that is real and partly expiring. That is a judgment.

## Risks & inversion

**Primary risk:** Kestrel cash falls on the way to 2030 and base metals do not replace it, while borrowings stay near $93 million.

- The 2026 Kestrel volume guide is about half of 2025 tonnes, and it is weighted to the second half.
- Weighted average shares fell from 252.398 million to 248.944 million. The ending count is missing. If it is higher, cash per share is lower.
- Currency: the model price is the London line converted at the spot pound rate. The Toronto line and the OTC line are different prints and are not used.

**Lens failure mode:** Valuing the pence price against dollar cash, or treating Kestrel as a perpetual royalty.

**Upside / downside from price:** Upside is base metals growing enough to lift the $27.4 million after Kestrel fades. Downside is a coal cliff with the debt still there.
