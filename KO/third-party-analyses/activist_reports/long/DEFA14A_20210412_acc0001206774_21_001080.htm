Additional Procedures for Beneficial Owners to Participate in the Virtual 2021 Annual Meeting of Shareowners

At the 2021 Annual Meeting of Shareowners, in addition to the procedures established for beneficial owners to vote electronically at the meeting by registering in advance, the virtual meeting platform for this year contains additional functionality that will permit most beneficial owners to attend, vote and submit questions during the meeting by using the control number located in the voting instruction form or email received from the bank, broker or other nominee through which they own their shares of Common Stock. However, we continue to urge beneficial owners to consider using the preregistration process outlined in the 2021 Proxy Statement to participate in the meeting.
