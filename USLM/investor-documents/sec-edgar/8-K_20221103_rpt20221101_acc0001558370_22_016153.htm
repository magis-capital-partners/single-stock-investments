Item 5.03.  AMENDMENTS TO ARTICLES OF INCORPORATION OR BYLAWS; CHANGE IN FISCAL YEAR.

​

On October 28, 2022, Ray M. Harlin, a valued director of United States Lime & Minerals, Inc. (the “Company”), passed away. Mr. Harlin served as a director of the Company since 2018.

​

On November 1, 2022, the Company Board of Directors (the “Board”) amended Section 2 of Article Three of the Company’s Bylaws to decrease the size of the Board from seven to six directors.
