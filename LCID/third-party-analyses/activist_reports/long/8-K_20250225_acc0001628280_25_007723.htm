Item 1.01 Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement.
2025 GIB Credit Facility
On February 24, 2025, Lucid LLC, a limited liability company established in Saudi Arabia and a subsidiary of Lucid Group, Inc. (“Lucid” or the “Company”), entered into an agreement to renew the 2023 GIB Credit Facility with Gulf International Bank Saudi Arabia (“GIB”) (the “2025 GIB Credit Facility”) maturing on February 24, 2028 to increase the credit facility committed amount from SAR 1.0 billion (approximately $266.1 million) to SAR 1.9 billion (approximately $506.7 million). GIB is a related party of the Public Investment Fund, which is an affiliate of Ayar Third Investment Company, the controlling stockholder of the Company. Loans under the 2025 GIB Credit Facility may be used for general corporate purposes, have a maturity of no more than 12 months, and bear interest at a rate of 1.40% per annum over SAIBOR (based on the term of borrowing) and associated fees. The Company is required to pay a quarterly commitment fee of 0.25% per annum based on the unutilized portion of the 2025 GIB Credit Facility.
The foregoing description of the terms of the 2025 GIB Credit Facility does not purport to be complete and is qualified in its entirety by reference to the full text of the 2025 GIB Credit Facility agreement, which will be filed as an exhibit to the Company’s Quarterly Report on Form 10-Q for the fiscal quarter ending March 31, 2025.
