Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.

 

The Company intends to file a preliminary prospectus supplement with the Securities and Exchange Commission (the “Preliminary Prospectus Supplement”) on October 16, 2024 in connection with a proposed underwritten public offering (the “Offering”) of its Class A common stock, par value $0.0001 per share (the “Common Stock”). The Preliminary Prospectus Supplement is expected to include the following disclosure under the heading “Recent Developments”:

 

“Preliminary Financial Results and Operational Information
