ITEM 1.

SECURITY AND ISSUER

This Amendment No. 1 (this “Amendment”) amends and supplements the Schedule 13D filed by the Reporting Persons on August 9, 2021 (the “Schedule 13D”) relating to the shares of common stock, $0.0001 par value (“Common Stock”), of Hippo Holdings, Inc. (the “Issuer”). The address of the principal executive offices of the Issuer is 191 Castro Street, Mountain View CA 94041.

 

ITEM 3.

SOURCE AND AMOUNT OF FUNDS OR OTHER CONSIDERATION

Item 3 of the Schedule 13D is hereby amended to include the following:

On April 8, 2022, pursuant to a distribution in-kind effected by Fifth Wall Ventures SPV IV L.P., LEN FW Investor, LLC (“Len FW”) acquired 22,126,674 shares of Common Stock for no consideration.

 

ITEM 5.

INTEREST IN SECURITIES OF THE ISSUER

Item 5(a) of the Schedule 13D is hereby amended and restated as follows:

(a) As of the date of this Amendment, one of the Reporting Persons owns 51,465,797 shares of the Issuer’s Common Stock, constituting approximately 9.1% of the Issuer’s outstanding Common Stock. In addition, that Reporting Person holds an irrevocable proxy to vote 25,723,624 shares of the Issuer’s Common Stock. Those shares, together with the shares owned by the Reporting Person, constitute 13.6% of the outstanding Common Stock. However, because that Reporting Person has agreed not to participate in a vote or consent, or make a decision, with regard to more than 9.99% of the voting interests in the Issuer, the Reporting Persons deny that any of them is the beneficial owner of shares that are subject to the irrevocable proxy to the extent they would increase a Reporting Person’s voting interest above 9.99%.

This percentage is calculated based on 566,266,370 outstanding shares of common stock of Hippo Holdings Inc., as set forth in the Issuer’s Annual Report on Form 10-K filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission on March 14, 2022.
