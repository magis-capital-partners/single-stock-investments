Item 1.01.

Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement.

Revolving Credit Facility

On October 26, 2026, Lennar Corporation (the “Company”) amended and restated its ninth amended and restated credit agreement, dated as of November 25, 2024, governing the Company’s unsecured revolving credit facility (the “Revolving Credit Facility”) by entering into a tenth amended and restated credit agreement (the “Revolving Credit Agreement”) to, among other things, increase the lenders’ aggregate commitments to $3.795 billion, extend the maturity of the Class A lenders’ commitments, totaling $3.545 billion, from November 2029 to November 2031 and remove the 0.10% credit spread adjustment previously applicable to loans accruing interest by reference to the Secured Overnight Financing Rate (“SOFR”). The Revolving Credit Facility also includes Class B commitments of $225 million maturing in May 2027 and a new Class C commitment of $25 million maturing in November 2029. The Revolving Credit Facility also introduced the ability for the Class A loans accruing interest by reference to SOFR to have a 0.10% applicable margin reduction, and a 0.025% commitment fee reduction, if certain rating conditions with respect to the Company are satisfied. The Revolving Credit Facility has a $750 million accordion feature, subject to additional commitments. As a result, aggregate potential borrowing capacity under the Revolving Credit Facility increased from $3.500 billion to $4.545 billion.

The Revolving Credit Agreement is among the Company, as borrower, JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A., BofA Securities, Inc. and Mizuho Bank, Ltd., as joint bookrunners, the several lenders from time to time parties thereto and JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A., as issuing lender and administrative agent. The Revolving Credit Facility consists of revolving loans under which amounts may be borrowed, repaid and redrawn. The proceeds available under the Revolving Credit Facility may be used for working capital and general corporate purposes, including acquisitions.

In connection with the Revolving Credit Agreement, certain of the Company’s wholly owned subsidiaries, which are primarily homebuilding subsidiaries, entered into a tenth amended and restated guarantee agreement (the “Revolving Credit Guarantee Agreement”), dated as of October 26, 2026, pursuant to which those subsidiaries guarantee the Company’s obligations under the Revolving Credit Agreement.

The foregoing descriptions of the Revolving Credit Agreement and the Revolving Credit Guarantee Agreement do not purport to be complete and are qualified in their entirety by reference to the full terms of those agreements, copies of which are filed as Exhibits 10.1 and 10.2, respectively, to this Current Report on Form 8-K and incorporated by reference herein.

Term Loan Facility

On October 26, 2026, the Company amended and restated the delayed draw term loan credit agreement, dated as of May 14, 2025, governing its unsecured delayed draw term loan credit facility (the “Term Loan Facility”) by entering into an amended and restated delayed draw term loan credit agreement (the “Term Loan Credit Agreement”), pursuant to which the lenders extended to the Company a $40 million additional delayed draw term loan from the Term Loan Facility’s accordion feature, thus increasing the aggregate outstanding amount of delayed draw term loans under the Term Loan Facility from $1.71 billion to $1.75 billion and reducing the available amount to be drawn under the accordion feature from $400 million to $360 million. The term loans mature in May 2028, subject to a one-time option of the Company to extend the maturity to May 2029, subject to certain conditions. The Term Loan Credit Agreement also removed the 0.10% credit spread adjustment previously applicable to loans accruing interest by reference to SOFR and introduced the ability for the loans accruing interest by reference to SOFR to have a 0.10% applicable margin reduction if certain rating conditions with respect to the Company are satisfied.

The Term Loan Credit Agreement is among the Company, as borrower, Truist Securities, Inc., JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A., Banco Bilbao Vizcaya Argentaria, S.A. New York Branch, BofA Securities, Inc., Goldman Sachs Bank USA and Wells Fargo Securities, LLC, as joint lead arrangers and joint bookrunners, the several lenders from time to time parties thereto and JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A., as administrative agent. The Term Loan Facility consists of term loans. Once borrowed, amounts cannot be redrawn even if repaid. The proceeds available under the Term Loan Facility may be used for working capital and general corporate purposes, including acquisitions.
