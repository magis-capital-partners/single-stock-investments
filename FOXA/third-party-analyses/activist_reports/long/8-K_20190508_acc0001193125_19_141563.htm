Item 2.02.  Results of Operations and Financial Condition.

On May 8, 2019, Fox Corporation (the “Company”) released its financial results for the quarter ended March 31, 2019. A copy of the Company’s press release is attached as Exhibit 99.1 to this Current Report on Form 8-K and incorporated herein by reference.

The information in this report shall not be deemed “filed” for purposes of Section 18 of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as amended (the “Exchange Act”), or incorporated by reference in any filing under the Securities Act of 1933, as amended, or the Exchange Act, except as shall be expressly set forth by specific reference in such filing.

Item 8.01.  Other Events.

On May 8, 2019, FOX Sports, a unit of the Company, and The Stars Group Inc. (“The Stars Group”) announced plans to launch FOX Bet, a national media and sports wagering partnership in the United States, and entered into a long-term commercial agreement through which FOX Sports will provide The Stars Group with an exclusive license to use certain FOX Sports trademarks. The Company also agreed to acquire 4.99% of The Stars Group’s issued and outstanding common shares for $236 million.

A copy of the press release issued by FOX Sports and The Stars Group is attached hereto as Exhibit 99.2 and incorporated herein by reference.
