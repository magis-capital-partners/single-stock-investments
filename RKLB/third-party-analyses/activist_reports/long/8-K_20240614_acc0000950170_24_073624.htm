Item 5.07 Submission of Matters to a Vote of Security Holders.

(a)-(b) On June 12, 2024, Rocket Lab USA, Inc. (the “Company”) held its 2024 Annual Meeting of Stockholders (the “Meeting”). As of the record date of April 16, 2024 (the “record date”), there were 492,683,716 shares of the Company’s common stock, $0.0001 par value (“Common Stock”), outstanding (each entitled to one vote per share). Of the total 492,683,716 shares of the Company’s Common Stock outstanding as of the record date, 352,859,013 shares were represented at the Meeting. The Company’s stockholders considered three proposals at the Meeting, each of which is described in more detail in the Company’s definitive proxy statement filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission on April 24, 2024. The number of votes cast for and against (or withheld) and the number of abstentions and broker non-votes with respect to each proposal voted upon are set forth below.
