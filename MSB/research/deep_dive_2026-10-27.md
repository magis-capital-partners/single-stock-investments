## Business & moat

The trust is paid under the Peters and Cloquet lease assignments when Northshore ships iron-ore pellets from the trust lands. Base overriding royalties are a percentage of gross proceeds. The percentage steps up with annual tons. **[Fact]** Bonus royalties apply only when the pellet price clears an adjusted threshold. In the quarter ended 31 July 2026 that bonus was zero. Fee royalties were $232,146. Production and shipments from trust lands were 975,722 tons in the quarter and 1,881,853 tons in the six months, and 100% of those shipments originated on trust lands.

The customer is one company. Northshore operates the mine. Cleveland-Cliffs owns Northshore. **[Fact]** Since Cliffs bought ArcelorMittal USA, more pellets have gone to Cliffs affiliates. The royalty agreement uses a highest-contract-price rule for those sales. The trustees and the operator are in arbitration over the 2022-2023 idle period and over intercompany royalties from 2023 onward. The 10-Q says the case is proceeding on the tribunal's schedule. This pass does not estimate the award.

The 31 July 2026 balance sheet lists liabilities of $2,252,687. That is a distribution payable of $656,001 and accrued expenses of $1,596,686. **[Fact]** There is no borrowings line. Total assets were $19,843,209. Unallocated reserve was $17,590,519 at 31 July 2026, down from $22,781,200 a year earlier.

### Mental models

A trust with one lessee is a pass-through of that lessee's price and volume, minus the trust's own expenses. Legal fees rose in the quarter. Net loss for the three months was $95,013. **[Fact]** Inversion: if the bonus stays off and the trustees keep the distribution at zero, the unit is a claim on a suspended coupon plus an unresolved arbitration, not a yield security.

### Business mechanics

Northshore ships pellets. The trust records base, bonus, and fee royalties. The trustees decide the distribution from the reserve. They can pay a small distribution, as in August, and then pay none, as announced in October. The trust does not operate the mine and does not borrow against it.

### Moat
