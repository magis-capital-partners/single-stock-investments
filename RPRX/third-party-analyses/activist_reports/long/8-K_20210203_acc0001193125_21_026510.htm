Item 8.01.

Other Events.

Pursuant to the 2017 agreement with Perrigo Company plc, Royalty Pharma plc was required to pay Perrigo a contingent milestone payment of $400.0 million if payments from Biogen for Tysabri® in respect of 2020 exceeded $351.0 million. On February 3, 2021, Royalty Pharma announced that Biogen has informed Royalty Pharma that payments for Tysabri® in respect of 2020 will be less than $351 million. Accordingly, Royalty Pharma is not required to pay the contingent milestone.
