Item 1.01

Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement.

On August 12, 2021, Franklin Resources, Inc. (the “Company”) completed its offering and sale of $350 million aggregate principal amount of its 2.950% Notes due 2051 (the “2051 Notes”) and an additional $100 million aggregate principal amount of its 1.600% Notes due 2030 (the “Additional 2030 Notes” and, together with the 2051 Notes, the “Notes”).

The Notes were issued by the Company on August 12, 2021 under an Indenture, dated as of October 6, 2020 (the “Base Indenture”), between the Company and The Bank of New York Mellon Trust Company, N.A., as Trustee, as supplemented by the Officer’s Certificate, dated August 12, 2021, regarding the issuance of the Additional 2030 Notes and establishing the terms of the 2051 Notes (the “2021 Officer’s Certificate”). The Base Indenture, as supplemented by the 2021 Officer’s Certificate and that certain Officer’s Certificate establishing the terms of the 1.600% Notes due 2030, dated October 19, 2020 (the “2020 Officer’s Certificate”), is referred to as the “Indenture.”

The Additional 2030 Notes will have the same CUSIP number and constitute a single series under the Indenture with the $750 million principal amount of the Company’s 1.600% Notes due 2030 originally issued on October 19, 2020 (the “Original 2030 Notes”, and together with the Additional 2030 Notes, the “2030 Notes”). Interest on the Additional 2030 Notes will be payable semi-annually on April 30 and October 30 of each year, beginning on October 30, 2021. The interest payment to be made on the Additional 2030 Notes on October 30, 2021 will include pre-issuance accrued interest from and including April 30, 2021 to, but excluding, the issue date. The 2030 Notes will mature on October 30, 2030. The 2030 Notes are unsecured and unsubordinated obligations of the Company and rank equal in right of payment with all other unsecured and unsubordinated indebtedness of the Company from time to time outstanding. The 2030 Notes are structurally subordinated to all existing and future indebtedness of the Company’s subsidiaries.

Interest on the 2051 Notes will be payable semi-annually on February 12 and August 12 of each year, beginning on February 12, 2022. The 2051 Notes will mature on August 12, 2051. The 2051 Notes are unsecured and unsubordinated obligations of the Company and rank equal in right of payment with all other unsecured and unsubordinated indebtedness of the Company from time to time outstanding. The 2051 Notes are structurally subordinated to all existing and future indebtedness of the Company’s subsidiaries.

The 2030 Notes may be redeemed prior to July 30, 2030 (the date that is three months prior to the maturity date) in whole or in part at any time, at the Company’s option, at a “make-whole” redemption price, plus accrued and unpaid interest on the 2030 Notes to be redeemed to, but not including, the redemption date. The 2030 Notes may be redeemed on or after July 30, 2030 in whole or in part at any time, at the Company’s option, at a redemption price equal to 100% of the principal amount of the 2030 Notes to be redeemed, plus accrued and unpaid interest on the 2030 Notes to be redeemed to, but not including, the redemption date.

The 2051 Notes may be redeemed prior to February 12, 2051 (the date that is six months prior to the maturity date) in whole or in part at any time, at the Company’s option, at a “make-whole” redemption price, plus accrued and unpaid interest on the 2051 Notes to be redeemed to, but not including, the redemption date. The 2051 Notes may be redeemed on or after February 12, 2051 in whole or in part at any time, at the Company’s option, at a redemption price equal to 100% of the principal amount of the 2051 Notes to be redeemed, plus accrued and unpaid interest on the 2051 Notes to be redeemed to, but not including, the redemption date.
