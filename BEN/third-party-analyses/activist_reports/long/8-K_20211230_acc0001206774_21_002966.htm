Item 7.01 Regulation FD Disclosure.

On December 24, 2021, Franklin Resources, Inc. (the "Company") entered into a share repurchase agreement under Rule 10b5-1 of the Securities and Exchange Act of 1934 (the “Exchange Act”), with Morgan Stanley & Co. effective January 3, 2022.

Under the share repurchase agreement, the number of shares to be repurchased will depend on the prices at which Franklin Resources, Inc.’s common stock is repurchased and is expected to be completed by June 30, 2022. The share repurchases made under the share repurchase agreement will be consistent with the Company’s previously disclosed approach to share repurchases and will be completed under the company’s current share repurchase authorization, which currently has 30,867,872 in remaining authorized shares.
