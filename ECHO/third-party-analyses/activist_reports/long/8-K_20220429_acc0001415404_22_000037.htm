Item 5.07.  Submission of Matters to a Vote of Security Holders.

On April 28, 2022, EchoStar Corporation (“EchoStar”) held its 2022 Annual Meeting of Shareholders (the “2022 Annual Meeting”).

The following matters were voted upon at the 2022 Annual Meeting:

1.Election of R. Stanton Dodge, Michael T. Dugan, Charles W. Ergen, Lisa W. Hershman, Pradman P. Kaul, C. Michael Schroeder, Jeffrey R. Tarr and William D. Wade as directors to serve until the 2023 Annual Meeting of Shareholders or until their respective successors shall be duly elected and qualified; and

2.Ratification of the appointment of KPMG LLP as EchoStar’s independent registered public accounting firm for the fiscal year ending December 31, 2022.
