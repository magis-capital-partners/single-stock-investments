Item 5.07.  Submission of Matters to a Vote of Security Holders.

On April 30, 2020, EchoStar Corporation (“EchoStar”) held its 2020 Annual Meeting of Shareholders.

The following matters were voted upon:

1.
Election of R. Stanton Dodge, Michael T. Dugan, Charles W. Ergen, Anthony M. Federico, Pradman P. Kaul, C. Michael Schroeder, Jeffrey R. Tarr and William David Wade as directors to serve until the 2021 Annual Meeting of Shareholders or until their respective successors shall be duly elected and qualified;

2.
Ratification of the appointment of KPMG LLP as EchoStar independent registered public accounting firm for the fiscal year ending December 31, 2020; and

3.
Non-binding advisory vote on the compensation of EchoStar’s named executive officers.
