This filing relates solely to preliminary communications made before the commencement of a tender offer by Pacific Merger Sub, Inc., a Delaware corporation (“Purchaser”) and a wholly owned subsidiary of Gilead Sciences, Inc., a Delaware corporation (“Parent”), to acquire all of the outstanding shares of common stock of CymaBay Therapeutics, Inc., a Delaware corporation (the “Company”), at a price of $32.50 per share, net to the seller in cash, without interest and subject to any withholding of taxes, pursuant to an Agreement and Plan of Merger, dated February 11, 2024, among the Company, Parent and Purchaser.
