Item 8.01

Other Events.

On November 27, 2022 (Hong Kong time), MGM China Holdings Limited (“MGM China”), an indirect subsidiary of MGM Resorts International (the “Company”) with its ordinary shares of common stock listed on The Stock Exchange of Hong Kong Limited (the “HKSE”), filed with the HKSE an announcement (the “Announcement”) that on November 26, 2022 (Hong Kong time), MGM Grand Paradise Limited, a non-wholly owned subsidiary of MGM China, received notification from the Macau Government that it has been granted a provisional award of a new 10-year gaming concession contract to operate games of chance or other games in casinos in Macau commencing on January 1, 2023. A copy of the Announcement is filed herewith as Exhibit 99.1.
