ITEM 7.01.REGULATION FD DISCLOSURE.
On December 14, 2021, American Airlines Group Inc. (“AAG”) issued a press release relating to the members of the senior leadership team in connection with the previously announced transition plan to be effective March 31, 2022. The press release is furnished herewith as Exhibit 99.1 and is incorporated by reference into this Item 7.01.
In addition, on December 14, 2021, AAG issued a press release announcing the retirement of Elise Eberwein, Executive Vice President of People and Global Engagement, effective June 30, 2022. The press release is furnished herewith as Exhibit 99.2 and is incorporated by reference into this Item 7.01.
