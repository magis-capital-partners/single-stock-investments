Item 1.01

Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement.

As previously disclosed, in connection with the transactions contemplated by that certain Agreement and Plan of Merger, dated as of November 2, 2022 (the “Merger Agreement”), among Flame Acquisition Corp. (“Flame”), Sable Offshore Holdings LLC (“Holdco”), and Sable Offshore Corp. (“SOC” and, together with Holdco, “Sable”), Holdco entered into subscription agreements (the “Initial Sable PIPE Subscription Agreements”) with certain investors (such investors, the “Initial Sable PIPE Investors”), pursuant to which the Initial Sable PIPE Investors agreed to purchase, in the aggregate, 7,450,000 limited liability company membership interests in Holdco designated as Class B shares (“Holdco Class B shares”) at a price of $10.00 per share, for an aggregate commitment amount of approximately $74,500,000 (the “Initial Sable PIPE Investment”).

On December 12, 2023, Holdco entered into subscription agreements (the “Additional Sable PIPE Subscription Agreements” and, together with the Initial Sable PIPE Subscription Agreements, the “Sable PIPE Subscription Agreements”) with certain investors (such investors, the “Additional Sable PIPE Investors” and, together with the Initial Sable PIPE Investors, the “Sable PIPE Investors”), pursuant to which the Additional Sable PIPE Investors agreed to purchase, in the aggregate, 18,045,000 Holdco Class B shares at a price of $10.00 per share, for an aggregate commitment amount of approximately $180,450,000 (the “Additional Sable PIPE Investment” and, together with the Initial Sable PIPE Investment, the “Sable PIPE Investment”).

The Sable PIPE Subscription Agreements provide, among other things, that, in the event the mergers contemplated by the Merger Agreement are consummated, the Sable PIPE Investors will be deemed to have subscribed for and will purchase Flame Class A common stock at the same price per share and, by operation of law pursuant to the mergers contemplated by the Merger Agreement, Flame will have succeeded to Holdco’s obligations under the Sable PIPE Subscription Agreements. The Sable PIPE Subscription Agreement with Metamorphic Pecan, LLC (“Metamorphic”) provides that, for so long as Metamorphic continues to own not less than 10% of the total issued and outstanding Flame Class A shares, Metamorphic will be entitled to designate one Class II director nominee to Flame’s board of directors, and, following the consummation of the mergers, Flame will take all necessary action to include such director nominee in the slate of nominees recommended by Flame’s board of directors (or authorized committee thereof) to Flame’s stockholders for election as a director at each annual meeting of Flame’s stockholders during which Class II directors are being nominated. None of the other Sable PIPE Subscription Agreements contain a similar provision entitling any Sable PIPE Investors to designate any director nominee to Flame’s board of directors.

Holdco intends to pursue additional commitments to purchase Holdco Class B shares under substantially similar subscription agreements prior to the Closing, provided that such additional commitments, together with the Sable PIPE Investment, will not exceed $525,000,000.

A copy of the form Additional Sable PIPE Subscription Agreement is filed with this Current Report on Form 8-K as Exhibit 10.1 and is incorporated herein by reference. The foregoing description of the Additional Sable PIPE Subscription Agreements, including the Sable PIPE Subscription Agreement with Metamorphic, does not purport to be complete and is qualified in its entirety by reference to the full text of the form Additional Sable PIPE Subscription Agreement filed with this report.
