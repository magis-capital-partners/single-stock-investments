## What this business is
CRH plc is a global building materials company (aggregates, cement, asphalt, and building products) listed on the NYSE with an Irish parent and primary U.S. operating footprint. **[Fact]** FY2025 consolidated revenue was **$37.45 billion** per `investor-documents/sec-edgar/10-K_20260218_rpt20251231_acc0001628280_26_009043.htm`. **[Fact]** FY2025 net income attributable to shareholders was **$3.75 billion** (diluted EPS **$5.51**) on the same filing. **[Fact]** The group reports through an Americas division (materials and building solutions) and an International division (international solutions), per segment disclosures in the FY2025 10-K and the October 2025 prospectus supplement (`424B5_20251007_rpt_acc0001193125_25_233453.htm`).

### Business mechanics (Hohn)

Revenue grew from **$32.72 billion** (FY2022) to **$37.45 billion** (FY2025), about **4.6%** CAGR per filing extracts in `ssi_report_2026-08-07.md`. Operating cash flow accelerated to **$5.62 billion** in FY2025 while capital spending rose to **$2.71 billion**, so growth consumes cash as well as generating it. Share count fell to **668.2 million** by April 2026 through buybacks, supporting per-share metrics but not eliminating balance-sheet leverage.

### Moat (Munger)

**Narrow moat (inference):** route density and quarry reserves in Americas materials; not a software-like widening moat. Pricing power is regional and cyclical. Debt-funded scale expansion can destroy moat economics if returns on acquired assets fall below the cost of debt.
