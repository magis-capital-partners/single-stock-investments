Commencing on or after April 29, 2024, Target Corporation will send the following communication to shareholders with Target.com email addresses or who expressly consented to receive communications and vote by proxy via the Internet.

 

To Our Shareholders

At Target’s Annual Meeting of Shareholders to be held on June 12, 2024, owners of Target shares will vote on important matters, including the election of our Board of Directors. If you hold shares of Target stock, including shares owned through Target’s 401(k) Plan (the Plan), you are strongly encouraged to participate in this important process by exercising your right to vote. To help inform your perspective, Target’s 2024 Proxy Statement includes details about items of business up for vote this year, as well as information related to executive compensation, corporate governance, and financial performance. Please remember to exercise your right to vote this year in conjunction with Target’s Annual Meeting of Shareholders.
