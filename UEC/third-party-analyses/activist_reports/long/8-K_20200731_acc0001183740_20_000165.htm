SECTION 5 –CORPORATE GOVERNANCE AND MANAGEMENT 

Item 5.07.Submission of Matters to a Vote of Security Holders 

Results of the Annual General Meeting

An Annual General Meeting of Shareholders (the “AGM”) of the Company was held on July 30, 2020 to approve the agenda items described below.

Proxies for the AGM were solicited pursuant to Regulation 14A under the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as amended, and there was no solicitation in opposition to the Company’s solicitation.

A total of 78,773,850 common shares (42.76% of the184,187,920 issued and outstanding shares of the Company’s common stock entitled to vote as of June 4, 2020, the record date for the AGM) were present in person or by proxy, constituted a quorum for the transaction of business and were voted at the AGM. The agenda items submitted at the AGM were passed as described below.
