Item 5.07 Submission of Matters to a Vote of Security Holders.

The Annual Meeting of Stockholders (the “Annual Meeting”) of Teradata Corporation (“Teradata” or the “Company”) was held on April 17, 2018. At the Annual Meeting, the holders of a total of 111,241,513 shares of the Company’s common stock entitled to vote were present in person or represented by proxy, constituting approximately 92% of the total shares issued and outstanding and entitled to vote at the Annual Meeting. Stockholders voted on four matters:

 

  1) a proposal to elect Lisa R. Bacus, Timothy C.K. Chou, James R. Ringler, and John G. Schwarz as Class II directors;

 

  2) an advisory (non-binding) vote on executive compensation (a “say-on-pay” vote);

 

  3) a proposal to approve the amended and restated Teradata Employee Stock Purchase Plan; and

 

  4) a proposal to ratify the appointment of PricewaterhouseCoopers LLP as the Company’s independent registered public accounting firm for 2018.
