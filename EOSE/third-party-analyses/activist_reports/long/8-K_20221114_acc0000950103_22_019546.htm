Item 1.01 Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement.

 

On November 14, 2022, Eos Energy Enterprises, Inc. (the “Company”) and YA II PN, LTD (“Yorkville”) entered into Amendment No. 2 (the “Amendment No. 2”) to the Standby Equity Purchase Agreement dated as of April 28, 2022 (as amended by this Amendment No. 2 and Amendment No. 1 dated as of June 13, 2022, the “SEPA”), to decrease the commitment amount under the SEPA from $200,000,000 to $125,000,000. With the exception of the decrease in the commitment amount, the terms of the SEPA remain unchanged, and the SEPA, as amended, remains in full force and effect.

 

The foregoing description of the Amendment No. 2 does not purport to be complete and is qualified in its entirety by reference to the full text of the Amendment No. 2, a copy of which is filed with this Current Report on Form 8-K as Exhibit 10.1 hereto and is hereby incorporated by reference.
