Item 8.01 Other Events.
On August 31, 2023, Eos Energy Enterprises, Inc. (the “Company”) issued a press release announcing that the U.S. Department of Energy (the “DOE”) issued a Conditional Commitment Letter to the Company for a loan of an aggregate principal amount of up to $398.6 million through the DOE’s Clean Energy Financing Program. Certain technical, legal and financial conditions must be satisfied and diligenced to the satisfaction of the DOE before the DOE enters into definitive financing documents with the Company and funds the loan.
