## What it is

Partners Value Investments L.P. is a publicly traded partnership whose job is to hold Brookfield paper for the partnership group. The listed Equity LP unit is **PVF.UN**. Preferred units are **PVF.PR.U**. A sister company, Partners Value Investments Inc., lists exchangeable shares (**PVF.A**) and preferreds (**PVF.PR.V**). Warrants expired **June 30, 2026**. Units were split **ten-for-one** on August 8, 2025.

Operating subsidiaries (Partners Value Investments Inc. and Partners Value Split Corp.) hold most of the Brookfield shares. The partnership also owns a smaller book of other marketable securities and private-fund interests, plus cash.

## How to read it

PVF.UN is no longer the deep-discount Brookfield wrapper it used to be. After the 2025 split and the June 2026 warrant expiry, the unit trades **around** last reported NAV and **above** a Brookfield mark-to-market roll. You are not being paid a holdco discount for the 89% Brookfield asset mix. You are paying a small premium for a less liquid TSXV vehicle that still has preferreds, borrowings, and 77.6 million exchangeable shares in the fully diluted count.

If the goal is Brookfield exposure, **BN at $37.31** is the cleaner claim on the same cash-flow engine, and it is the name this folder actually values. PVF.UN is a related-party look-through, useful only if a discount re-opens.
