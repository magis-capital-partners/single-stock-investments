Commencing on July 26, 2021, Electronic Arts Inc. will use the following supplemental information in communicating with certain stockholders.

Dear Stockholder:

Electronic Arts Inc. (“we”, the “Company” or “EA”) is holding its 2021 annual meeting of stockholders (the “Annual Meeting”) on August 12, 2021. On June 25, 2021, we filed a definitive proxy statement (the “Proxy Statement”) with the Securities and Exchange Commission (“SEC”) in connection with our Annual Meeting, which is available, along with our 2021 annual report to stockholders, at https://materials.proxyvote.com/ or on our investor relations website at https://ir.ea.com/financial-information/annual-reports-and-proxy-information/default.aspx and we have posted a supplemental Stockholder Outreach Presentation on our Investor Relations website at https://ir.ea.com that we have included with this filing.

Our Commitment To Not Grant Special Equity Awards Through at Least the End of Fiscal Year 2026

As disclosed in the Proxy Statement, special equity awards are not part of our regular executive compensation program. We committed to not grant special equity awards to our named executive officers (“NEOs”) outside of our regular compensation program in fiscal year 2022 and our intention was that this moratorium would extend significantly beyond this timeframe. After considering continued stockholder concerns regarding the use of special equity awards, the Compensation Committee has determined explicitly to extend our commitment to not grant any special equity awards to our NEOs outside of our regular compensation program through at least the end of fiscal year 2026. This is significantly beyond the expiration of any outstanding special equity awards, as those performance periods end in 2023. The Compensation Committee’s extended commitment is in addition to the extensive actions we have taken in response to the 2020 say-on-pay vote, as outlined in the Proxy Statement, the Stockholder Outreach Presentation, and our letter filed with the SEC on July 16, 2021.

For the foregoing reasons, we firmly believe support for our say-on-pay proposal is warranted and we ask that you vote FOR the advisory vote to approve NEO compensation.
