## Repairs

- RSS archival normalizes URL objects to HTTPS strings and supplies content type;
  unresolved announcement dates bind as null. Exact terminal SQLite news failures
  are archived before one idempotent retry. Completion must be observed separately.
- A publisher excerpt records detection but does not prove complete terms or a
  correction. A private reviewed issuer-source request binds publisher URL, issuer
  CIK, original-body digest and review clock. The independently resolved ticker
  must match. Missing or corrupt originals fail closed.
- Clause-bound dates distinguish action, legal and trading dates, including
  afternoon effective times. Explicit unresolved implementation conditions remain
  blocked. A single archived amendment takes precedence over its superseded
  source; the repair appends a decision without replacing original clocks.
- A closed quote helper can restart after bounded backoff, restoring its selected
  subscriptions only after closure. Sanitized transport categories remain separate
  from budget warnings. Recent priority names may replace stale subscriptions
  after a bounded one-minute interval; the existing 128-symbol ceiling remains.
- Missing FIGI does not discard an otherwise verified common-share quote: an
  exact active USD common-share reference/CIK plus an actual live raw-symbol
  mapping can identify the observed Databento instrument in its own namespace.
  This never invents a FIGI, CUSIP, IBKR conid, locate or permission. Reference
  refresh, mapping and observation clocks remain explicit. Bounded round-robin
  reference refresh prevents unresolved names from starving later subscriptions.
- Issuer filing-delinquency diagnoses are source-bound, checksum verified and
  distinct from financial rejection. They create no facts and do not relax the
  200-day freshness rule. ITP's latest recovered September 2025 statements are
  stale; its October 2026 primary disclosure reports overdue annual/quarterly
  filings. Subsequent sufficiently fresh statements are still needed.
