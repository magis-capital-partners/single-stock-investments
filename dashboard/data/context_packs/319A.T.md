## What this business is (stable)
Next Generation Technology Group buys Japanese manufacturing companies and keeps them. The customers are the industrial buyers of the subsidiaries, which make parts and equipment and, in one case, rent and sell used forklifts. The parent does not run a single factory. It buys the whole company, usually for cash, and leaves the local name in place. It then tries to raise cash generation with a playbook it calls NGP. The shares trade on the Tokyo Stock Exchange Growth market as 319A.
