## What this business is (stable)
Dino Polska runs a nationwide chain of mid-sized grocery stores in Poland, mostly in smaller towns and on the edges of larger cities. Shoppers buy everyday food with a heavy fresh mix (meat, produce, bakery) from a standardized format of roughly five thousand products. The company owns much of its logistics: distribution centers replenish stores on a tight cycle so shelves stay stocked without depending on wholesalers. At 31 March 2026 the network had 3,094 stores and twelve distribution centers. Almost all revenue is retail grocery sales in Poland.

## Why the market might be wrong (stable)

The predictive attribute is reinvestment quality: cash from operations still covers growth CapEx, like-for-like sales stayed positive at 4.4%, and white space in several provinces is not exhausted. The inefficiency is confusing growth CapEx with a broken cash engine.
