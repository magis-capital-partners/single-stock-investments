## What this business is (stable)
Wynn Resorts is a developer and operator of luxury integrated casino resorts. **[Fact]** Through roughly **72%** ownership of Wynn Macau, Limited, the company runs Wynn Palace and Wynn Macau in Macau. **[Fact]** In the U.S., it owns and operates Wynn Las Vegas and Encore on the Strip and Encore Boston Harbor in Massachusetts. **[Fact]** FY2025 consolidated operating revenues were **$7.14 billion** (up **0.1%** year over year). **[Fact]** The company also holds a **40%** equity interest in a joint venture building Wynn Al Marjan Island in the United Arab Emirates, targeted to open in **2027**. **[Inference]** Owner economics are driven by gaming win (especially VIP and premium mass in Macau), hotel occupancy and average daily rate, food and beverage spend, and capital spending to maintain ultra-luxury positioning.

## Why the market might be wrong (stable)
