## What this business is (stable)
New URC bolts **Sweetwater Royalties** (perpetual Wyoming Green River trona and soda ash royalties plus a large fee land estate) onto **Uranium Royalty Corp.** (uranium royalties, streams, and residual physical uranium). **[Fact]** Sweetwater's operators (WE Soda, Tata, Sisecam, Solvay/American Soda) have mined the basin for about 50 years; New URC owns **92%** of Sweetwater. **[Fact]** URC brings uranium deal skills and a mostly pre-cash royalty book; legacy shareholders own about **41%** of the combined company after the arrangement closed **2026-07-27**. **[Fact]** The public parent moved to Delaware and listed on NASDAQ **2026-07-28**, exiting the Canadian PFIC profile for *new* U.S. holders after domestication.

## Why the market might be wrong (stable)
The market may still price this as a sleepy foreign uranium royalty name, or as a soda-ash story at the wrong point in the cycle. **[Inference]** The real security is a **levered scarce-land royalty stub**: cash today is weak because soda ash is in a deep downturn, but the mineral estate is long-duration and capital-light for the owner. **[Inference]** Mispricing can also come from **deal optics** (deemed **US$3.64**) versus **equity math after US$625m debt**. **[Fact]** Pro forma FY2025 Sweetwater royalty revenue fell to **US$59.3m** from **US$82.5m** prior year (circular pro formas), consistent with generational soda-ash oversupply commentary from basin operators. Predictive attribute: **market_structure_discount** (complex deal, leverage, PFIC history, and uranium label on a trona-heavy cash engine).
