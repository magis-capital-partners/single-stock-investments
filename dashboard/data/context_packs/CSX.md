## What this business is (stable)
CSX Corporation (NYSE: CSX) operates a Class I freight railroad across 23 eastern states, the District of Columbia, and two Canadian provinces. **[Fact]** FY2025 revenue was **$14.09 billion**, down about **3%** from the prior year per the 2026 proxy summary. **[Fact]** FY2025 operating cash flow was **$4.61 billion** and capital spending was **$2.90 billion**, leaving **$1.71 billion** of normalized owner earnings. **[Inference]** CSX is an eastern U.S. rail compounder: a high fixed-cost network with pricing power on captive lanes, cyclical exposure to industrial and intermodal volumes, and ongoing capital needs to maintain track and equipment.

## Why the market might be wrong (stable)
The market may be paying for **network durability and precision-scheduled railroading efficiency** while underweighting **$18.9 billion of total debt** on the June 2026 balance sheet. A stale 2012 debt tag had implied roughly half that leverage. **[Inference]** At **$50.27**, the stock trades near **7 times** our base proof value per share after refreshing debt from filings, so the market embeds reinvestment returns and terminal economics well above our bounded base case. Insider activity over the past year shows net selling exceeding open-market buys, which weakens a pure "insiders know more" bull case unless sales are largely planned programs.
