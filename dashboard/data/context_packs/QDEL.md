## What this business is (stable)
QuidelOrtho is a post-merger diagnostics company (Quidel plus Ortho Clinical Diagnostics, 2022). **[Fact]** It sells lab instruments, transfusion-medicine tests, point-of-care rapid assays, and early-stage molecular diagnostics to hospitals, labs, and blood centers. **[Fact]** Revenue is largely recurring consumables on installed bases. **[Fact]** FY2025 revenue was **$2,730.2M** (-1.9% YoY per `10-K_20260219...htm`); adjusted EBITDA was **$597.0M** (21.9% margin). ## Why the market might be wrong (stable)
