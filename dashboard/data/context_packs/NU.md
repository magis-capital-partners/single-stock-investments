## What this business is (stable)
Nu Holdings Ltd. (ticker **NU**) is a Cayman holding company for a digital money platform operating mainly in **Brazil, Mexico, and Colombia**. **[Fact]** As of December 31, 2025 it served **131 million** customers with credit and prepaid cards, demand deposits and Money Boxes, Pix payments, personal and SME loans, investments, and insurance distribution. **[Fact]** FY2025 total revenue was **US$15.8 billion** (+37%) and net income attributable to the parent was **US$2.87 billion** (+45%). **[Fact]** Customers fund the balance sheet with **US$41.9 billion** of deposits, mostly payable on demand, while an interest-earning credit book and card receivables drive net interest and fee income.
