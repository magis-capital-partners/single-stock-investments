## What this business is (stable)
AMREP Corporation (NYSE: AXR) is a Rio Rancho, New Mexico land developer and a small homebuilder. It owns about 16,200 acres in Sandoval County, sells developed lots and raw land to builders, and builds single-family homes itself. Fiscal 2026 revenue was $52.8 million. The company has 52 employees, almost no borrowed money, and a large cash balance. A defined-benefit pension and an old fulfillment business are no longer operating segments. The stock is a claim on cash, houses in process, and land carried at historical cost.

## Why the market might be wrong (stable)
The predictive attribute is acreage that the balance sheet still carries at cost, in a town where finished lots have sold for about $820,000 an acre and two bulk raw tracts sold near $4,600 an acre. The inefficiency is that a thin stock with three holders owning about half the shares is priced off recent earnings, which collapsed when developed-lot sales paused. At the October 22, 2026 close of $20.91, the equity is worth about $111 million against $141 million of book equity and $48.7 million of cash. That is a discount to book. It is not, on the filing split we can actually see, a price of zero for the raw acres. There is no hard catalyst. Cash and the absence of debt are the cushion while land sales stay episodic.
