## What this business is (stable)
Dollar General Corporation (NYSE: DG) operates more than **20,000** small-box discount stores in **47** U.S. states, focused on rural and small-town communities where larger retailers are scarce. **[Fact]** FY2025 net sales were **$42.7 billion**, up from **$40.6 billion** in FY2024. **[Fact]** The mix is heavily consumables (food, health and beauty, cleaning), with seasonal, apparel, and home categories filling out the basket. **[Fact]** Stores average roughly **7,400 square feet**, use a low-cost build model, and rely on a centralized distribution network. **[Inference]** Dollar General is a high-turn consumables compounder whose economics depend on store density, purchasing scale, and reliable in-stock execution in price-sensitive markets.
