## What this business is (stable)
CrowdStrike Holdings sells cloud-native endpoint and identity security software on a subscription model. Customers pay recurring fees for the Falcon platform, which consolidates threat detection, response, and adjacent modules (identity, cloud workload, log management). Revenue reached $4.81 billion in fiscal 2026 (year ended January 31, 2026) per `CRWD/investor-documents/sec-edgar/10-K_20260305_rpt20260131_acc0001535527_26_000010.htm`. The company generates substantial cash from operations ($1.61 billion in fiscal 2026) while reporting a GAAP net loss ($162.5 million) because stock-based compensation and acquisition-related costs remain large relative to reported earnings.

## Why the market might be wrong (stable)
