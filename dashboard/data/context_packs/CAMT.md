## What this business is (stable)
Camtek Ltd. (NASDAQ: CAMT) is an Israeli developer and manufacturer of automated optical inspection and metrology systems for the semiconductor industry. **[Fact]** Its tools inspect and measure integrated-circuit features on wafers from front-end through back-end and post-dicing assembly steps, with emphasis on advanced packaging, heterogeneous integration, and silicon carbide substrates. **[Fact]** FY2025 revenue was **$496.1 million**, operating income **$128.2 million**, and normalized owner cash (operating cash flow minus capital spending) roughly **$127.5 million** on **45.8 million** shares. **[Fact]** The company completed a **$500 million** convertible notes issuance in 2025, leaving **$519.8 million** of non-current convertible debt on the balance sheet at year-end against **$177.8 million** cash. **[Inference]** Camtek sits in the semiconductor capital-equipment cycle but has a growing mix tied to AI-related advanced packaging spend.

## Why the market might be wrong (stable)
The market may be paying for durable AI-driven inspection growth while underweighting **convertible debt dilution and interest burden**, **customer concentration** among leading foundries and OSATs, and **semiconductor cap-ex cyclicality**. **[Fact]** The FY2025 Form 20-F states that approximately **50%** of revenue comes from products supporting heterogeneous integration and advanced packaging, with much of expected growth tied to AI-related capital spending. **[Inference]** If AI packaging investment slows or export restrictions bite China customers, revenue can fall faster than the multiple implies. **[Fact]** Net income fell to **$50.7 million** in FY2025 from **$118.5 million** in FY2024 despite higher revenue, signaling that not all top-line growth converted to reported earnings. At **$147.09**, the stock embeds sustained high reinvestment returns that filing-grounded owner cash does not yet support.
