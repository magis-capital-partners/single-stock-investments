## What this business is (stable)
Cummins Inc. (NYSE: CMI) designs, manufactures, and services diesel and natural-gas engines, generator sets, and related components for on-highway trucks, construction, mining, marine, and power-generation markets. **[Fact]** FY2025 revenue was **$33.67 billion** and operating income was **$4.03 billion**. **[Fact]** FY2025 operating cash flow was **$3.62 billion** and capital spending was **$1.24 billion**, leaving **$2.39 billion** of normalized owner earnings. **[Fact]** Reportable segments include Engine, Distribution, Components, Power Systems, and Accelera (zero-emissions technologies). **[Inference]** Cummins is a global industrial compounder with cyclical end markets, a large installed base that supports aftermarket revenue, and a strategic pivot toward hydrogen, battery, and fuel-cell products through Accelera.

Insider activity shows large net sales versus modest open-market buys over the past year, which weakens the "insiders know more" bull case unless those sales are largely planned 10b5-1 programs.
