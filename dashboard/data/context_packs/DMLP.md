## What this business is (stable)
Dorchester Minerals, L.P. is a Dallas-based master limited partnership that owns net royalty and mineral interests across U.S. oil and gas basins and distributes essentially all available cash to common unitholders each quarter. **[Fact]** The partnership has no operating employees; revenue is royalty and net profits interest cash from properties operated by third parties. **[Fact]** As of December 31, 2025, **48,255,450** common units were outstanding (`DMLP/investor-documents/sec-edgar/10-K_20260224_rpt20251231_acc0001437749_26_005376.htm`). **[Fact]** FY2025 operating revenues were **$152.8 million**, net income **$57.4 million** (**$1.16** per unit), and declared distributions **$2.775346** per unit (same 10-K). **[Fact]** The partnership agreement requires quarterly distributions equal to **100%** of available cash after reserves (same 10-K, Business section).

## Why the market might be wrong (stable)
Yield-oriented investors may treat Dorchester as a static bond proxy and miss accretive growth from unit-funded mineral acquisitions in 2024-2025. **[Inference]** GAAP net income understates cash distributions because of timing between production and cash receipts. **[Inference]** Screeners focused on earnings yield rather than distribution yield may mis-rank the name versus simpler royalty trusts. **[Inference]** Proof-first component math at base case sums to about **$19.24** per unit of economic value versus a price near **$27**, suggesting the market embeds acreage optionality and mid-cycle commodity assumptions not yet reproduced in filing-locked owner-cash paths.
