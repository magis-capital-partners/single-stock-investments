ITEM 5.02.
Departure of Directors or Certain Officers; Election of Directors; Appointment of Certain Officers; Compensatory Arrangements of Certain Officers.

On January 7, 2021, Las Vegas Sands Corp. (the “Company”) announced that Chairman and Chief Executive Officer, Sheldon G. Adelson, will take a medical leave of absence, effective immediately, to receive further treatment for lymphoma. The Company’s Board of Directors (the “Board”) has appointed Robert G. Goldstein, President and Chief Operating Officer, as Acting Chief Executive Officer and Acting Chairman, effective immediately. Mr. Goldstein will continue in his role as President and Chief Operating Officer. Mr. Goldstein has been the Company’s President and Chief Operating Officer and a member of the Board since 2015 and has held a variety of senior leadership positions since joining the Company in 1995.

ITEM 8.01.Other Events.

On January 7, 2021, Sands China Ltd. (“SCL”), a majority-owned subsidiary of Las Vegas Sands Corp., announced that its Chairman and Chief Executive Officer, Sheldon G. Adelson, will take a medical leave of absence, effective immediately, to receive further treatment for lymphoma. SCL’s Board of Directors (the “SCL Board”) has appointed Robert G. Goldstein as Acting Chief Executive Officer and Acting Chairman, effective immediately. SCL also announced the addition of Mr. Chum Kwan Lock, Grant to the SCL Board.
