ITEM 7.01.Regulation FD Disclosure
On November 26, 2022, Las Vegas Sands Corp. (the “Company”) and Sands China Ltd., the Company’s majority-owned subsidiary (“Sands China”), announced that Venetian Macau Limited, Sands China’s wholly-owned subsidiary (“VML”), has been provisionally awarded a new gaming concession for the operation of casino games of chance in Macao, subject to finalizing and entering into a gaming concession contract with the Macao government. The final terms and conditions of the gaming concession contract remain subject to further discussions with the Macao government. In addition, the entry into the gaming concession contract is conditional upon, among others, VML fulfilling certain capital and management participation requirements under Macao’s gaming law.

The information in this Item 7.01 shall not be deemed “filed” for purposes of Section 18 of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as amended, nor shall it be deemed incorporated by reference in any filing under the Securities Act of 1933, as amended, except as shall be expressly set forth by specific reference in any such filing.
