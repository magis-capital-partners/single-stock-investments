ITEM 5.03.Other Events.
On October 18, 2022, the Board of Directors (the “Board”) of Las Vegas Sands Corp. (the “Company”) approved an amendment (the “By-Laws Amendment”) to the Amended and Restated By-Laws of the Company. The By-Laws Amendment became effective on October 18, 2022. The By-Laws Amendment (i) makes compliance with the applicable provisions of Rule 14a-19 under the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as amended, a requirement for a stockholder’s eligibility to nominate a director candidate, (ii) requires a stockholder providing a notice of nomination to the Board or a notice of business at the annual or special meeting of stockholders to provide certain disclosures and updates to the original notice and make certain representations and (iii) reflects other minor updates.

The foregoing description is qualified in its entirety by reference to the copy of the By-Laws Amendment, which is filed as Exhibit 3.1 to this Current Report on Form 8-K.
