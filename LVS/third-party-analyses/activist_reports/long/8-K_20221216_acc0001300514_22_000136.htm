ITEM 8.01.Other Events.
On December 16, 2022, Las Vegas Sands Corp. (the “Company”) and Sands China Ltd., the Company’s majority-owned subsidiary (“Sands China”), announced that Venetian Macau Limited, Sands China’s wholly owned subsidiary, was awarded a new 10-year gaming concession for the operation of casino games of chance in Macao under a gaming concession contract entered into with Macao on the same day.
