Item 5.03 – Amendments to Articles of Incorporation or Bylaws; Change in Fiscal Year

 

--12-31

On December 10, 2019, the Board of Directors of Hecla Mining Company (the “Company”) approved an amendment to the Company’s Bylaws, which amendment is effective immediately. The amendment approved by the Board amends Article VII of the prior Bylaws of the Company to provide that the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware is the exclusive forum for the adjudication of certain disputes. Previously the Bylaws provided for any “state or federal court located within the State of Delaware” as the exclusive forums for those disputes.

 

The description of the amendment to the Company’s Bylaws contained herein is qualified in its entirety by, and should be read in conjunction with, the complete text of the Company’s Bylaws, as amended, which are attached hereto as Exhibit 3.1 and incorporated by reference into this Item 5.03.
