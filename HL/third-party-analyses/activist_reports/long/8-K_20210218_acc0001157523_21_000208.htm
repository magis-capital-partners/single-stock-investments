Item 2.02  Results of Operations and Financial Condition.

On February 21, 2021, Hecla Mining Company (the “Company”) issued a news release announcing the Company’s fourth quarter 2020 and twelve months ended December 31, 2020 financial results.  The news release is attached hereto as Exhibit 99.1 to this Form 8-K.

In accordance with General Instruction B.2 of Form 8-K, the information in this Item 2.02, including Exhibit 99.1, shall not be deemed to be “filed” for purposes of Section 18 of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as amended (the “Exchange Act”), or otherwise subject to the liability of that section, and shall not be incorporated by reference into any of the Company’s filings or other documents filed under the Securities Act of 1933, as amended, or the Exchange Act, except as shall be expressly set forth by specific reference in such filing.

Item 7.01  Regulation FD Disclosure.

In the news release included herein as Exhibit 99.1, the Company announced it will be conducting a series of videocasts that it plans to periodically publish commencing later in 2021 that will provide additional information on the Company, silver, and the industry. These will be available on the Company’s website at www.hecla-mining.com and various social media platforms. The information contained in item 7.01 of this Form 8-K shall be deemed furnished, not filed.

Item 8.01  Other Events

On February 18, 2021, the Company announced it would pay a dividend on its shares of common stock in the amount of $0.00875, to shareholders of record as of March 8, 2021, payable on or about March 19, 2021.  In addition to the common stock dividend, the Company also announced it declared a dividend of $0.875 on its Series B Cumulative Convertible Preferred Stock to shareholders of record as of March 15, 2021, payable on or about April 1, 2021.
