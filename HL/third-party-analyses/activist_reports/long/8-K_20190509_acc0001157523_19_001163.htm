Item 2.02.   Results of Operations and Financial Condition.

On May 9, 2019, Hecla Mining Company (the “Company”) issued a news release announcing the Company’s first quarter 2019 financial results.  The news release is attached hereto as Exhibit 99.1 to this Form 8-K.

In accordance with General Instruction B.2 of Form 8-K, the information in Item 2.02, including Exhibit 99.1, is being furnished and shall not be deemed to be “filed” for purposes of Section 18 of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as amended (the “Exchange Act”), or otherwise subject to the liability of that section, and shall not be incorporated by reference into any of the Company’s filings or other document filed under the Securities Act of 1933, as amended, or the Exchange Act, except as shall be expressly set forth by specific reference in such filing.

Item 8.01.   Other Events.

On May 9, 2019, the Company announced it would pay a dividend on its shares of common stock in the amount of $0.0025, to shareholders of record as of May 24, 2019, payable on or about June 4, 2019.  In addition to the common stock dividend, the Company also announced it declared a dividend of $0.875 on its Series B Cumulative Convertible Preferred Stock to shareholders of record as of June 14, 2019, payable on or about July 1, 2019.
