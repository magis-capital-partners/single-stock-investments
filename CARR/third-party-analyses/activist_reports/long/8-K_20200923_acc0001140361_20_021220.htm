Item 8.01
Other Events.

On September 22, 2020, Carrier Global Corporation (“Carrier”) issued a press release announcing that it sold 9.25 million B shares of Beijer Ref AB (“Beijer Ref”), equal to approximately 7.9% of the outstanding Class B shares, through its previously announced accelerated equity offering. Beijer Ref is listed on the Nasdaq Stockholm. The shares were priced at SEK 290 per share. Following the accelerated equity offering, Carrier continues to hold approximately 30% of the B shares of Beijer Ref, approximately 22% of the A shares of Beijer Ref and approximately 26% of the Beijer Ref total votes.

A copy of the press release is attached as Exhibit 99.1 to this report and is incorporated by reference herein.
