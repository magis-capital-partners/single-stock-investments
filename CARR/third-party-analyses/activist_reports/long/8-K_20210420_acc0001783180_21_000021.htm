Section 5 - Corporate Governance and Management

Item 5.07 Submission of Matters to a Vote of Security Holders.

Carrier Global Corporation (the “Company”) held its 2021 Annual Meeting of Shareowners on April 19, 2021. As of February 22, 2021, the record date for the meeting, 869,283,513 shares of the Company’s common stock were issued and outstanding. A quorum of 767,817,561 shares of common stock was present or represented at the meeting.
