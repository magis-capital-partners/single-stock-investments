The Walt Disney Company will begin distribution of the following letter to shareholders:


The Walt Disney Company March 22, 2024 Dear Fellow Shareholder, We are writing you regarding certain inaccurate assertions in the recommendation from ISS regarding Disney’s succession process ahead of our upcoming 2024 Annual Meeting. The Succession Planning Committee and the full Disney Board are engaged in and executing on a best-in-class, intensive, diligent and thorough succession planning process informed by the Committee members’ collective experiences in successful search processes at other large publicly traded companies. It remains a top priority not only to name a successor, but also to position the new CEO for long-term success at Disney by, among other things, surrounding the new CEO with a world-class team of senior executives who can work together to lead this iconic company into the future. The Committee met seven times in 2023, consistently discussing its progress with the full Board, and will continue to meet regularly until the succession process is completed. The Committee, comprised entirely of independent members of the Board and which includes two members who recently completed a successful CEO succession, are reviewing internal candidates as well as potential external candidates with the help of a well-regarded national search firm. Each internal candidate is going through a rigorous preparation process. This includes mentorship from Bob Iger and external coaching, engagement with all Board directors, and comprehensive reviews of each candidate. Our robust search process is actively proceeding, thoughtfully and carefully, to its conclusion and is designed to surface the next great leader for The Walt Disney Company. We urge shareholders to protect their investment and the future of the Company by voting the WHITE proxy card FOR only Disney’s 12 nominees TODAY. Sincerely, Members of the Succession Planning Committee Mark Parker (Chair) Executive Chairman, Nike Mary Barra Chair and CEO, General Motors Company Calvin McDonald CEO, Lululemon Athletica Inc. James Gorman Executive Chairman, Morgan Stanley 1
