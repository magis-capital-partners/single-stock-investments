The Walt Disney Company will begin distribution of the following letter to shareholders:

YOUR VOTE IS IMPORTANT TO US.


Your vote is very important. Please vote your shares today. Whether or not you plan to attend the Annual Meeting, we urge you to vote your shares TODAY by internet, phone or mail. INTERNET: Locate the control number shown on the front of the WHITE proxy card or WHITE voting instruction form included in this package and access the website indicated. SCAN: Your WHITE proxy card or WHITE voting instruction form included in this package includes a QR code for voting by mobile phone. PHONE: If you are a registered holder, you may submit your proxy by touch-tone telephone by dialing the number indicated on your WHITE proxy card. You will need to enter the control number shown on your WHITE proxy card. Please keep careful track and be sure to vote “FOR” no more than 12 nominees. MAIL: Mark, sign and date your WHITE proxy card or WHITE voting instruction form included in this package and return it in the postage-paid envelope provided. Please be careful to mark “FOR” for no more than 12 nominees.
