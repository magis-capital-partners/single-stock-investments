Item 7.01 Regulation FD Disclosure.
On October 19, 2026, EquipmentShare.com Inc (the “Company”) issued a press release announcing a further increase to its full-year 2026 financial guidance, reflecting strong customer demand, disciplined execution, and increasing productivity across its expanding rental network. A copy of the press release is furnished as Exhibit 99.1 to this Form 8-K and is incorporated by reference herein. The Company will provide its actual results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 at the time of its third quarter earnings call.
