## Business & moat

The 20-F treats Eskay Creek, Kiaka, and Greenstone as the material properties for mining-disclosure purposes. **[Fact]** Eskay is a 3.52% gold stream. The company pays 10% of the spot gold price on ounces delivered. Completion tests run to 30 September 2027. Construction was described as underway, with first production expected in the second quarter of 2027. That stream was bought on 10 April 2026 for $340 million cash and 2,054,906 shares. It is not 2025 revenue.

2025 attributable ounces were 9,815. Average realized gold price in the annual table was $3,671 an ounce. Net income was $20.332 million. Cash from operations was $16.945 million, and the pre-working-capital figure was $24.685 million on 92,771,182 weighted average shares. **[Fact]** The second figure is before working capital and is not operating cash. Finance and interest expense in the EBITDA bridge was $12.162 million for 2025. That is already large next to operating cash, and it predates the fully drawn $385 million balance described later in the report.

The facility interest is SOFR plus 2.25% to 3.75%. The leverage covenant steps from 6.00 times at the quarter ended 30 June 2026 down to 3.50 times by the quarter ended 30 September 2027. **[Fact]** Those are contractual tests, not a forecast that they will be met.

### Mental models

A stream is a toll with an ongoing payment. Versamet does not keep the full metal price. Greenstone, for example, includes a cash payment to the operator. Debt to buy the toll has to be serviced before the equity owner is paid. Counting pretax, pre-working-capital cash as owner earnings skips that service.

### Business mechanics

Operators deliver metal or pay a royalty. Versamet sells metal and records a cost of sales. It also borrows, and it issues shares. Ending shares were 93.4 million. The Eskay deal issued more after the year end. Dilution and debt are both live.

### Moat

The stream contracts can last for the life of a mine. **[Inference]** A life-of-mine contract is only a moat if the mine is built and the debt that bought the contract stays inside its covenants. Narrow, and unproven on the 2026 book, is the honest label. The registry says narrow because the 2025 contracts exist. The 2026 leverage is the open question.

## Risks & inversion

**Primary risk:** The leverage covenant steps down to 3.50 times by September 2027 while Eskay is still in construction, and the drawn balance was $385 million as of the annual report.

- Interest of $12.162 million already sat against $16.945 million of operating cash in 2025, before the larger draw.
- Eskay completion tests run to 30 September 2027. If they fail, the stream percentage changes under the agreement. This pass does not model that branch.
- One customer buys the metal from several streams. The 20-F says so. A sales-counterparty problem is different from a mine problem, and both exist.

**Lens failure mode:** Treating attributable ounces as cash. Ounces are before the ongoing payment, tax, interest, and debt principal.

**Upside / downside from price:** Upside is Eskay and the 2025 book covering the debt with cash left over. Downside is a covenant breach or a construction delay while the term loan is outstanding.
