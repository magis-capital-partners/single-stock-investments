On June 3, 2019, Robert J. McNally, President and Chief Executive Officer of EQT Corporation (the “Company”) sent the following communication to the Company’s employees:

 

Dear Fellow Employees,

 

This afternoon we issued a press release announcing that the independent members of our Board of Directors have sent a letter to all shareholders of EQT regarding the election of directors at our upcoming July 10 Annual Meeting. The letter highlights the strong financial and operational performance we have delivered and reiterates the Board’s recommendation that shareholders vote “FOR” the Company’s 12 director nominees on the GOLD universal proxy card.

 

I’ve been impressed with the enthusiasm across the Company. The financial and operational progress highlighted in the letter is a testament to all of you and your hard work. You have embraced our new plan, and it’s clearly paying off. In fact, EQT is now operating as a free cash flow machine and one of the Appalachian Basin’s low-cost leaders.

 

The new Board and management team assumed control in the fourth quarter of 2018 setting out to optimize lateral lengths, spacing and operating cadence. While we all know there is still more work to do, we’ve made tremendous progress to date. To summarize some of those accomplishments that demonstrate our transformation, we saw:

 

· 45% year-over-year improvement in rig move efficiency (Q1’19 vs. Q1’18);

· 25% reduction in drilling days / 1,000 ft vs. Q4’18;

· 40% improvement in frac crew moves (Q1’19 vs. Q1’18); and

· 70% reduction in completion non-productive time (Q1’19 vs. Q1’18).

 

You can find the letter here or by visiting VoteGoldForEQT.com. If you haven’t already, I recommend taking a few minutes to explore the site. It includes great background on our slate of director nominees as well as information regarding our Annual Meeting and how shareholders can vote their shares in support of the EQT team using the GOLD universal proxy card. I think you’ll be impressed with the caliber, qualifications and experience of EQT’s director nominees.

 

Please let us know if you have any questions and, as always, stay safe at work and at home.

 

Thank you,

 

Rob
