This Tender Offer Statement on Schedule TO (this “Schedule TO”) relates to the tender offer by Atom Acquisition Sub, Inc., a Maryland corporation (“Merger Sub”) and a wholly owned indirect subsidiary of Jacobs Engineering Group Inc., a Delaware corporation (“Parent”), for all of the outstanding shares of common stock, par value $0.001 per share (“Shares”), of The KeyW Holding Corporation, a Maryland corporation (“KeyW”), at a price of $11.25 per share, net to the seller in cash, without interest thereon and less any applicable withholding taxes, upon the terms and conditions set forth in the offer to purchase dated May 13, 2019 (the “Offer to Purchase”), a copy of which is attached as Exhibit (a)(1)(A), and in the related letter of transmittal (the “Letter of Transmittal”), a copy of which is attached as Exhibit (a)(1)(B), which, as each may be amended or supplemented from time to time, collectively constitute the “Offer.”
