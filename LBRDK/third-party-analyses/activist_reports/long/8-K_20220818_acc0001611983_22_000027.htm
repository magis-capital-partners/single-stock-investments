Item 7.01. Regulation FD Disclosure.

​

On August 17, 2022, Liberty Broadband Corporation (the “Company”) issued a press release (the “press release”) announcing that its board of directors declared a quarterly cash dividend which will be payable on October 17, 2022 to stockholders of record of the Company’s Series A Cumulative Redeemable Preferred Stock at the close of business on September 30, 2022. The full text of the press release is attached hereto as Exhibit 99.1 and is incorporated by reference into this Item 7.01.

In addition, on August 17, 2022, the Board of Directors of the Company authorized the repurchase of an additional $2 billion of the Company’s common stock. As a result, the remaining total authorization for repurchases of the Company’s common stock is approximately $2.5 billion as of August 17, 2022.

The disclosure in Item 7.01 of this Current Report on Form 8-K and the press release attached hereto as Exhibit 99.1 are being furnished to the Securities and Exchange Commission in satisfaction of the public disclosure requirements of Regulation FD and shall not be deemed "filed" for any purpose.
