Item 7.01. Regulation FD Disclosure.

 

GCI Liberty, Inc. (“GCI Liberty”), currently a wholly owned subsidiary of Liberty Broadband Corporation (“Liberty Broadband”), previously filed a registration statement on Form S-1, initially filed on March 31, 2025 (File No. 333-286272) (as amended, the “Registration Statement”), with the Securities and Exchange Commission (the “SEC”) to register the dividend of shares of GCI Liberty’s Series A GCI Group common stock, Series B GCI Group common stock and Series C GCI Group common stock to Liberty Broadband stockholders (the “distribution”), as of the date of the distribution, in order to effect the separation of GCI Liberty from Liberty Broadband (the “separation”), such that GCI Liberty will be an independent publicly traded company, in connection with the previously announced acquisition of Liberty Broadband by Charter Communications, Inc. (Nasdaq: CHTR).

 

On June 23, 2025, the Registration Statement was declared effective by the SEC. GCI Liberty subsequently filed a final prospectus on July 1, 2025, which forms a part of the Registration Statement and includes additional information related to the distribution and GCI Liberty’s business and management following the separation.

 

This Current Report on Form 8-K is being furnished to the SEC under Item 7.01 of Form 8-K in satisfaction of the public disclosure requirements of Regulation FD and shall not be deemed “filed” for any purpose.
