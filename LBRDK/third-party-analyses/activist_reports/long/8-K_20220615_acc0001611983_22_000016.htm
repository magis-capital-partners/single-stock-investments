Item 5.07. Submission of Matters to a Vote of Security Holders

​

At Liberty Broadband Corporation’s (the “Company”) annual meeting of stockholders held on June 14, 2022 (the “Annual Meeting”), the following proposals were considered and acted upon by the stockholders of the Company: (1) a proposal to elect Richard R. Green, Sue Ann R. Hamilton and Gregory B. Maffei to continue serving as Class II members of the Company’s board of directors until the 2025 annual meeting of stockholders or their earlier resignation or removal; and (2) a proposal to ratify the selection of KPMG LLP as the Company’s independent auditors for the fiscal year ending December 31, 2022 (the “auditors ratification proposal”).
