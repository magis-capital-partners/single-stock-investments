Item 7.01.  Regulation FD Disclosure.  

 

On July 12, 2019, Liberty Broadband Corporation (the “Company”) announced that interested shareholders and analysts are invited to participate in a brief quarterly Q&A session following the completion of the prepared remarks on GCI Liberty, Inc.’s (“GCI”) second quarter earnings conference call. During this Q&A session management will be accepting questions regarding both GCI and the Company. President and Chief Executive Officer, Greg Maffei, will host the conference call on Thursday, August 8th, at 5:00 p.m. (E.D.T.). During the call, Mr. Maffei may discuss the financial performance and outlook of both companies, as well as other forward looking matters.

   

This Current Report on Form 8-K and the press release attached hereto as Exhibit 99.1 are being furnished to the Securities and Exchange Commission under Item 7.01 of Form 8-K in satisfaction of the public disclosure requirements of Regulation FD and shall not be deemed "filed" for any purpose.
