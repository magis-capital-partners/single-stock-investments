Item 8.01 Other Events.

On September 23, 2021, General Electric Company issued a press release announcing the entry into an agreement to acquire BK Medical, a leader in advanced surgical visualization, from Altaris Capital Partners for a cash purchase price of $1.45 billion. The parties expect the transaction to close in 2022, subject to review by the relevant regulatory authorities.
