8.01 Other Events.

 

On November 9, 2021, General Electric Company (“GE”) announced it plans to form three industry-leading, global, investment-grade public companies from GE’s (i) Aviation business, (ii) Healthcare business and (iii) combined Renewable Energy, Power and Digital businesses. GE intends to pursue a tax-free spin-off of GE Healthcare in early 2023, in which GE expects to retain a stake of 19.9%, and to combine GE Renewable Energy, GE Power and GE Digital into one business and then pursue a tax-free spin-off of the combined business in early 2024.

A copy of the press release announcing this new strategic plan is attached hereto as Exhibit 99.1 and is incorporated herein by reference.
