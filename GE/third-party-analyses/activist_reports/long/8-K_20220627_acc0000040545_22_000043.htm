Item 5.02. Departure of Directors or Certain Officers; Election of Directors; Appointment of Certain Officers; Compensatory Arrangements of Certain Officers.

On June 27, 2022, General Electric Company (“GE”) announced that H. Lawrence Culp, Jr. will serve as Chief Executive Officer of GE Aviation, effective immediately, in addition to his current role as Chairman and CEO of GE. John Slattery, current President and CEO of GE Aviation, has been named Executive Vice President and Chief Commercial Officer of the business, effective immediately.

GE also announced that Russell Stokes, current Senior Vice President, GE and President and CEO of GE Aviation Services, has been named President and CEO, Commercial Engines and Services, effective immediately.
