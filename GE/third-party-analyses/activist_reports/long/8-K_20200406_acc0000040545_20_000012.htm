Item 2.01 Completion of Acquisition or Disposition of Assets.

On March 31, 2020, General Electric Company (the “Company” or “GE”) completed the sale of its BioPharma business in its Healthcare segment to Danaher Corporation (“Danaher”) for $21.4 billion ($21.1 billion of total consideration, reflecting $0.3 billion of working capital adjustments to the contractual price).  The consideration consisted of $20.7 billion in cash and $0.4 billion of pension liabilities that were assumed by Danaher.  The Company received cash of $20.3 billion on March 31st and an additional $0.4 billion on April 1st.

The unaudited pro forma financial information giving effect to the BioPharma sale transaction is filed herewith as Exhibit 99.
