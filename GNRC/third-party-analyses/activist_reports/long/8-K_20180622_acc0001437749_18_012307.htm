Item 5.07 Submission of Matters to a Vote of Security Holders.

 

On June 21, 2018, Generac Holdings Inc. (“the Company”) held its annual meeting of stockholders in Waukesha, Wisconsin. At the meeting, the Company’s stockholders (1) elected each of the Company’s director nominees listed below for a three-year term; (2) ratified the appointment of Deloitte & Touche LLP as the Company’s independent registered public accounting firm for the year ending December 31, 2018; and (3) approved an advisory, non-binding resolution to approve the compensation of the Company’s named executive officers.
