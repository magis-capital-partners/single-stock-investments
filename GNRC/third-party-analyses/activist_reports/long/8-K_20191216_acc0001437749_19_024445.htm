Item 1.01

Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement

 

Amendment of Term Loan

 

On December 13, 2019, Generac Power Systems, Inc. (the “Borrower”), a wholly owned subsidiary of Generac Holdings Inc. (the “Company”), amended that certain Credit Agreement, dated as of February 9, 2012 (as amended and/or restated, supplemented, or otherwise modified from time to time, the “Term Loan Credit Agreement”) (such amendment, the “Term Loan Amendment”), which among other things, extended the maturity date for the term loan under the Term Loan Credit Agreement (the “Term Loan”) from May 2023 to December 2026. Prior to the effectiveness of the Term Loan Amendment, the Borrower paid (in addition to certain interest and fees) $49.0 million on the existing outstanding principal balance of the Term Loan of $879.0 million for a new outstanding principal balance of the Term Loan of $830.0 million. The Term Loan Amendment is attached hereto as Exhibit 10.1 and incorporated herein by reference. The foregoing summary does not purport to be complete and is qualified in its entirety by reference to such exhibit.
