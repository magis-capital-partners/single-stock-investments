Important Notice Regarding the Availability of Proxy Materials for the
Arconic Inc. Annual Meeting of Shareholders to be Held on May 16, 2018

 

You are receiving this notice that the proxy materials for the Arconic Inc. 2018 Annual Meeting of Shareholders are available on the Internet. Follow the instructions below to view the materials and vote online or request a paper or email copy. Information about the items to be voted on and the location of the annual meeting are provided on the reverse side of this notice.

 

This communication presents only an overview of the more complete proxy materials that are available to you on the Internet or by mail. We encourage you to access and review all of the important information contained in the proxy materials before voting.

 

The 2018 Proxy Statement and 2017 Annual Report are available at:

 

www.ViewMaterial.com/ARNC 

  

 

Easy Online Access – A Convenient Way to View Proxy Materials an Vote

 

When you go online to view materials, you can also vote your shares.

 

Step 1: Go to www.ViewMaterial.com/ARNC and view the proxy materials.

 

Step 2: Click the icon to vote your shares.

 

Step 3: Enter the 11 digit control number (located by the arrow in the box below).

 

Step 4: Follow the simple instructions to record your vote.

 

You are able to vote online until 6:00 a.m. Eastern Time on May 16, 2018.

 

When you go online, you can also help the environment by consenting to receive electronic delivery of future materials.

 

 

  Obtaining a Copy of the Proxy Materials – If you want to receive a paper or email copy of the proxy materials, you must request one. There is no charge to you for requesting a copy. Please make your request for a copy as instructed on the reverse side on or before May 2, 2018 to facilitate timely delivery.

  

 

 

 

 

 

Notice of 2018 Annual Meeting of Shareholders

 

The 2018 Annual Meeting of Shareholders of Arconic Inc. will be held at the Lotte New York Palace Hotel, 455 Madison Avenue, New York, New York, 10022, on Wednesday, May 16, 2018, at 9:00 a.m. Eastern Time.

 

Items to be voted on at the meeting are listed below along with the Board of Directors’ recommendations.

 

The Board of Directors recommends that you vote FOR the following items:

 

1.Elect 13 directors to serve for a one-year term expiring at the 2019 Annual Meeting of Shareholders: James F. Albaugh, Amy E. Alving, Christopher L. Ayers, Charles Blankenship, Arthur D. Collins, Jr., Elmer L. Doty, Rajiv L. Gupta, David P. Hess, Sean O. Mahoney, David J. Miller, E. Stanley O’Neal, John C. Plant, and Ulrich R. Schmidt;

 

2.Ratify the appointment of PricewaterhouseCoopers LLP as the Company’s independent registered public accounting firm for 2018;

 

3.Approve, on an advisory basis, executive compensation;

 

4.Approve the 2013 Arconic Stock Incentive Plan, as amended and restated; and

 

The Board of Directors recommends that you vote AGAINST the following item:

 

5.Shareholder proposal regarding shareholding threshold to call special shareowner meeting.

 

Admission and Directions: If you wish to attend and vote at the meeting, you will need an admission ticket. To request an admission ticket, please follow the instructions set forth in the 2018 Proxy Statement in response to Question 3 in the section “Questions and Answers about the Annual Meeting and Voting.” If you need directions to the 2018 Annual Meeting, please call the Lotte New York Palace Hotel at 1-212-888-7000.

 

PLEASE NOTE: YOU CANNOT VOTE BY RETURNING THIS NOTICE. To vote your shares on a proxy card, you must request that a paper copy of the proxy materials be mailed to you by following the instructions below.

 

 

 

  Obtaining a Copy of the Proxy Materials – If you want to receive a paper or email copy of the proxy materials, you must request one. There is no charge to you for requesting a copy. Please make your request for a copy on or before May 2, 2018 to facilitate timely delivery.

 

PLEASE NOTE: You must use the control number (located by the arrow in the box on the front side of this notice) when requesting a set of proxy materials.
