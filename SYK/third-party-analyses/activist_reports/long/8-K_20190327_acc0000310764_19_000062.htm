ITEM 5.02
DEPARTURE OF DIRECTORS OR CERTAIN OFFICERS; ELECTION OF DIRECTORS; APPOINTMENT OF CERTAIN OFFICERS; COMPENSATORY ARRANGEMENTS OF CERTAIN OFFICERS

On March 25, 2019, Stryker Corporation (the "Company") and Michael D. Hutchinson agreed that, effective as of April 22, 2019, Mr. Hutchinson will transition from his current role of Vice President, Chief Legal Officer to the position of Vice President and Advisor to the Chairman and Chief Executive Officer of the Company. The Company has announced that Robert Fletcher will be joining the Company as Vice President, Chief Legal Officer effective April 22, 2019.

On March 25, 2019, Mr. Hutchinson entered into a Transition and Retention Agreement with the Company pursuant to which he will be employed as Vice President and Advisor to the Company's Chairman and CEO from April 22, 2019 through May 31, 2020. Under the terms of the agreement, Mr. Hutchinson will continue to receive base salary at his current annual rate through May 31, 2020 or when Mr. Hutchinson terminates employment, if earlier, and will be eligible for his 2019 year-end incentive bonus. In addition, in lieu of a 2020 incentive award, provided Mr. Hutchinson remains employed through May 31, 2020, he will receive a retention payment of $170,312.50.

The summary description of the Transition and Retention Agreement contained in this Form 8-k is not complete and is qualified in its entirety by, and should be read in conjunction with, the complete text of such agreement filed as Exhibit 10.1 to this Form 8-K and incorporated herein by reference.
