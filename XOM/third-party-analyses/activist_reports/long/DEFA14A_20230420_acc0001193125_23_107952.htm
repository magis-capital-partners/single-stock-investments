To our valued shareholder,

We are quickly approaching this year’s Annual Shareholder Meeting, which takes place on May 31, 2023. Leading up to the annual meeting, all shareholders are encouraged to vote on the items in our proxy statement. It’s important that you make your voice heard and vote your shares.

In 2022, the hard work and commitment of our people drove exceptional results. While our results benefited from a favorable market, our work to meet demand began years ago. We appreciate your support as we continue to work hard, innovate, and provide solutions that meet the growing needs of society reliably, affordably, and responsibly.

This year, we have 16 proposals on the ballot. Our Board of Directors has brought their collective experience in energy, new technologies, climate science, and many other areas to provide a carefully considered response for each one. Their guidance and vote recommendations are meant to steer our company in the best direction for our shared future. We encourage you to vote in line with the Board’s recommendations.

For instructions on how to vote your shares, please reach out to your bank or brokerage firm.

Kind regards,

Investor Relations Team
