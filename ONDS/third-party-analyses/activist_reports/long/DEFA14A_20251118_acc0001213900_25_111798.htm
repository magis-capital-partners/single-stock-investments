Item 8.01. Other Events.

 

Special Meeting of Stockholders – Thursday, November 20, 2025

 

Ondas Holdings Inc. (the “Company”) will hold its Special Meeting of Stockholders (the “Meeting”) for the purpose of considering and voting on a charter amendment proposal and incentive plan amendment proposal, as described in the Company’s definitive proxy statement filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission on October 20, 2025, on Thursday, November 20, 2025, at 10:00 a.m. Eastern Time. The Meeting will continue to be held at One Marina Park Drive, Suite 1410, Boston, MA 02210.

 

Only stockholders of record, as of the record date, October 8, 2025 (the “Record Date”), are entitled to vote at the Meeting, either in person or by proxy. Proxies previously submitted in respect of the Meeting will be voted at the postponed Meeting unless properly revoked, and shareholders who have previously submitted a proxy or otherwise voted need not take any further action.

 

Stockholders who need assistance in submitting their proxy or voting their shares should call the Company’s proxy solicitor, Alliance Advisors, LLC, at (855) 325-6668.
