Item 2.02.Results of Operations and Financial Condition
    On October 9, 2023, Applied Digital Corporation. (the “Company”) issued a press release announcing its financial results for the three months ended August 31, 2023.
    A copy of such press release is attached as Exhibit 99.1 hereto and incorporated herein by reference.
Subsequent to the issuance of the press release, the Company made an accrual of $2.3 million for loss on legal settlement. The accrual resulted in the following updated metrics:
•Accrued liabilities increased from $10.4 million to $12.7 million
•Net loss increased from $9.6 million or $(0.10) loss per basic and diluted share, to $11.9 million, or $(0.12) loss per basic and diluted share
•EBITDA (a non-GAAP measure) decreased from EBITDA of $0.8 million to an EBITDA loss of $1.5 million.
