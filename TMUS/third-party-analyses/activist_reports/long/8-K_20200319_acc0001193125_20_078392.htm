Item 8.01 Other Events.

As previously announced on April 29, 2018, T-Mobile US, Inc., a Delaware corporation (“T-Mobile”), and Sprint Corporation, a Delaware corporation (“Sprint”), along with the other parties signatory thereto, entered into a Business Combination Agreement (as amended, the “Business Combination Agreement”). The Business Combination Agreement provides for, among other things and subject to the terms and subject to the conditions set forth therein, the merger of a wholly owned subsidiary of T-Mobile with and into Sprint, with Sprint continuing as the surviving corporation and as a wholly owned subsidiary of T-Mobile (the “Merger”).

On March 19, 2020, T-Mobile issued a press release announcing that it is currently financially prepared to close the Merger, based on its previously secured commitments for bridge financing and senior credit facility financing, subject to the satisfaction of customary closing conditions under its commitment letter. A copy of the press release is attached hereto as Exhibit 99.1.
