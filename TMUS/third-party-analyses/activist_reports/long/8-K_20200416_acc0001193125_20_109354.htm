Item 5.02 Departure of Directors or Certain Officers; Election of Directors; Appointment of Certain Officers; Compensatory Arrangements of Certain Officers.

As previously announced on March 12, 2020, David Carey is retiring from his position as Executive Vice President, Corporate Services of T-Mobile US, Inc. (the “Company”). On April 10, 2020, the Company and Mr. Carey determined that Mr. Carey would depart the Company effective April 13, 2020.

In connection with his departure, Mr. Carey will be eligible to receive certain severance payments and benefits described in, and, in accordance with the terms of, his existing employment, severance and equity award agreements with the Company.
