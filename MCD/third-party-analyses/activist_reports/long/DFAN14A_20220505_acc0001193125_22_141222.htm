Sunny Isles Beach, Florida — May 4, 2022 — Carl C. Icahn, who collectively with his affiliates is an engaged shareholder of McDonald’s Corporation (NYSE: MCD) (“McDonald’s” or the “Company”), today issued a comprehensive presentation that details the urgent case for adding individuals with environmental, social and governance (“ESG”) expertise to the Company’s Board of Directors (the “Board”). As a reminder, Mr. Icahn has nominated two highly qualified and independent ESG experts – Maisie Ganzler and Leslie Samuelrich – for election at the Company’s 2022 Annual Meeting of Shareholders.
