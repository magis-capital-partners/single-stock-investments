On May 24, 2022, McDonald’s Corporation (“McDonald’s”) issued a statement to the media, a copy of which is attached hereto as Exhibit 1.

 

Exhibit 1

 

Diversity, equal opportunity and an inclusive workplace culture are at the heart of McDonald’s core values. We are serious about holding ourselves accountable to our public commitments to drive equitable opportunity for all – commitments we make public, along with our transparent disclosures of diversity, equity and inclusion data, on our corporate website. Our executive compensation is linked to improving representation of women and historically underrepresented groups in leadership, and we are committed to maintaining equal pay for women and historically underrepresented groups. We’re continuing to increase purchases from diverse-owned suppliers, and we’ve launched a franchisee recruitment initiative to help increase the number of new diverse franchisees. While we’ve made good progress thus far, we will remain focused on continued action to accelerate meaningful and overdue societal change.
