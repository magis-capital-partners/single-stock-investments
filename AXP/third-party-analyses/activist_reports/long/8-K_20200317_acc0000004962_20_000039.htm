Item 7.01 Regulation FD Disclosure
The following information is furnished under Item 7.01 – Regulation FD Disclosure:

On March 17, 2020, American Express Company hosted an Investor Update Call, where the company’s senior executives discussed recent business trends and long-term strategies and provided an update on the impact of COVID-19 on its financial performance.
