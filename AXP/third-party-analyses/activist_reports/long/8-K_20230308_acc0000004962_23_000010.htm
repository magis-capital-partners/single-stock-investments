Item 8.01 Other Events
On March 8, 2023, American Express Company (the “Company”) issued a press release announcing the authorization to repurchase up to 120 million of its common shares, from time to time, subject to market conditions and in accordance with the Company’s capital plans that are, from time to time, reviewed and not objected to by the Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System. The Company also announced an increase to the quarterly dividend on its common shares from $0.52 per share to $0.60 per share. A copy of the press release is attached as Exhibit 99.1 to this Current Report on Form 8-K and incorporated herein by reference.
