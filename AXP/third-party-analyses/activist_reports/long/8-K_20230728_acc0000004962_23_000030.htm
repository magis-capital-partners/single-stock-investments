Item 8.01 Other Events.
Senior Notes Offering
Exhibits are filed herewith in connection with the issuance by American Express Company (the “Company”), on July 28, 2023, of $1,200,000,000 aggregate principal amount of 5.389% Fixed-to-Floating Rate Notes due July 28, 2027 (the “2027 Fixed-to-Floating Rate Notes”), $1,500,000,000 aggregate principal amount of 5.282% Fixed-to-Floating Rate Notes due July 27, 2029 (the “2029 Fixed-to-Floating Rate Notes”) and $300,000,000 aggregate principal amount of Floating Rate Notes due July 28, 2027 (the “Floating Rate Notes” and, together with the 2027 Fixed-to-Floating Rate Notes and the 2029 Fixed-to-Floating Rate Notes, the “Senior Notes”) pursuant to a Prospectus Supplement dated July 25, 2023 to the Prospectus dated February 12, 2021, filed as part of the Company’s Registration Statement on Form S-3 (No. 333-253057). The Senior Notes were issued under a senior indenture, dated as of August 1, 2007, between the Company and The Bank of New York Mellon, as trustee (the “Trustee”), as supplemented by the first supplemental indenture thereto, dated as of February 12, 2021, and the second supplemental indenture thereto, dated as of May 1, 2023, each between the Company and the Trustee.
Subordinated Notes Offering

Exhibits are filed herewith in connection with the issuance by the Company, on July 28, 2023, of $500,000,000 aggregate principal amount of 5.625% Fixed-to-Floating Rate Subordinated Notes due July 28, 2034 (the “Subordinated Notes”) pursuant to a Prospectus Supplement dated July 25, 2023 to the Prospectus dated February 12, 2021, filed as part of the Company’s Registration Statement on Form S-3 (No. 333-253057). The Subordinated Notes were issued under a subordinated indenture, dated as of August 1, 2007, between the Company and the Trustee, as supplemented by the second supplemental indenture thereto, dated as of May 26, 2022, and the third supplemental indenture thereto, dated as of July 28, 2023 (the “Third Supplemental Indenture”), each between the Company and the Trustee.

The preceding is a summary of the terms of the Third Supplemental Indenture and the Subordinated Notes, and is qualified in its entirety by reference to the Third Supplemental Indenture attached hereto as Exhibit 4.1 and the Form of Subordinated Notes attached hereto as Exhibit 4.2, and each is incorporated herein by reference as though it were fully set forth herein.
