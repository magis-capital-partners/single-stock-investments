Commencing on or about May 11, 2020, Fidelity National Information Services, Inc. sent the following communication to certain shareholders.

Distribution: FIS employees owning shares of FIS stock
From: Chip Keller, Corporate Secretary
Re: Vote Your FIS Shares


May 11, 2020

Distribution: Employees owning shares of FIS stock

Dear Colleagues:

Our annual shareholder meeting will be held on Thursday, May 28. Our records indicate that you own shares of FIS stock, either through the ESPP Plan or through a broker, and we encourage you to vote those shares at your earliest convenience. Your participation is critical to the corporate governance process.

The proposals being voted on at this year's shareholder meeting are:

1.Election of 10 Director Nominees to the FIS Board of Directors
2.Advisory Vote on Executive Compensation
3.Ratification of Independent Auditors


Our Board of Directors recommends that you vote in favor of Proposals 1, 2 and 3.

All shareholders of FIS recently received the proxy statement with details about these proposals and a communication on how to vote over the internet or through the mail. These instructions included your control number, which is required to vote your shares online at www.proxyvote.com.

The deadline for voting is Wednesday, May 27 for all FIS shareholders.

If you have any questions regarding the voting procedures, please contact Jennifer Lane at 407.551.8315 or jennifer.lane@fisglobal.com.

Please vote your shares today!


Best Regards,

Chip Keller
Corporate Secretary
