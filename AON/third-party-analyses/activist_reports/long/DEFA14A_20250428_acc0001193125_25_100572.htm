 Annual General Meeting Notice

Aon plc’s Annual General Meeting of Shareholders will be held on June 27, 2025 online via live webcast at meetnow.global/MDCGLRH and in person at 70 Sir John Rogerson’s Quay, Dublin 2, Ireland at 8:30 a.m. Irish Standard Time.

To attend the meeting in person, upon arrival, please present your admission ticket or proof of ownership or legal proxy from your broker, and photo identification at the registration desk.

To access the virtual meeting, you must have the 15-digit control number that is printed in the circle in the shaded bar located on the reverse side of this form. Note that attending the Annual Meeting virtually will not allow you to vote at the Annual Meeting.

Proposals to be voted on at the meeting are listed below along with the Board of Directors’ recommendations.
