Item 1.04 Mine Safety – Reporting of Shutdowns and Patterns of Violations.

On June 6, 2022, the Company received a section 107(a) order issued by the Mine Safety and Health Administration (“MSHA”) under the Federal Mine Safety and Health Act of 1977 at its Fort Dodge Mine in Fort Dodge, Iowa. The order stated that a miner was standing and working on top of the secondary portal canopy directly below loose and unconsolidated material. Heavy rain on the date of the inspection had further loosened material on the high wall. The miner was exposed to risk of injuries from falling material.

 

The miner was an employee of a third-party contractor. Martin Marietta took corrective action. No one was injured in the cited incident.
