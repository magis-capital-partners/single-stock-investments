## What this business is

Tiptree Inc. (NASDAQ: TIPT) allocates capital. It sold The Fortegra Group to DB Insurance on May 29, 2026, and sold Reliance First Capital on May 1, 2026. At June 30, 2026 the balance sheet is mostly cash and marketable securities, with a large tax bill and no debt. Management says the next phase is acquisitions in insurance, asset management, and specialty finance.

## Derived sum, not a reported line

Cash $946.933 million plus marketable securities $158.233 million minus the current tax payable $204.760 million is $900.406 million. That is the "roughly $900 million" of resources whose redeployment drives the thesis. It is arithmetic on the June 30, 2026 10-Q. It is not a line the company reports, and it ignores the $7.627 million lease.
