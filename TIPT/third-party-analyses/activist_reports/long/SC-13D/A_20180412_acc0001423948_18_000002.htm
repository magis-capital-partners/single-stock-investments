Item 4.
Purpose of Transaction

Item 4 is hereby amended and restated in its entirety with the following:

On April 10, 2018, Tiptree Inc. (“Tiptree”) completed a merger with its subsidiary, Tiptree Financial Partners, L.P. (“TFP”) with Tiptree continuing as the surviving company (the “Reorganization Merger”). Prior to the Reorganization Merger, Tiptree owned 84% of TFP with the remaining 16% of TFP owned by limited partners of TFP. In the Reorganization Merger, each limited partner of TFP received as consideration for its TFP partnership units (the “LP Units”), (i) the number of Class A common stock that they would have been entitled to exchange into using the existing exchange mechanic at a rate of 2.798 shares of Tiptree Class A common stock (“Tiptree Shares”) per LP Unit (ii) a warrant, which expires on September 30, 2018, to purchase Tiptree Shares at an exercise price of $11.33 per share (the “2018 Warrant”) and (iii) warrants to acquire Tiptree Shares on the same economic terms as their existing warrants to acquire TFP partnership units (the “2022 Warrant”).
