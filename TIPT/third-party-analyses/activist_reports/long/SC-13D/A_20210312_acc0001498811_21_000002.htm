Item 5.
Interest in Securities of the Company

Item 5 is hereby amended and restated in its entirety with the following:

For purposes of this Item 5(a), as of March 8, 2021 (the latest date the Company reported its Common Stock outstanding), the Company had 32,538,486 shares of Common Stock outstanding.

(a) As of the date of this Amendment No. 25, Michael Barnes may be deemed to beneficially own 9,386,343 Tiptree Shares over which Mr. Barnes has sole voting and dispositive power, consisting of 8,492,731 Tiptree Shares Mr. Barnes owns directly and 893,612 Tiptree Shares issuable upon exercise of warrants, expiring on June 30, 2022, to acquire Common Stock (the “2022 Warrants”). These shares represent approximately 28.08% of the Tiptree Shares outstanding based on 33,432,098 shares of Common Stock, which consists of (i) 32,538,486 Tiptree Shares outstanding and (ii) 893,612 Tiptree Shares issuable upon exercise of the 2022 Warrants Mr. Barnes owns directly.

As of the date of this Amendment No. 25, Arif Inayatullah may be deemed to beneficially own 3,373,948 Tiptree Shares over which Mr. Inayatullah has sole voting and dispositive power, consisting of 2,972,094 Tiptree Shares Mr. Inayatullah owns directly and 401,854 Tiptree Shares issuable upon exercise of the 2022 Warrants. These shares represent approximately 10.24% of the Tiptree Shares outstanding based on 32,940,340 shares of Common Stock, which consists of (i) 32,538,486 Tiptree Shares outstanding and (ii) 401,854 Tiptree Shares issuable upon exercise of the 2022 Warrants Mr. Inayatullah owns directly.

(b) Mr. Barnes has the sole power to dispose of and the sole power to vote the 8,492,731 Tiptree Shares directly owned by him and the 893,612 Tiptree Shares issuable upon exercise of the 2022 Warrants Mr. Barnes owns directly.

Mr. Inayatullah has the sole power to dispose of and the sole power to vote the 2,972,094 Tiptree Shares directly owned by him and the 401,854 Tiptree Shares issuable upon exercise of the 2022 Warrants Mr. Inayatullah owns directly.

(c) In this Amendment No. 25, any transactions in Tiptree Shares disclosed on a Form 4 filed by a Reporting Person during the period ending on the filing date of such amendment and starting on the later of the date 60 days before such filing date and the date of the most closely preceding amendment is and shall be incorporated into Item 5(c) with respect to such amendment without any further notice.

(d) Not applicable.

(e) Not applicable.
