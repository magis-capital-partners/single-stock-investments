Item 2.05 Costs Associated with Exit or Disposal Activities.

 

On September 21, 2022, Inpixon informed its employees that it was taking steps to streamline its operations and conserve cash resources. These steps, which would include layoffs, were anticipated to reduce its global employee headcount by approximately 20%. Departing employees will receive a cash compensation package based on tenure, a portion of which will be subject to the terms and conditions of a separation and release agreement, as well as an option to elect in for additional career transition and outplacement services. The layoff is expected to be completed by September 30, 2022, subject to compliance with statutory notice periods, where applicable, and result in a one-time expense of up to approximately $962,000.

 

This decision was determined following a review by management of Inpixon’s global operations in connection with cost saving initiatives aimed at conserving cash resources in response to anticipated continued economic uncertainty and market volatility.
